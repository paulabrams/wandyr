## Wandyr Magic: Heart-Cost Casting

### Core Rules

The player says what the spell should do and spends Hearts.
More Hearts = more effect. The player decides.

**Safety Threshold:** The lower of character Level or spells learned in that school.
- At or under threshold: Spell works
- Over threshold: Roll the Oracle; on NO, something goes wrong

### Unlearned Spells

Casting from a spellbook you haven't learned counts as risky. Roll the Oracle.

That's it. Three rules.
