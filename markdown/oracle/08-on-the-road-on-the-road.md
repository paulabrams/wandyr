# On the Road {#on-the-road}

## SIMPLE WEATHER {#simple-weather}

Roll d6:

1. Clear  
2. Warm  
3. Cloudy  
4. Wet  
5. Cold  
6. Combo

On Combo, roll again and combine:

1. Clear, Cloudy \= Partly cloudy  
2. Clear, Warm \= Warm sunny  
3. Warm, Warm \= Extreme heat  
4. Cloudy, Wet \= Rain  
5. Clear, Wet \= Intermittent drizzle  
6. Cold, Wet \= Snow  
7. Clear, Cold \= Frosty  
8. Cold, Cold \= Extreme cold  
9. Warm, Cold \= Thunderstorm  
10. Wet, Dry \= Thunderstorm  
11. Wet, Wet \= Downpour  
12. Wet, Wet, Warm \= Hurricane  
13. Cold, Cold, Wet \= Blizzard  
14. Warm, Clear, Dry \= D drought  
15. Cloudy, Cold, Wet \= Sleet  
16. Clear, Cold, Wet \= Freezing rain  
17. Warm, Wet, Cloudy \= Muggy, oppressive heat  
18. Cold, Clear, Dry \= Bitter windchill  
19. Warm, Cloudy, Wet \= Tropical storm  
20. Clear, Warm, Wet \= Rainbow weather
