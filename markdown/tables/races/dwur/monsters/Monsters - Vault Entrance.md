# Random Encounters - Vault Entrance Area

*Roll 1d20. Check every 2 turns, encounter on 6.*

1. **Marmot Colony** (2d4) - HD 1/4, AC 9; harmless, may chatter warnings about danger
2. **Mountain Goats** (1d6) - HD 1, AC 7; agile climbers, flee unless cornered
3. **Dwur Bell-Ringer** (1) - HD 2, AC 5; watchful guardian, carries signal horn
4. **Alpine Ravens** (2d6) - HD 1/4, AC 8; intelligent, may have observed intruders
5. **Protective Goatherd's Dog** (1) - HD 1+1, AC 7; loyal, barks warnings of approach
6. **Tatzelwurm** (1) - HD 3, AC 6; serpentine predator, attacks from rocky outcrop
7. **Dahu** (1) - HD 2, AC 8; trickster goat, tries to lead party astray
8. **Cave Bear** (1) - HD 6, AC 6; territorial, may have den near entrance
9. **Barbegazi** (1d3) - HD 1, AC 8; snow dwellers, may help or hinder
10. **Tschäggättä** (1d2) - HD 2, AC 7; masked chaos-bringers, steal or frighten
11. **Alpine Festival-goers** (1d4) - HD 1, AC 9; human/gnome revelers seeking shelter
12. **Ancestral Spirit** (1) - HD 2*, AC 5; ghostly Dwur, offers cryptic guidance
13. **Mountain Lion** (1) - HD 3+2, AC 6; stalker, attacks from ambush
14. **Alpine Fox** (1d2) - HD 1, AC 7; cunning, may lead to hidden paths
15. **Mountain Eagle** (1) - HD 2, AC 7; soars overhead, may signal danger
16. **Alpine Marmot** (1d4) - HD 1/2, AC 8; curious, may approach for food
17. **Mountain Hare** (1d3) - HD 1/2, AC 8; fast, may lead to water sources
18. **Alpine Vulture** (1d2) - HD 2, AC 6; carrion eater, circles overhead
19. **Mountain Weasel** (1) - HD 1, AC 8; stealthy, may steal small items
20. **Alpine Spirit** (1) - HD 3*, AC 5; nature guardian, tests travelers

## Environmental Hazards (Roll when encounter = 1-2)

- **Sudden Weather** (50%) - Fog, snow, or wind
- **Rockslide** (30%) - DEX save DC 15 or 2d6 damage
- **Avalanche Warning** (20%) - Distant rumbling, seek shelter
