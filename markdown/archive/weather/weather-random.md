# Weather (d66)

**1.1** Clear & Mild - The sky is blue and the air is gentle.
**1.2** Overcast - Grey clouds blanket the sky, muting the light.
**1.3** Windy - Gusts tug at cloaks and rattle leaves.
**1.4** Foggy - Visibility drops as a thick mist descends.
**1.5** Light Rain/Snow - Gentle precipitation falls.
**1.6** Heavy Rain/Snow - A soaking downpour or thick snowfall.

**2.1** Drizzle - Constant, fine rain that soaks everything.
**2.2** Downpour - Sudden, intense rain that floods paths.
**2.3** Thunderstorm - Lightning flashes and thunder rolls.
**2.4** Monsoon - Relentless driving rain for hours.
**2.5** Flood - Waters rise, washing out roads and bridges.
**2.6** Hail - Pellets of ice batter the ground.

**3.1** Cold Snap - Temperatures plummet suddenly.
**3.2** Frost - Ice crystals coat every surface.
**3.3** Ice Storm - Rain freezes on contact, coating the world in glass.
**3.4** Blizzard - Blowing snow blinds and freezes.
**3.5** Freezing Fog - Mist turns to ice on everything it touches.
**3.6** Black Ice - Invisible ice makes travel treacherous.

**4.1** Heatwave - Oppressive heat shimmers off the ground.
**4.2** Drought - The land is parched and cracks.
**4.3** Dust Storm - A wall of grit scours the land.
**4.4** Wildfire - Smoke fills the air as fire spreads.
**4.5** Ash Fall - Grey ash drifts down like snow.
**4.6** Dry Lightning - Thunder without rain, sparking fires.

**5.1** Strong Winds - Walking requires effort against the gale.
**5.2** Gale Force - Branches snap and debris flies.
**5.3** Tornado - A funnel cloud tears through the landscape.
**5.4** Hurricane - Devastating winds and rain.
**5.5** Landslide - Earth slips and crashes down slopes.
**5.6** Avalanche - Snow rushes down the mountain.

**6.1** Blood Moon - The moon turns a deep crimson.
**6.2** Aurora - Dancing lights fill the night sky.
**6.3** Meteor Shower - Streaks of light cross the heavens.
**6.4** Acid Rain - Precipitation burns plants and skin.
**6.5** Rain of Frogs - Small amphibians fall from the sky.
**6.6** Star Jelly - Gelatinous blobs appear after the rain.
