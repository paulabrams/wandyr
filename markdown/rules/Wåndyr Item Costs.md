# Wandyr Item Costs

This assumes coinage consistent with the original roleplaying game.

## Approximate Item Cost

* **Common items** cost \~1 gp per item slot. Examples: clothing, tools, etc.  
* **Uncommon items** used by a fairly narrow range of people cost \~5 gp per item slot. Examples: weapons, armor, shields.  
* **Rare items** that are expensive to produce cost \~10 gp per item slot. Examples: books, music instruments, Arcana.

## High-Quality Items

Higher quality items have an added cost based on the enhancements.

Aesthetic enhancements add at least 10 gp depending on the details. Functional enhancements such as “+1” adds 100 gp, and “+2” 1,000 gp. So an “elegant knight’s sword, \+1” costs at least 120 gp to purchase (5 gp per slot, “elegant” 10 gp, “+1” 100 gp).

The Guide may also allow players to upgrade existing items, so long as they can find a skilled craftsperson and have a reasonable approach in mind.

### Weapons

High quality weapons are more *lethal*. The Guide may allow players who want to purchase high quality weapons to freely describe it as they like, as long as it’s adding *more lethality and damage*. They might think that a good sword is always \+1 and a two-handed sword is always \+2, or that spiked maces are \+1 and wavy-bladed swords are \+2. But they can’t go the other direction and claim “brass knuckles \+1” are more lethal than a sword; that would need some kind of magic.

### Armor

High quality armor is more *protective*. The Guide may allow players who want to purchase high quality armor to freely describe it as they like, as long as it’s adding *more coverage and protection*. They might think that chainmail is always \+1 and plate is always \+2, or a viking helm is \+1 and a great helm is \+2. But they can’t go the other direction and claim an “armored collar \+1” is more protective than a common helmet; that would need some kind of magic.

### Other Items

Higher quality items of other kinds function better than normal items of the same kind. For example a high-quality guitar would have better sound.

### Upgrading Items

Can I upgrade my items to become \+1? Yes, by paying the 100 gp to buy “+1”, provided that you can find someone to do the work.
