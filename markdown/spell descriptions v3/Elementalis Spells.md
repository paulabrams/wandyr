# Elementalis (Elemental) Spells

*Primal forces, elements, and weather. The Baal inscribe dragon magic in burning runes carved in stone. Practitioners control weather, create barriers of ice and fire, command gravity, and shape elements.*


**2.1.1** *Control Weather* - Influence weather patterns in small area. **Instant** Create a brief weather phenomenon like a gust of wind, temperature shift, or light precipitation that affects the immediate area for one quick tactical advantage. Works best with existing weather conditions rather than creating dramatic changes from nothing. **Active** Establish sustained weather control over a 100-foot radius, allowing you to gradually shift conditions like changing rain intensity, temperature, or wind direction throughout the encounter. You can adjust conditions to your needs continuously. **Level** Weather control becomes more precise and far-reaching.

**2.1.2** *Crystal Growth* - Encourage formation of crystals from mineral deposits. **Instant** Create tiny crystal fragments worth d6 silver pieces from any mineral content you touch, suitable for simple decoration or trade. Works best with existing mineral veins rather than common stone. **Active** Produce larger, more valuable crystals worth 1-10 gold pieces, carefully shaping them from available mineral content over time. You can coax precious gems from mineral-rich sources with sustained effort and magical enhancement. **Level** Crystal quality improves, formation success rate increases.

**2.1.3** *Dragon's Breath* - Exhale dragonflame from your mouth like true dragon. **Instant** Release a 15-foot cone of searing dragonflame dealing 1d6 damage to all creatures in the area. The breath appears with draconic visual effects and the distinctive roar of dragonflame. Targets can dive for cover to reduce damage. **Active** Maintain a continuous stream of dragonflame throughout the encounter, allowing you to sweep the cone across multiple targets or sustain pressure on a single area. The flames may ignite flammable objects and leave behind smoldering embers that continue to burn. **Level** Dragonflame becomes more intense and dragon-like.

**2.1.4** *Earth Shape* - Mold stone: Make natural earth materials malleable like potter's clay. **Instant** Quickly reshape a small amount of clay, mud, or soft stone into a simple form that hardens immediately. Works best on natural earth and stone but cannot affect worked masonry or metal. **Active** Work with up to 10 cubic feet of material, carefully sculpting complex shapes and forms with your hands until satisfied. The material remains malleable throughout your work and hardens into its new permanent shape when you finish. You can create detailed sculptures and functional objects. **Level** material workability improves, harder substances become moldable, shaping precision increases.

**2.1.5** *Earthquake* - Shake ground: Create tremors in the earth that destabilize the area. **Instant** Generate a single powerful shake in a 20-foot radius that can topple objects and cause creatures to stumble. The tremor is strong enough to trigger avalanches or collapses in already unstable areas. **Active** Maintain continuous tremors throughout the encounter that give creatures disadvantage on balance and fine motor actions while potentially triggering environmental hazards. The shaking can rattle doors, topple unstable objects, and cause people to lose their footing continuously. **Level** tremor range gradually expands, seismic intensity deepens, ground disruption becomes more pronounced.

**2.1.6** *Element Master* - Complete control: Demonstrate precise control over all four classical elements. **Instant** Create a brief but impressive display of elemental mastery that showcases your magical prowess across fire, water, earth, and air simultaneously. Emphasizes finesse over raw power. **Active** Maintain continuous control over fire, water, earth, and air at once throughout the encounter, allowing complex manipulations and sustained demonstrations. You can maintain a flame in one hand, shape water with another, levitate small stones, and direct air currents simultaneously. This impressive display grants advantage on social interactions with those who witness it. **Level** number of elements controlled simultaneously, complexity of manipulations.

**2.2.1** *Elemental Wall* - Create barrier: Create a barrier of elemental energy. **Instant** Raise a temporary wall 10 feet long and 5 feet high that provides immediate cover but dissipates quickly. You can choose the elemental type when creating the wall. **Active** Maintain a stable elemental barrier that persists for the encounter, allowing you to reposition or reshape it as needed. You can choose ice (blocks movement), fire (deals 1d6 damage to those passing through), stone (solid barrier), or compressed air (deflects projectiles). The wall has 5 Hearts worth of durability and can be broken with sufficient force. **Level** wall length increases by +5 feet per Level, durability +3 Hearts per Level spent.

**2.2.2** *Fire Shield* - Protective flames: Surround yourself with a warm aura of protective flame. **Instant** Create a brief flare of protective fire that damages one creature striking you and provides momentary warmth and light. The controlled flames won't ignite your equipment. **Active** Maintain a continuous fiery aura that consistently punishes melee attackers and provides ongoing warmth and illumination throughout the encounter. The shield provides comfortable warmth in cold conditions and sheds light like a torch. Anyone who strikes you in melee combat takes 1d6 fire damage from the protective flames. **Level** flames burn hotter and more intensely, protective coverage expands, can shield additional allies.

**2.2.3** *Fog Cloud* - Create mist: Generate a thick cloud of mist or steam. **Instant** Create a burst of fog in a 20-foot radius that provides immediate concealment but disperses quickly. The fog heavily obscures vision but does not block sound or movement. **Active** Maintain a persistent fog cloud that you can move or reshape, providing ongoing tactical advantage throughout the encounter. Strong wind can disperse it, but you can see through your own fog clearly, giving you an advantage against creatures blinded by it. **Level** mist spreads further and grows denser, duration extends, weather resistance improves.

**2.2.4** *Force Wall* - Energy barrier: Create an invisible barrier of compressed air. **Instant** Generate a brief force barrier that can deflect a single attack or projectile before dissipating. The barrier is invisible but can be felt when touched. **Active** Maintain a stable wall 15 feet long and 10 feet high that consistently blocks attacks and can support weight throughout the encounter. The wall can stop arrows, thrown weapons, and even charging creatures with a successful resistance roll. It provides partial cover against ranged attacks and can support weight like a solid surface. **Level** wall size increases by +5x5 feet per Level, strength increases.

**2.2.5** *Gravity Shift* - Change gravity: Alter gravity's pull on objects and creatures. **Instant** Briefly affect gravity for objects under 50 pounds, causing them to float, fall upward, or become heavier for a moment. Creates immediate tactical opportunities. **Active** Maintain gravitational manipulation in a 10-foot radius, allowing sustained effects on movement and positioning throughout the encounter. You can make objects float weightlessly, fall upward, or become three times heavier. Creatures can resist the effect but find their movement awkward and difficult, allowing for creative tactical solutions. **Level** gravitational control becomes more precise, affects larger masses, manipulation range gradually expands.

**2.2.6** *Heat Metal* - Make objects burning: Make metal objects uncomfortably hot. **Instant** Cause a single metal object within 30 feet to become briefly hot enough to force someone to drop it. The heat is not enough to cause serious burns but creates immediate discomfort. **Active** Maintain intense heat on metal objects throughout the encounter, causing ongoing discomfort and tactical disadvantage to those using them. The metal glows faintly red and radiates warmth, with heated armor causing disadvantage on actions and weapons becoming difficult to hold. **Level** number of objects affected, heat intensity and damage increase.

**2.3.1** *Icy Touch* - Spread ice: Touch any surface to create slippery ice. **Instant** Create a small patch of ice around the touched area that provides immediate but temporary hazard. The ice is thin but makes movement treacherous. **Active** Maintain ice formation that spreads 10 feet in all directions and persists throughout the encounter. Creatures moving across the ice must make balance checks or risk falling prone. The ice won't support heavy objects but makes movement treacherous for enemies and allies alike. **Level** frost spreads more quickly and extensively, ice becomes thicker and more persistent, cold intensifies.

**2.3.2** *Increase Gravity* - Triple weight: Dramatically increase gravity's pull in a localized area. **Instant** Create a brief gravitational surge that can force flying creatures to land and make everyone stumble. Dropped objects deal double damage during the surge. **Active** Maintain tripled gravity in a 15-foot radius throughout the encounter, consistently hampering movement and preventing flight. Affected creatures move at half speed and have disadvantage on jumping or climbing attempts. Flying creatures must land or risk falling as their wings cannot support the increased weight. **Level** gravitational field spreads naturally, weight effects become more pronounced, control precision improves.

**2.3.3** *Lightning Strike* - Call electricity: Call down bolts of lightning from sky or fingertips. **Instant** Strike a single target within 100 feet with lightning that deals 1d6 electrical damage and jumps to one nearby creature for half damage. The flash briefly illuminates the area and creates a sharp crack of thunder. **Active** Continuously channel electrical energy throughout the encounter, allowing multiple strikes or sustained electrical attacks on targets within range. Metal armor provides no protection and may conduct the electricity to other touching metal objects. **Level** electrical potency intensifies, lightning becomes more precise, may arc to additional targets.

**2.3.4** *Liquid Air* - Make air swimmable: Transform air into a thick, viscous substance. **Instant** Briefly thicken the air in a small area, slowing movement and creating unusual tactical conditions. The air remains breathable but feels heavy and humid. **Active** Maintain a 20-foot cube of liquid air that allows swimming through the atmosphere and dramatically alters battlefield mobility throughout the encounter. Creatures can "swim" through the air at their normal swimming speed while those who cannot swim sink slowly downward, creating unique tactical opportunities. **Level** atmospheric transformation deepens, viscosity control improves, affected area expands organically.

**2.3.5** *Mist Form* - Become vapor: Transform your body into a cloud of mist. **Instant** Briefly become incorporeal mist to avoid a single attack or squeeze through a small opening before reforming. Strong winds can affect your movement while in this form. **Active** Maintain vapor form for extended infiltration throughout the encounter, allowing you to pass through small openings and under doors. You move at half speed but can squeeze through any gap larger than one inch. Physical attacks pass harmlessly through you, but you cannot manipulate objects or cast other spells. **Level** duration increases, can include allies, mist density and resistance improve.

**2.3.6** *Mountain's Strength* - Earth power: Draw strength from the earth beneath your feet. **Instant** Gain a surge of earth-born power for a single feat of strength, breaking objects or overpowering opponents. The power requires contact with natural earth or stone. **Active** Maintain doubled physical power throughout the encounter as long as you remain in contact with natural earth or stone. Your carrying capacity doubles and you have advantage on feats of strength like breaking down doors or lifting heavy objects. You can punch through thin stone walls or bend iron bars with effort, but the power fades if you lose earth contact. **Level** strength multiplier increases, duration extends, additional physical enhancements.

**2.4.1** *Rain Make* - Create downpour: Summon a localized rainstorm that follows your movements. **Instant** Create a brief but heavy downpour that can extinguish fires or provide immediate water in a small area. The rain is heavy enough to extinguish normal fires. **Active** Maintain a persistent rainstorm in a 50-foot radius that moves with you and affects the battlefield throughout the encounter. The rain provides fresh drinking water while reducing visibility to 20 feet within the storm. The ground becomes muddy and slippery, creating ongoing environmental hazards. **Level** storm coverage expands naturally, rainfall becomes heavier and more sustained, weather effects intensify.

**2.4.2** *Sculpt Elements* - Shape materials: Reshape natural elemental materials into useful forms. **Instant** Quickly reshape a small amount of natural material (stone, metal, wood, ice, or clay) into a simple form. The shaping creates permanent changes to the material's form but not its essential properties. **Active** Work with up to 50 cubic feet of material, carefully sculpting complex shapes and forms with concentration and precision throughout the encounter. You cannot create moving parts or complex mechanisms, but can fashion tools, weapons, or artistic sculptures from naturally occurring materials. **Level** material responsiveness improves, shaping precision increases, can work with larger quantities and denser substances.

**2.4.3** *Steam Form* - Become vapor: Transform into a cloud of hot steam. **Instant** Briefly become scalding steam to escape danger or move through a small opening, dealing damage to creatures you pass through. The steam dissipates if exposed to cold or strong winds. **Active** Maintain steam form for extended mobility throughout the encounter, allowing you to pass through small spaces and rise with air currents. The steam is hot enough to deal 1d6 damage to creatures you pass through. You can see and hear normally but cannot manipulate objects or speak clearly, though water-based attacks can restore dispersed vapor. **Level** vapor form becomes more stable and controllable, heat effects intensify, transformation duration improves.

**2.4.4** *Stone Skin* - Harden flesh: Transform your skin to have the texture and durability of stone. **Instant** Briefly harden your skin to resist a single attack, reducing damage from physical sources. Your skin takes on a granite-like appearance and feels cool to the touch. **Active** Maintain stone-like skin that provides ongoing resistance to physical damage throughout the encounter, reducing all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage by half. The transformation makes you heavier and slightly slower, but provides excellent protection against conventional weapons. **Level** damage reduction improves, duration extends, can protect allies.

**2.4.5** *Stone Tell* - Read earth memories: Touch natural stone to learn about events in its presence. **Instant** Gain a brief glimpse of recent significant events that happened near the stone you're touching. Ancient stones may provide glimpses from centuries past. **Active** Delve deeper into the stone's memories throughout the encounter, accessing a fuller history of major events like battles, constructions, or gatherings over extended periods. You experience these memories as hazy visions showing shapes and movements but not fine details or conversations. These visions are often fragmented and require careful interpretation. **Level** memory depth increases, clarity improves, range of accessible events expands.

**2.4.6** *Storm Call* - Summon thunder: Create an intense thunderstorm with lightning, rain, and chaos. **Instant** Generate a brief but violent storm burst with lightning strikes and thunder that affects the immediate area. Lightning provides intermittent bright illumination while thunder masks other sounds. **Active** Maintain a persistent thunderstorm in a 30-foot radius with ongoing lightning strikes and environmental effects throughout the encounter. Lightning flashes every few seconds, dealing 1d6 damage to one random creature in the area each round. The constant thunder makes communication difficult while rain extinguishes fires and creates treacherous footing. **Level** radius increases by +15 feet per Level, lightning frequency and damage increase.

**2.5.1** *Summon Cube* - Control earth: Raise a cube of solid earth or stone from the ground. **Instant** Quickly raise a temporary earth cube that provides immediate cover or creates an obstacle before sinking back down. The cube has 3 Hearts worth of durability. **Active** Maintain a 5-foot cube of earth within 30 feet that you can reposition by concentrating on it throughout the encounter. The cube can serve as cover, a platform, or an obstacle depending on your needs and can move up to 10 feet per round. The cube crumbles to normal earth when destroyed or when you cease maintaining it. **Level** earth responds more readily, cubes become denser and more durable, control precision improves.

**2.5.2** *Summon Idol* - Create statue: Shape earth, stone, or clay into a human-sized statue. **Instant** Quickly form a crude statue that can perform one simple action like pointing or gesturing before becoming inert. The statue moves slowly and deliberately, obviously artificial. **Active** Maintain an animated idol that can perform ongoing simple tasks like standing guard, pointing directions, or holding objects throughout the encounter. The idol understands basic commands in your language and has minimal intelligence but cannot fight or cast spells. It's impressive to observers who aren't familiar with magic. **Level** idol size and intelligence increase, can create multiple idols.

**2.5.3** *Thunder Call* - Create sonic boom: Generate a deafening thunderclap that damages enemies. **Instant** Create a single massive thunderclap at a point within 60 feet that deals 1d6 sonic damage and deafens creatures within 20 feet. You can choose to be immune to the effect when casting. **Active** Maintain ongoing thunder effects throughout the encounter, allowing multiple thunderclaps or sustained sonic pressure in the targeted area. The sound is loud enough to trigger avalanches, break fragile objects, and wake sleeping creatures within a quarter mile, making it useful for signaling or environmental manipulation. **Level** thunder becomes more resonant and powerful, sonic effects spread further, sound intensity deepens.

**2.5.4** *Tide Control* - Command waters: Control the flow and level of natural bodies of water. **Instant** Cause a sudden change in water level or current that can affect boats, create waves, or expose hidden areas briefly. The effect cannot create water from nothing. **Active** Maintain control over water within 100 feet throughout the encounter, allowing you to raise or lower levels by up to 5 feet and redirect currents. You can change the direction of currents or create small waves on rivers, lakes, ponds, and coastal areas. Sudden changes can strand boats or flood low areas as needed. **Level** water responds more readily to your will, control range expands naturally, tidal effects become more pronounced.

**2.5.5** *Upwell* - Create spring: Cause fresh water to bubble up from the ground. **Instant** Create a brief upwelling of fresh water that provides immediate hydration or can be collected quickly. The water is clean and safe to drink, appearing even where no groundwater naturally exists. **Active** Maintain a steady spring that produces 10 gallons per minute throughout the encounter, creating a reliable water source for the group. The spring creates a small pool that provides enough water for drinking, cooking, and basic cleaning needs while you maintain it. **Level** spring flow becomes more reliable and abundant, water quality improves, flow sustainability increases.

**2.5.6** *Water Breathing* - Breathe liquid: Grant yourself and others the ability to breathe water as air. **Instant** Provide brief underwater breathing capability for emergency situations or short dives. The spell doesn't grant swimming abilities or protection from water pressure. **Active** Maintain water breathing for yourself and up to three other creatures throughout the encounter, allowing extended underwater exploration and activity. This allows underwater exploration and swimming without concern for drowning while still being able to breathe air normally. Only provides the ability to extract oxygen from water. **Level** duration extends, can affect +2 additional creatures per Level.

**2.6.1** *Water Shape* - Control liquid: Command water to take shapes that serve tactical purposes. **Instant** Quickly reshape a small amount of water into a simple form like a barrier or tool that holds briefly before flowing away. The water remains liquid to the touch despite its shape. **Active** Maintain precise control over up to 100 gallons of water throughout the encounter, shaping it into complex forms like walls, bridges, or stairs. The water maintains its new form as if it were a solid object but remains liquid to the touch. You can create structures that support weight and block passage, with the shaped water returning to natural flow when you stop maintaining concentration. **Level** water becomes more responsive and malleable, shape complexity improves, formed structures become more stable.

**2.6.2** *Web Weave* - Spin natural nets: Create barriers of fibrous vines or thick spider webs. **Instant** Rapidly grow a small area of webs or vines that can entangle one or two creatures briefly. The webs are sticky and strong but can be cut or burned away. **Active** Maintain extensive webbing that fills a 20-foot square area with persistent entangling effects throughout the encounter. Creatures moving through the webs are slowed to quarter speed and may become entangled if they fail to break free. The webs are strong enough to hold human-sized creatures, providing ongoing battlefield control. **Level** web coverage spreads organically, strands become stronger and more adhesive, entanglement effects improve.

**2.6.3** *Whirlwind* - Create vortex: Generate a spinning column of air that moves across the battlefield. **Instant** Create a brief whirlwind that strikes one area, dealing damage and scattering objects before dissipating. The wind is strong enough to extinguish fires and scatter loose papers. **Active** Maintain a controllable whirlwind 10 feet wide and 30 feet tall that moves at your direction up to 30 feet per round throughout the encounter. The whirlwind can pick up small objects, dust, and debris while creatures caught in the vortex take 1d6 damage and are lifted 10 feet before being deposited roughly. **Level** vortex becomes more powerful and focused, wind intensity increases, control precision improves.

**2.6.4** *Wind Shield* - Air protection: Surround yourself with a swirling barrier of protective wind. **Instant** Create a brief wind barrier that can deflect a single ranged attack or blow away small threats. Small flying creatures like insects are blown away by the wind. **Active** Maintain a constantly swirling wind shield that provides ongoing protection from projectiles and environmental hazards throughout the encounter. The wind deflects arrows, thrown weapons, and other projectiles, providing partial cover against ranged attacks. The constant air movement keeps you cool and your scent is carried away for stealth. **Level** protection improves, can shield allies, wind strength increases.

**2.6.5** *Wind Walk* - Float on breeze: Step onto currents of air and walk through the sky. **Instant** Take a brief aerial step or leap using wind currents to cross gaps or reach elevated positions. You cannot hover in one place without forward movement. **Active** Maintain the ability to walk on air currents at normal walking speed throughout the encounter, ascending or descending gradually as needed. Strong winds can affect your direction. You remain about 10-20 feet above the ground and must land gently when you cease maintaining the effect. **Level** altitude increases, speed improves, can carry allies.

**2.6.6** *Zephyr* - Ride the winds: Transform into a gust of wind for rapid movement. **Instant** Briefly become wind to move quickly across short distances or escape immediate threats. Physical attacks cannot harm you in this form. **Active** Maintain wind form for extended travel throughout the encounter, moving at ten times your normal speed and passing through openings larger than a few inches. You cannot manipulate objects or cast other spells in this form. You can reform when desired or when you stop maintaining the effect, appearing wherever the wind carries you. **Level** speed multiplier increases, duration extends, can carry objects or allies.
