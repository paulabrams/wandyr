# Initiative

1. Melee (longer weapon first)
2. Ranged
3. Magic

Melee attacks occur first as long as the attacker is in reach or can move into reach. Ranged fighters are advised to shoot first (prior to melee fighters moving into range). When in doubt, the Guide will decide, or ask the Oracle, or declare it's simultaneous.

Enchanted spells cast from weapon Arcana use that weapon's timing. A wizard staff goes on melee timing, a bow or wand or orb goes on ranged timing. Non-weapons (e.g. a wizard ring or hat) isn't a weapon and goes on magic timing.
