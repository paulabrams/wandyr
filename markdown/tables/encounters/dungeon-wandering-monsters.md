## Usage Notes

- **Rolling System**: Each table uses d6 for encounter type, then d6 for specific creature
- **Special Encounters**: Entries marked (sp, sw) indicate special or sweet encounters focused on roleplay
- **Level Scaling**: Choose appropriate level ranges based on party strength
- **Biome Mixing**: Feel free to combine elements from different biome tables for unique environments
- **Frequency**: Roll for wandering monsters every 10-20 minutes of game time or when dramatically appropriate
