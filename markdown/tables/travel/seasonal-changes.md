# Seasonal Changes

Roll 2d6 for seasonal effects on the world:

**1.1** Spring Awakening - New life emerges everywhere  
**1.2** Summer Growth - Plants and animals flourish  
**1.3** Autumn Harvest - Bounty is gathered  
**1.4** Winter Rest - Nature sleeps and recovers  
**1.5** Rainy Season - Water brings life and mud  
**1.6** Dry Season - Heat and dust dominate

**2.1** Planting Time - Seeds are sown in hope  
**2.2** Growing Season - Crops mature rapidly  
**2.3** Harvest Time - Food is gathered and stored  
**2.4** Dormant Period - Life slows and conserves  
**2.5** Flood Season - Rivers overflow their banks  
**2.6** Drought Period - Water becomes scarce

**3.1** Migration Time - Animals move to new areas  
**3.2** Breeding Season - Creatures seek mates  
**3.3** Hibernation - Some animals sleep deeply  
**3.4** Awakening - Sleeping creatures stir  
**3.5** Storm Season - Weather becomes violent  
**3.6** Calm Period - Weather is mild and pleasant

**4.1** Flowering - Plants bloom and produce seeds  
**4.2** Fruiting - Trees and bushes bear food  
**4.3** Leaf Fall - Trees prepare for winter  
**4.4** Budding - New growth appears  
**4.5** Pollination - Insects spread plant life  
**4.6** Seed Dispersal - Plants spread to new areas

**5.1** Bird Migration - Feathered creatures travel  
**5.2** Fish Spawning - Aquatic life reproduces  
**5.3** Insect Swarming - Small creatures multiply  
**5.4** Mammal Breeding - Large animals mate  
**5.5** Reptile Activity - Cold-blooded creatures emerge  
**5.6** Amphibian Awakening - Frogs and toads appear

**6.1** Magical Bloom - Magic becomes more potent  
**6.2** Fey Activity - Otherworldly beings appear  
**6.3** Spirit Walking - Dead become more visible  
**6.4** Elemental Stirring - Natural forces awaken  
**6.5** Shadow Lengthening - Darkness grows stronger  
**6.6** Light Returning - Illumination increases
