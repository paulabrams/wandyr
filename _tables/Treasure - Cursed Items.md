# Random Treasure - Cursed Items

*Roll 1d20 for items with dangerous properties.*

1. **Bloodthirsty Axe** - Compels violence; fine dwur axe, red-stained blade
2. **Whispers Ring** - Constant paranoia; silver band, bearer hears threats
3. **Memory Stone** - Absorbs personality; smooth obsidian, slowly erases self
4. **Frozen Cloak** - Spreads cold; beautiful fur, chills wearer and allies
5. **Hungry Bag** - Devours items; leather satchel, randomly eats contents
6. **Weeping Helm** - Constant sorrow; iron helmet, bearer cannot feel joy
7. **Lying Map** - Shows false paths; accurate-looking, leads to danger
8. **Shadow Lantern** - Attracts undead; brass lamp, draws hostile spirits
9. **Greed Coins** - Compulsive hoarding; beautiful gold, bearer cannot spend
10. **Nightmare Mask** - Disturbing dreams; festival mask, causes prophetic terrors
11. **Soul Mirror** - Reflects past lives; polished silver, shows previous incarnations
12. **Time Watch** - Ages bearer rapidly; golden timepiece, speeds up aging
13. **Truth Tongue** - Forces honesty; silver pendant, bearer cannot lie
14. **Pain Bracelet** - Amplifies suffering; iron band, magnifies all pain
15. **Lonely Crown** - Isolates from others; golden circlet, makes bearer unapproachable
16. **Hunger Belt** - Never satisfied; leather strap, bearer always feels starving
17. **Sleep Stone** - Constant drowsiness; smooth pebble, bearer cannot stay awake
18. **Rage Gauntlets** - Uncontrollable anger; iron gloves, bearer flies into rages
19. **Fear Torch** - Attracts nightmares; wooden torch, bearer sees horrors everywhere
20. **Death Bell** - Foretells demise; brass bell, rings when bearer approaches death

## Curse Removal

- **Remove Curse** spell (if available)
- **Dwur ritual** performed at clan shrine
- **Stellar Forge** technology might reverse effects
- **Mountain spirits** may lift curse for service
- **Time limit** - some curses fade after 1d6 weeks
