# Masterwork Armaments: Alternative Benefits

## Problem

"Heavy" armaments use +1/+2 (100gp / 1,000gp). Replace these plus bonuses with **benefits that are player choices**, not flat bonuses.

**Magic items** can have +1/+2/+3 bonuses—what makes them magical is their unique powers (see magic item alignment system). A +1 magic sword is fine; it's the powers that make it special.

**Core rules to respect:** Armaments 5gp/slot. Two-handed weapons: 2d6 keep best; one-handed, keep worst.

---

## Tiers

- **Masterwork** or **of Proof** (100gp): one benefit.
- **Exceptional** or **Battle-tested** (1,000gp, rare): one stronger benefit.

Pick or assign one benefit per item. Naturally heavy stuff (ax, polearm, mail) is easier to find at these grades.

---

## Benefits (choice-based)

Each benefit is something the player **chooses** to do, not a passive bonus.

**Weapons**

- **Precise strike:** Once per combat, when you hit, you may ask the Oracle if you strike a vital point. On Yes, deal 6 Hearts damage.
- **Full leverage** *(two-handed only):* When you hit two-handed with Sweet, you may ask the Oracle if you put full weight behind the blow. On Yes, you may trip, push, or disarm as well as deal damage.
- **Versatile grip** *(two-handed only):* When you use it one-handed, you may roll 2d6 and keep the higher (instead of keep worst).
- **Devastating blow:** Once per Muster, when you hit, you may declare a devastating strike. Roll 3d6, keep highest two, for damage.

**Armor**

- **Deflect:** Once per combat, when you would take damage, you may ask the Oracle if the armor deflects the blow. On Yes, reduce damage by 1d6 Hearts.
- **Turn the blow:** Once per Muster, when you would be hit, you may ask the Oracle if the armor turns it. On Yes, you take no damage.

**Shields**

- **Perfect block:** Once per combat, when you would be hit, you may ask the Oracle if the shield intercepts. On Yes, you take no damage, but the shield loses 1 Iron Heart until repaired.
- **Shield bash:** When you block an attack, you may immediately counter with the shield for d6 Hearts damage.
- **Turn the blow:** Once per Muster, when you would be hit, you may ask the Oracle if the shield stops it. On Yes, you take no damage and the shield is fine.
- **Shield wall:** When an adjacent ally is attacked, you may extend your shield to them. They gain 1 Iron Heart against that attack.

---

## Naming

Use **of Proof** / **Proofed** for certified, tested gear (armor of proof, shield of proof, proved sword). Use **Battle-tested** or **Exceptional** for the higher tier.

**Magic items** can have +1/+2/+3 bonuses and unique powers (see magic item alignment system). The powers are what make them magical, not the plus bonus.

---

## Examples

- **Proved longsword (100gp):** *Precise strike*
- **Proved round shield (100gp):** *Perfect block* (intercept once per combat; shield damaged on success)
- **Battle-tested polearm (1,000gp):** *Devastating blow* or *Full leverage*
