# Illusione (Illusion) Spells

*Perception, thought, and dreams. The subtle magic of the Belerions, woven from phantasmal scripts that deceive the senses. Illusionists create illusions, alter memories, influence minds, and bend perceived reality.*

**4.1.1** *Arcane Eye* \- Flying sensor: Create an invisible, flying eye that you can control telepathically within 100 feet of your position. The eye can move through spaces as small as 1 inch in diameter and provides vision as if it were your own. You cannot cast spells through it, and bright light or magical detection can reveal its presence. **Level** (additional eyes created). **Instant**: Brief glimpse through the eye for reconnaissance. **Active**: Sustained surveillance throughout the encounter, allowing continuous scouting and observation.  
**4.1.2** *Auditory Illusion* \- Create sounds: Produce any sound you have heard before at a location within 60 feet. The sound can be as quiet as a whisper or as loud as a shouting person, and can move around at your will. You can create voices, animal noises, footsteps, or environmental sounds. Listeners who examine the sound closely may realize it's an illusion with careful attention. **Level** (simultaneous sound sources). **Instant**: Single sound effect for distraction or misdirection. **Active**: Continuous audio environment with moving and changing sounds throughout the scene.  
**4.1.3** *Bewitch* \- Enchant mind: Cloud the thoughts of one creature within 30 feet, making them confused and distracted. The target has difficulty concentrating and making decisions, suffering disadvantage on mental tasks and spellcasting. They remain aware but feel foggy and uncertain. The effect breaks if they take damage or someone helps them focus. **Level** (additional targets affected). **Instant**: Brief mental confusion causing disadvantage on their next action. **Active**: Sustained mental fog affecting all actions throughout the encounter.  
**4.1.4** *Blur* \- Distort appearance: Make your outline constantly shift and waver, making you difficult to target precisely. Attackers have disadvantage on attack rolls against you as your exact position becomes unclear. The effect is subtle to casual observers but noticeable to anyone trying to interact physically. The blurring becomes more pronounced when you move quickly. **Level** (additional targets protected). **Instant**: Brief distortion providing advantage on one defensive action. **Active**: Continuous blurring effect protecting you throughout the encounter.  
**4.1.5** *Charm* \- Create friendship: Make one person within 30 feet regard you as a trusted friend. They won't do anything obviously harmful to themselves or their true allies, but will be helpful and give you the benefit of the doubt. The charmed person acts naturally but with a positive disposition toward you. When the effect ends, they may realize they were magically influenced. **Level** (additional targets charmed). **Instant**: Momentary surge of goodwill for one favorable reaction. **Active**: Sustained friendship throughout the scene, maintaining their helpful attitude.  
**4.1.6** *Detect Magic* \- See magic: Sense the presence and general nature of magical auras within 30 feet. You can determine the approximate power level and school of magic, and whether items or creatures are magical. The spell reveals illusions, enchantments, and other magical effects as faint glowing outlines visible only to you. Focusing on a specific aura reveals more detailed information. **Level** (magical sight range expands naturally, aura detail clarity improves, can perceive subtler magical traces). **Instant**: Quick scan revealing magical presences and their general nature. **Active**: Continuous magical sight allowing detailed analysis throughout the encounter.

**4.2.1** *Disguise* \- Alter appearance: Create an illusion that changes your appearance, allowing you to look like any humanoid of roughly your size. The disguise includes clothing, hair, skin tone, and minor physical features, but not your voice or mannerisms. Physical inspection or touch reveals the illusion, but casual observation is completely fooled. **Level** (disguise complexity and detail). **Instant**: Quick disguise for a brief deception or escape. **Active**: Sustained disguise that you can adjust and maintain throughout the scene.  
**4.2.2** *Dream Message* \- Send sleeping visions: Send a message to any sleeping person you know within 100 miles. The message appears as a vivid dream that the target will remember clearly upon waking. You can include simple images, emotions, and spoken words, though complex information may be distorted by the dreaming mind. **Level** (additional sleeping targets). **Instant**: Brief dream message with simple content. **Active**: Extended dream sequence allowing detailed communication and emotional connection.  
**4.2.3** *Dream Walking* \- Enter dreams: Project your consciousness into the dreams of a sleeping person you can see within 30 feet. You experience their dream as if it were real and can interact with dream elements and the dreamer's subconscious. You can gather information about their memories, fears, and desires, but cannot directly control the dream content. **Level** (depth of dream penetration and control). **Instant**: Quick glimpse into their current dream state for basic impressions. **Active**: Full dream immersion allowing deep exploration of their subconscious until they wake or you withdraw.  
**4.2.4** *Duplicate* \- Create copies: Create 1d4 identical illusory copies of yourself that mimic your movements. The duplicates occupy the same space as you initially, then spread out as you move. When a duplicate takes any damage or is touched, it immediately disappears. Attackers must guess which version is real, causing attacks to hit duplicates instead. **Level** (number of duplicates created). **Instant**: Single duplicate that lasts only moments for one deceptive action. **Active**: Multiple duplicates that persist throughout the encounter, continuously confusing enemies.  
**4.2.5** *Emotional Aura* \- Project feeling: Emanate a subtle emotional influence in a 20-foot radius. You can choose one emotion such as calm, fear, joy, or sadness that affects all creatures in the area. Those affected feel the emotion as if it arose naturally, though strong-willed individuals may resist or recognize the artificial nature. The effect enhances existing emotions rather than overriding them. **Level** (emotional influence spreads further and becomes more compelling, can blend multiple complementary emotions, resistance becomes more difficult). **Instant**: Brief emotional pulse affecting immediate reactions. **Active**: Sustained emotional atmosphere throughout the scene.  
**4.2.6** *False Memory* \- Plant fake memory: Implant a false memory into one person's mind within touch range. The memory feels completely real and integrates naturally with their existing memories. The false memory cannot be obviously impossible or completely contrary to their nature and beliefs. Over time, especially if contradicted by evidence, they may question its authenticity. **Level** (memory complexity and permanence). **Instant**: Brief false memory of a simple event or detail. **Active**: Complex false memory of extended events that becomes deeply embedded in their recollection.

**4.3.1** *Feign Death* \- Appear dead: Make yourself or a willing creature appear completely dead. The target's breathing and heartbeat become undetectable, their body temperature drops, and they become rigid and pale. They remain conscious but cannot move or speak, and take no damage from most sources. Even close medical examination concludes death unless magical detection is used. **Level** (additional targets affected). **Instant**: Brief death-like state for avoiding one attack or check. **Active**: Sustained death illusion throughout the scene, perfect for extended deception.  
**4.3.2** *Greed* \- Create obsession: Cause one creature within 30 feet to become obsessively fixated on a specific object you designate. The target will prioritize acquiring or protecting that object above almost all other concerns, though they won't obviously endanger themselves. They rationalize their behavior as completely natural and reasonable. When the effect ends, they're confused about their intense interest. **Level** (additional targets affected). **Instant**: Brief moment of obsession affecting one decision or action. **Active**: Sustained fixation driving their behavior throughout the encounter.  
**4.3.3** *Hypnotize* \- Question trance: Put a willing or unconscious creature within touch range into a deep trance where they answer questions truthfully. The subject speaks in a monotone voice and responds only to direct questions about things they know. They cannot lie but may give incomplete answers if poorly questioned. When it ends, they remember being questioned but it feels dreamlike. **Level** (depth of mental access and revelation). **Instant**: Single truthful answer to one important question. **Active**: Extended questioning session allowing thorough interrogation throughout the scene.  
**4.3.4** *Illusory Spray* \- Disorient with light: Create a burst of shifting, colorful lights in a 15-foot cone that dazes and confuses creatures. Those caught in the display see phantom images, distorted colors, and disorienting patterns that make concentration difficult. Affected creatures have disadvantage and may stumble or drop held items. The lights are purely illusory but create real sensory overload. **Level** (light patterns become more complex and disorienting, sensory overload intensifies, confusion effects deepen). **Instant**: Single blinding flash causing brief disorientation. **Active**: Continuous light show maintaining disorientation throughout the encounter.  
**4.3.5** *Invisibility* \- Become unseen: Make yourself or one creature you touch completely invisible. The target cannot be seen by normal sight, but they still make sounds, leave footprints, and can be detected by other senses. Equipment and clothing also become invisible. The spell creates a perfect optical illusion rather than true concealment. **Level** (additional targets made invisible). **Instant**: Brief invisibility for one surprise action or escape. **Active**: Sustained invisibility throughout the encounter, ending if they attack or cast spells.  
**4.3.6** *Mass Suggestion* \- Group command: Plant the same suggestion in the minds of up to 6 creatures within 60 feet. The suggestion must be reasonable and phrased as a course of action. Targets will pursue the suggested action as if it were their own idea, unless it would obviously harm them. The suggestion fades when completed, leaving no memory of magical influence. **Level** (additional targets affected beyond base 6). **Instant**: Brief impulse affecting immediate group behavior. **Active**: Sustained suggestion driving coordinated group action throughout an extended period.

**4.4.1** *Major Image* \- Perfect illusion: Create a realistic illusion up to 20 feet in each dimension that includes sight, sound, smell, and temperature effects. The illusion can move and change according to your will within the spell's area. It can mimic complex scenes like people talking, fires burning, or flowing rivers. Physical interaction reveals the illusion's true nature, but all other senses are completely deceived. **Level** (illusion becomes more convincing and detailed, sensory effects intensify, can encompass larger scenes naturally). **Instant**: Single impressive scene for immediate impact. **Active**: Complex, evolving illusion that responds to events throughout the encounter.  
**4.4.2** *Mind Maze* \- Trap thoughts: Trap one creature's consciousness within 30 feet in an illusory mental labyrinth. The target's body stands motionless while their mind navigates an endless maze of their own memories and fears. They can attempt to escape by solving mental puzzles or overcoming psychological obstacles. While trapped, they're completely unaware of the outside world and vulnerable to attacks. **Level** (maze complexity and escape difficulty). **Instant**: Brief mental confusion causing one lost action. **Active**: Sustained mental trap requiring continuous escape attempts throughout the encounter.  
**4.4.3** *Mind Shield* \- Protect thoughts: Create a barrier around your mind that prevents others from reading your thoughts, emotions, or memories. The shield blocks telepathy, mind-reading magic, and other mental intrusions while allowing your own mental abilities to function normally. Creatures attempting to access your mind sense only static or false thoughts you project. The protection is invisible but absolute. **Level** (additional minds protected). **Instant**: Brief mental protection against one intrusion attempt. **Active**: Sustained mental barrier protecting against all intrusions throughout an extended period.  
**4.4.4** *Mirror Image* \- Create duplicates: Surround yourself with 1d4+1 identical illusory copies that move independently. Each duplicate occupies its own space and can move up to 30 feet from you before vanishing. When attacked, roll randomly to see if the duplicate or you is hit - duplicates disappear when struck. The images mimic your actions imperfectly, making it difficult to determine which is real. **Level** (number of mirror images created). **Instant**: Single mirror image for one defensive action. **Active**: Multiple independent duplicates that persist and regenerate throughout the encounter.  
**4.4.5** *Mirror Walk* \- Step through glass: Step into any mirror or reflective surface large enough to fit through and emerge from another reflective surface within 100 feet. You must be able to visualize or have seen the destination surface. You can bring one other willing creature touching you. The spell works on windows, polished metal, still water, or any clear reflective surface. **Level** (additional creatures transported). **Instant**: Single teleportation through mirrors for escape or positioning. **Active**: Continuous ability to step between reflective surfaces throughout the scene.  
**4.4.6** *Perfect Illusion* \- Undetectable fake: Create an illusion of any object no larger than 5 feet in any dimension that is completely convincing to all senses. The illusion has apparent weight, texture, temperature, and even taste if appropriate. It can be picked up, manipulated, and examined closely without revealing its true nature. Only magical detection or illusion-revealing spells can penetrate this perfect deception. **Level** (object size increases by 5 feet per dimension per Level). **Instant**: Brief perfect illusion for one critical deception. **Active**: Sustained perfect illusion that maintains its deception throughout an extended period.

**4.5.1** *Phantom Sound* \- Distant noise: Create a sound that appears to come from anywhere within 120 feet. The sound can be a voice, animal call, footsteps, or any noise you've heard before, and can move around at your will. Volume ranges from a whisper to a shout, and you can make it fade in and out or change pitch and tone. The sound is completely convincing unless listeners examine it carefully. **Level** (sound projection becomes more versatile and convincing, can create multiple simultaneous sounds, acoustic precision improves). **Instant**: Single sound effect for immediate distraction or misdirection. **Active**: Continuous phantom sounds that can move and change throughout the encounter.  
**4.5.2** *Phantasmal Force* \- Moving illusion: Create an illusion visible only to one creature within 60 feet that manifests their fears or expectations. The phantasm deals psychic damage as the target's mind makes the illusion feel real. The target can attempt to disbelieve the illusion, but their own psychology works against them. The illusion appears completely real to the victim but is invisible to others. **Level** (additional targets affected by personal phantasms). **Instant**: Brief nightmare vision causing immediate psychological trauma. **Active**: Sustained personal torment that continuously affects the target throughout the encounter.  
**4.5.3** *Programmed Illusion* \- Triggered effect: Create an illusion up to 10 feet in each dimension that activates when specific conditions are met. You set the trigger condition (such as "when someone opens this door") and the illusion that appears (visual and auditory). The illusion plays out when triggered, then resets to trigger again. The programmed effect can activate multiple times. **Level** (illusion complexity and sensory scope). **Instant**: Simple triggered illusion that plays once when conditions are met. **Active**: Complex programmed illusion that can trigger repeatedly and persist for extended periods.  
**4.5.4** *Read Mind* \- Hear thoughts: Listen to the surface thoughts of one creature within 30 feet, hearing their current mental commentary as whispered words. You gain insight into their immediate intentions, concerns, and reactions, but cannot access deep memories or hidden knowledge. The target may sense something if they're mentally strong or magically sensitive. The spell picks up active thoughts, not subconscious processes. **Level** (depth of mental access and memory reach). **Instant**: Brief glimpse of their current thoughts and immediate intentions. **Active**: Continuous monitoring of their surface thoughts throughout the encounter.  
**4.5.5** *Scry* \- Share vision: Create a magical sensor within 1 mile that allows you to see and hear as if you were present at that location. You must have visited the location before or have a clear mental image from detailed descriptions. The sensor is invisible and intangible but can be detected by magical means. Your body remains in a trance-like state while your senses are projected. **Level** (scrying becomes clearer and more stable, distance limitations relax gradually, can maintain longer viewing sessions). **Instant**: Brief glimpse of a distant location for reconnaissance. **Active**: Sustained remote viewing allowing continuous observation throughout the scene.  
**4.5.6** *Silent Image* \- Create scene: Project a purely visual illusion up to 15 feet in each dimension that you can move and alter. The image has no sound, smell, or physical presence, but can display any scene you can imagine with perfect visual clarity. You can make it move naturally within its space, change colors and shapes, or cycle through different images. Close physical inspection reveals its incorporeal nature. **Level** (visual complexity and detail improve dramatically, can maintain multiple changing scenes simultaneously, clarity becomes more convincing). **Instant**: Single static image for immediate visual deception. **Active**: Dynamic visual illusion that can move and change throughout the encounter.

**4.6.1** *Spectacle* \- Create grand illusion: Fill a 50-foot radius area with an impressive illusion involving all senses. You might create the appearance of a royal court, a dragon's lair, or a magical forest complete with sounds, smells, and tactile sensations. The illusion can include moving creatures and environmental effects that respond to observers. While convincing to all senses, physical interaction with major elements reveals their illusory nature. **Level** (illusion becomes more immersive and believable, environmental complexity increases, sensory authenticity deepens). **Instant**: Brief spectacular display for dramatic effect or distraction. **Active**: Sustained grand illusion that evolves and responds throughout the encounter.  
**4.6.2** *Telepathy* \- Project thoughts: Establish a mental connection with any creature you've met within 100 miles. You can communicate wordlessly across any distance, sharing thoughts, emotions, and simple images instantly. The connection works both ways unless the target actively resists, allowing for true mental conversation. Language barriers don't matter as thoughts transfer meaning directly, and communication cannot be intercepted by normal means. **Level** (additional simultaneous telepathic connections). **Instant**: Brief telepathic message for urgent communication. **Active**: Sustained mental link allowing continuous conversation throughout the scene.  
**4.6.3** *True Seeing* \- Pierce deception: See through all illusions, transmutations, and deceptions within 120 feet. You perceive the true form of shapeshifted creatures, see invisible objects and beings, detect magical disguises, and recognize illusions for what they are. Hidden doors, secret passages, and concealed objects become visible to your enhanced sight. The spell also reveals the true nature of magical auras and enchantments. **Level** (truth vision becomes more penetrating and comprehensive, can pierce deeper deceptions, perception of magical nature intensifies). **Instant**: Brief moment of true sight to pierce one specific deception. **Active**: Sustained true vision revealing all deceptions throughout an extended period.  
**4.6.4** *Vision* \- Personal illusion: Create a prophetic vision, false memory, or symbolic dream in one creature's mind within touch range. The vision feels deeply significant and memorable to the target. You can craft the vision to convey warnings, inspiration, false information, or guidance, though complex details may be distorted by the recipient's interpretation. The target believes they experienced a genuine supernatural revelation unless given strong reason to doubt. **Level** (vision complexity and emotional impact). **Instant**: Brief prophetic flash or symbolic moment. **Active**: Extended visionary experience with detailed symbolic narrative.  
**4.6.5** *Visual Illusion* \- Static images: Create a fixed visual illusion up to 10 feet in each dimension. The illusion cannot move or change but can depict any static scene you desire with perfect visual detail. Common uses include disguising doors as walls, making walls appear as windows, or creating false decoration. The illusion has no physical presence and provides no actual concealment, only visual deception revealed by touch or examination. **Level** (visual fidelity and detail become exceptional, can create larger static scenes, deception becomes more thorough and convincing). **Instant**: Simple visual change for immediate deception. **Active**: Permanent visual alteration that persists until dispelled or destroyed.  
**4.6.6** *X-Ray Vision* \- See through matter: See through solid objects within 30 feet, as if they were transparent glass. You can look through walls, doors, containers, and clothing to observe what lies beyond or within. The vision penetrates up to 3 feet of stone, 1 foot of metal, or any thickness of wood or similar organic material. Living tissue appears as shadowy outlines, allowing you to see hidden weapons, injuries, or concealed objects. **Level** (penetrating vision becomes more powerful and clear, can see through denser materials, perception detail improves dramatically). **Instant**: Quick x-ray scan to check one specific area or object. **Active**: Sustained x-ray vision allowing continuous observation throughout the encounter.
