## YE OLDE SHOPPES & THEIR SECRETS {#ye-olde-shoppes-&-their-secrets}

#### Row 1: Market Square Shops

**1.1** **Goodman's General Store**  
A cramped but tidy shop offering daily necessities. Barrels of dried beans, wheels of cheese, bundles of candles, and racks of common tools fill every corner. A tall mirror stands beside the counter "to prevent theft by watching blind corners."

*Secrets: True Name \- The Magpie's Nest. Madame Corvida transforms shoplifters into small, black birds she keeps in ornate cages throughout the shop. The more expensive the stolen item, the smaller the bird. Her mirrors show those with light fingers their final form, while the True mirror is the full-length security glass, its bronze frame decorated with perched birds that seem to watch the customers.*

**1.2** **The Village Chandlery**  
The neat rows of candles and soaps fill the shop with a pleasant aroma. Bundles of herbs hang from the rafters, and a large mirror "reflects light to brighten the shop" behind the soap-cutting table.

*Secrets: True Name \- Yesterday's Treasures. Old Miss Tempus renders thieves into her finest soap, sold in bars that sometimes weep. The faint scent of lavender barely masks another odor. Her mirrors show those who'll next become clean, while the True mirror is the broad shop mirror, its surface rippling like liquid soap.*

**1.3** **The Jolly Butcher**  
The busiest shop in the square, always well-stocked with fresh meats and sausages. A massive mirror hangs behind the counter "to make the shop appear larger."

*Secrets: True Name \- The Curious Cat. The suspiciously feline Madame Felina transforms difficult customers into "choice cuts" hanging from her hooks. Her mirrors reveal which parts are still human, while the True mirror is the large shop mirror, its surface occasionally showing flickers of movement among the hanging meats.*

**1.4** **The Miller's Daughter**  
The town's largest flour mill, always busy with farmers bringing grain. A tall mirror stands near the grain sacks "to check for weevils in the flour."

*Secrets: True Name \- The Grinding Stone. The miller's daughter transforms thieves into grain, grinding them between the millstones. Her pale flour sometimes screams when baked. The True mirror is the flour-checking glass, its surface swirling with faces in the dust.*

**1.5** **The Crooked Nail**  
A bustling blacksmith's shop, filled with the ring of hammers and smell of coal. A broad mirror hangs "to better catch the light for detailed work."

*Secrets: True Name \- The Forge Heart. Master Smith transforms shoplifters into nails, hooks, and hinges. Every piece of ironwork contains a trapped soul. The True mirror is the work mirror, glowing red-hot when reflecting the guilty.*

**1.6** **The Green Thumb**  
A well-tended herb shop bursting with plants and dried bundles. A tall mirror stands "to help the plants grow with reflected sunlight."

*Secrets: True Name \- The Garden's Hunger. The herbalist feeds thieves to her carnivorous plants, their nutrients producing especially potent herbs. The True mirror is the sun-catching glass, showing green tendrils reaching for the unwary.*
