# NPC Reaction Oracle

Roll 1d20. Note sweet and spicy.

1. **Hostile** - Openly aggressive, may attack
2. **Suspicious** - Distrustful, questions motives
3. **Fearful** - Afraid, may flee or hide
4. **Dismissive** - Ignores or belittles party
5. **Reluctant** - Unwilling but not hostile
6. **Neutral** - Indifferent, business-like
7. **Interested** - Curious but cautious
8. **Pleasant** - Polite and helpful
9. **Friendly** - Warm and welcoming
10. **Enthusiastic** - Excited to meet party
11. **Trusting** - Believes party completely
12. **Admiring** - Respects party's abilities
13. **Protective** - Wants to help/shelter party
14. **Generous** - Offers gifts or aid
15. **Reverent** - Treats party as honored guests
16. **Devoted** - Swears loyalty to party
17. **Awestruck** - Overwhelmed by party's presence
18. **Submissive** - Defers to party's wishes
19. **Worshipful** - Treats party as divine beings
20. **Fanatical** - Willing to die for party
