## Deep Gallery Hazards

- **Thin air** - CON save every hour or exhausted
- **Weird echoes** - Voices in unknown languages
- **Unstable floor** - May collapse into deeper pits
- **Strange lights** - Phosphorescent minerals, may be hypnotic
- **Temperature drops** - Sudden cold, risk of frostbite
