## Cultural Notes

The Illythrhi represent the fallen aspect of the Aethiiri, those who have turned away from the light and embraced darkness. They maintain the same naming conventions as their light-aligned cousins but often take on darker epithets and titles.

Their societies are often hidden and secretive, operating in the shadows and maintaining ancient grudges against their light-aligned kin.
