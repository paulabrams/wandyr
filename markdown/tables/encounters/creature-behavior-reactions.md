# Creature Behavior & Reactions

## MONSTER REACTIONS

Roll 2d6:

2. **Hostile** - Attacks immediately with full aggression
3. **Aggressive** - Threatens and prepares to attack  
4. **Unfriendly** - Suspicious and defensive posture
5. **Wary** - Cautious but not immediately threatening
6. **Cautious** - Observes and evaluates situation
7. **Neutral** - Neither friendly nor hostile
8. **Curious** - Interested in party's presence
9. **Friendly** - Approaches without hostility
10. **Helpful** - Offers assistance or information
11. **Protective** - Guards party from other dangers
12. **Devoted** - Joins party temporarily or permanently
