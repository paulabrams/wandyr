# Celestial Events

Roll 2d6 for celestial phenomena that affect the world:

**1.1** Blood Moon - Dark creatures gain power, magic is amplified  
**1.2** Solar Eclipse - Light magic weakened, shadows grow stronger  
**1.3** Meteor Shower - Falling stars grant wishes to pure hearts  
**1.4** Aurora Borealis - Northern lights reveal hidden paths  
**1.5** Comet Sight - Prophecies written in the sky  
**1.6** Star Alignment - Ancient portals open briefly

**2.1** Lunar Eclipse - Moon magic enhanced, werewolves transform  
**2.2** Solar Flare - Fire magic empowered, crops wither  
**2.3** Planetary Conjunction - Reality becomes malleable  
**2.4** Shooting Stars - Dreams come true for dreamers  
**2.5** Stellar Wind - Knowledge flows from the heavens  
**2.6** Cosmic Storm - Time flows differently

**3.1** Black Sun - Light magic fails, darkness reigns  
**3.2** Golden Hour - All magic costs are halved  
**3.3** Silver Night - Moonlight reveals hidden truths  
**3.4** Dawn's Promise - New beginnings bring hope  
**3.5** Twilight's Veil - Boundaries between worlds thin  
**3.6** Midnight's Gift - Dark magic flows freely

**4.1** Star Birth - New magic schools discovered  
**4.2** Celestial Dance - Fey creatures emerge  
**4.3** Heaven's Gate - Angels descend briefly  
**4.4** Hell's Portal - Demons escape their prison  
**4.5** Void Rift - Reality tears open  
**4.6** Dream Storm - Sleepers share visions

**5.1** Time Distortion - Past and future blur together  
**5.2** Reality Shift - Laws of physics bend  
**5.3** Memory Storm - Forgotten memories surface  
**5.4** Prophecy Moon - Future events revealed  
**5.5** Soul Wind - Spirits walk the earth  
**5.6** Life Bloom - All living things flourish

**6.1** Death's Shadow - Undead rise from graves  
**6.2** Life's Light - Wounds heal miraculously  
**6.3** Wisdom's Star - Knowledge flows freely  
**6.4** Chaos Storm - Random magic effects  
**6.5** Order's Ray - Law and structure strengthen  
**6.6** Balance Dawn - All forces find equilibrium
