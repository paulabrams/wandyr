## The Primal School of Vitae

### Origins and Philosophy
Vitae magic springs from the ancient understanding that all life is interconnected through blood, sap, and the eternal cycle of nature. In the deep forests of Dorthonia, where the Wood Elves and Kith first learned to commune with the raw power of existence, they discovered that true life magic requires understanding the harmony between all living things. The earliest practitioners learned that healing demands sacrifice, growth requires blood, and power comes from embracing the natural cycles of birth, death, and renewal.

The school teaches that magic flows through the blood and sap of all living things - from the smallest plant to the mightiest beast - and that true mastery comes from understanding that all life shares the same vital essence. Practitioners believe that by embracing the natural cycles and understanding the primal nature of existence, they can channel the raw power of life force to heal, protect, and maintain the balance of nature. This philosophy acknowledges both the beauty and the harsh reality of life while using this understanding for beneficial purposes.

### Practitioners and Titles
**The Hallow**: The most common practitioners, these are Wood Elves and Kith who have learned to channel the raw power of life through understanding the natural cycles of blood, sap, and sacrifice. They serve as healers, warriors, and guardians, maintaining harmony with the natural world.

**Bloodweavers**: Masters who specialize in blood magic and the connection between animal blood and plant sap, able to use life force to heal others or gain power. Their spellbooks are written in blood and bound with living skin.

**Lifeshapers**: Advanced practitioners who can perceive and manipulate the vital currents that flow through all living things - from the sap in trees to the blood in veins. They often become advisors to tribal leaders or guardians of sacred groves.

**Thornwardens**: Vitae specialists who focus on protective magic, using blood sacrifices and primal rituals to ward against threats while maintaining the natural balance. They often patrol the borders between Dorthonia and Nan-Avathar.

### Dress and Appearance
Vitae practitioners favor natural materials and earth tones, often stained with blood and decorated with bone and antler. Their robes are typically woven from plant fibers and animal hides, dyed with blood and natural pigments that shift color with the seasons. Many wear living accessories: crowns of thorns that draw blood, belts of braided sinew, or cloaks lined with fur that still twitches with residual life.

Their spell components are kept in leather pouches that smell of blood, earth, and growing things. Senior practitioners often bear ritual scarification that resembles blood vessels or bone patterns, and their eyes sometimes take on a crimson or amber tint from years of channeling life energy through sacrifice.

### Sacred Components and Materials
**Blood Ink**: The signature material of Vitae magic, this ink is brewed from the practitioner's own blood, mixed with crushed berries and spring water blessed by moonlight. When used to inscribe spells, the ink continues to flow and pulse, with words that can rewrite themselves as the caster's life force changes.

**Heartwood Staves**: Carved from trees that died naturally, these staves retain the tree's life memory and amplify nature magic. The most prized are made from trees that lived through multiple centuries and are often decorated with bone and blood.

**Blood Dew**: Collected at dawn from plants that have been watered with blood, these dewdrops serve as focusing elements for healing and sacrificial spells. Each type of plant imparts different properties - rose blood dew for emotional healing, oak blood dew for strength.

**Sacrifice Stones**: Small river stones that have been buried in fertile soil and anointed with blood for a full year. They become infused with concentrated life energy and are used to anchor longer-lasting Vitae effects.

**Bone Charms**: Carved from the bones of sacrificed animals or fallen warriors, these charms correspond to different aspects of Vitae magic - wolf bones for strength, deer bones for grace, bear bones for protection.

### Traditions and Practices
Vitae magic-users often begin each day with the "Blood Meditation," cutting their palms and letting their blood flow into the earth while extending their consciousness into the root networks of nearby plants. They maintain personal gardens where they grow their spell components and perform sacrificial rituals to test new magical techniques.

During the changing of seasons, Vitae practitioners gather for "Bloodtide Ceremonies" where they share knowledge, perform group sacrifices, and renew their vows to protect the natural world through blood and sacrifice. These gatherings often take place in hidden clearings marked by standing stones covered in blood-stained ogham runes.

Many Vitae magic-users undertake pilgrimages to the Worldtree, a massive oak said to grow at the heart of Dorthonia, whose roots supposedly extend to every forest in the known world. Touching its bark while offering blood is believed to grant deeper understanding of life's primal nature and the power of sacrifice.
