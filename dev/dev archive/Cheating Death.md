# Cheating Death

When steel meets fate, Wåndyr should feel sharp—quick turns, hard choices, and consequences you feel. Letting PCs drip into negatives, bargaining with potions, or juggling edge-case stabilization can bog that feeling down. This variant treats 0 Hearts as death in the scene, but keeps the campaign moving: heroes can respawn at camp at the next Muster—if they're willing to bear a mark from beyond.

This preserves tension ("we might lose them right now"), removes the healing-tax mini-game, and hands agency back to the player at the exact moment it matters most: Is this a heroic end, or do we cheat Death?

## RULES

**Trigger:** When a character reaches 0 Hearts, they die (drop immediately out of action). Allies may drag the body if fictionally possible.

**At Camp (Next Muster):** The player chooses one:

- **Let Death Have Them:** The character is gone. Consider awarding a small narrative boon to the party (relic, omen, rumor) at the GM's discretion.
- **Cheat Death:** The character respawns at camp at the next Muster (not sooner), with no permanent Heart loss. Roll d66 on the Cheating Death Marks table and record the result.

**Tone Key:** Any result containing a 1 is Spicy (ominous, cursed, scarred). Any result containing a 6 is Sweet (mystical, blessed). A result with both (e.g., 16, 61) can carry both flavors.

**Sting:** Cheaters of Death sit out the remainder of the current expedition/session and return next session. (Optional: also skip one Camp Action, at GM discretion.)

**Repeat Offenders:** Each time a character cheats Death again, roll anew (reroll duplicates or keep multiple marks). After three cheats, Death's attention is fixed—the next 0 Hearts is likely final unless the table (or a miracle) says otherwise.

## CHEATING DEATH MARKS

**1.1** Jagged black scar across throat that never fades   **1.2** Left hand withers and blackens at the fingertips   **1.3** Deep claw marks rake down your spine   **1.4** Teeth rot and fall out one by one over a week   **1.5** Open wound on your chest that seeps but never closes   **1.6** Bone-deep scars pulse with whispers from beyond

**2.1** Permanent limp as one leg drags behind   **2.2** Steel grows cold and dull in your hands for a moment before striking   **2.3** You leave damp footprints on dry stone   **2.4** Ravens trail your path for three days after each death you witness   **2.5** The dying mistake you for an old friend and try to speak   **2.6** Animals growl and bare teeth when you approach

**3.1** Eyes cloud white when you sleep; sometimes stay that way   **3.2** Candles bow their flames toward you when you enter   **3.3** Sudden noises never startle you again   **3.4** Your scars settle into unreadable runes   **3.5** A corpse exhales when you touch it   **3.6** The next creature to die near you passes peacefully

**4.1** Chunks of hair fall out leaving patchy bald scars   **4.2** Your footsteps echo as if down a long corridor, even outdoors   **4.3** You dream vividly of those you have slain and wake knowing one true thing   **4.4** You sense unseen watchers and know when eyes are on you   **4.5** Spirits step aside to let you pass   **4.6** Moonlight reveals ghostly figures only you can see

**5.1** Skin splits and cracks like dried earth across your arms   **5.2** The air thickens when you speak of death; others fall quiet   **5.3** Water resists you—you cannot drown unless you wish it   **5.4** You feel the instant a nearby soul departs   **5.5** Under moonlight, your skin holds a soft, pale glow   **5.6** A dying fire rekindles when you sleep beside it

**6.1** Bone protrudes through skin at your collarbone   **6.2** You can smell rain a minute before the first drop—always   **6.3** On restful nights, the stars seem closer above your bedroll   **6.4** The first wound you take each dawn leaves no scar   **6.5** Children and animals trust you without cause   **6.6** Death shows mercy once: ignore a single fatal blow this campaign

## GM NOTES

Keep the table mostly narrative. If you want a nudge, limit it to tiny, flavorful edges (1/scene advantage to a single check, 1/session omen, a one-time "ignore a wound" on 6.4/6.6, brief hesitation from undead on 3.5, etc.).

Avoid permanent Heart penalties—no downward spiral. The sting is the time cost (missing the rest of the expedition) and the mark that colors future scenes.
