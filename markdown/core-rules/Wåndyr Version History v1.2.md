# Wåndyr Version History

## Version 1.2 - January 17, 2026

- Added Journeys as an alternative to Rumors
- Replaced Turn Clock system with simpler Turns/Rounds/Adventures system
- Two-handed weapons: roll 2d6, keep best (if used one-handed, keep worst)
- Simplified combat right-of-way rules: ready beats unready; clearer hierarchy (melee > missiles, reach when approaching, smaller in close quarters); added "when in doubt, simultaneous" default
- Clarified Invocation resolution: added guidance for Guide to determine effects using Lore, Alignment, context, and Oracle; spell level equivalent up to Hearts lost

## Version 1.1 - January 7, 2026

### Major System Overhauls

#### Alignment System replaces Party Level

- **Old:** Party Level was a shared group advancement costing 2,000+ gp
- **New:** Alignment measures individual connection to concepts (magic school, combat style, faction, being). Count relevant Advancements and divide by 3.
- **Rationale:** Creates more personalized character progression tied to actual play choices rather than gold accumulation.

#### Morale System added (new major section)

- **Leadership:** Party Leader gains Hearts equal to chosen Alignment Level; can absorb damage for companions
- **Hospitality:** Sharing provisions restores 1 Heart per character; Host role with Comforts
- **Music:** Bard role can restore Hearts via performance; Songs grant morale benefits
- **Animal Companions:** Animals in Item Slots can restore 1 Heart per Muster
- **Rationale:** Captures the Hobbit-inspired theme of fellowship and home-longing on the road.

#### Attempting the Extraordinary (new section)

- Unified mechanic for heroic feats, spells, and invocations
- Pay Hearts equal to Level; maximum Level limited by relevant Alignment
- Guide sets Oracle target based on situation

#### Death System overhaul (new section)

- At 0 Hearts, choose Offscreen or Onscreen death
- **Offscreen:** Uncertain death; may return via Invocation at future Muster
- **Onscreen:** One final heroic action, requires Sweet to succeed
- Double Spicy (1,1) = immediate final death

### Oracle and Resolution Changes

#### Sweet and Spicy expanded

- **Old:** Sweet/Spicy added stylistic flair; Double 6s/1s gave bonus/penalty
- **New:** Double Sweet/Spicy triggers extreme twists—ask the Oracle a question. Double Sweet can lead to learning Knacks (3 XP).

#### Insight categories revised

- **Old:** TRAIT, ITEM, SKILL, NAME, ASSIST, EFFORT
- **New:** TRAIT, SKILL, ITEM, BYNAME, FEAT, EFFORT
- **FEAT added:** Advantage if you've overcome this foe/situation before in the game
- **ASSIST removed:** Streamlined

#### Other Oracle changes

- Who Asks? clarified: Players roll for direct character actions; Guide rolls for unestablished facts
- Area of Effect rules added for dragonbreath, fireballs, etc.

### Magic System Overhaul

- Canting added as 7th magic school (Thievery, Thieves' Cant)
- Spell Casting restructured: Prerequisites require Spellbook (or known spell); loud precise words

### Other Changes

- Rumor Updates: Guide updates each rumor at Muster; roll and combine if characters didn't act
- Location Updates: Roll and combine on visit to add detail
- Game Flow section added with Turn Clock mechanics (glass bowl + dice)
- Combat Rounds defined as 10-second intervals
- Camp Followers: Non-mustered characters follow to set up camp
- Sandbox Play section moved to back of book as summary
- Saving Throws section removed (effects now use Oracle directly)

## Version 0.7

### Sweet and Spicy: clarified intent that this is about "style, not substance"

- **Old:** If any die rolls 6 the answer is more "sweet", and if any die rolls 1 the answer is more "spicy". Never remove a 1 or 6 from the roll.
- **New:** When any die rolls a 6, the outcome is "Sweet," adding a stylistic flair. Double 6s indicate a bonus or benefit. Conversely, if any die rolls a 1, the outcome is "Spicy," also adding style. Double 1s signify a penalty or complication.
- **Rationale:** The original examples were about a guard sniped with "sweet" leaving a spotless uniform. This is "style"...there isn't extra damage or other guards dying as collateral. However, players have had a ton of fun with sweet and spicy, sometimes getting a bit carried away. The new wording attempts to create space for this (since it's fun), but also creates room for an appropriate cost.

### Reframe Melee Combat broad penalty as a more specific bonus

- **Old:** A character is "in Melee" if they are within reach (~10 feet) of an enemy who is making a melee attack. The character has Disadvantage on all rolls unless they attempt an attack with a close combat weapon.
- **New:** When two opponents attempt melee attacks against each other, reduce hits to each other by 1 Heart.
- **Rationale:** This preserves the intention that characters in melee should do melee, not other kinds of actions, but reframes it as a bonus rather than a severe un-fun penalty.

### Other changes

- Initiative: minor edits to clarify intent
