# Wandyr Levels

1. Traits, Skills, and Names all start at level 1. Can raise the level by paying xp equal to next level
2. New Skills and Names cost 1 xp.
3. New Traits cost xp equal to current number of Traits.

## Rationale/Notes

- Traits are broader and more foundational so its harder to get more of them. However, that is mostly a flaw of traits—it would be better if they were specific—can we define traits so they are as specific as skills?
- Trait levels and skill levels would need an in-game benefit to justify spending xp on them.
- Should we replace "Assist" with "Expertise"? (A weapon or armor skill might damage or add iron hearts, but that does not address other examples.)
- Should we remove traits or make them just about roleplaying? Magic schools could become skills.
