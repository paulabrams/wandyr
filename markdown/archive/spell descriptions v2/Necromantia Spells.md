# Necromantia (Death) Spells

*Death, spirits, and decay. The forbidden magic of the Avathars, inscribed in blood and bone. Practitioners bind spirits, animate dead, drain life force, and manipulate the boundary between life and death.*

**6.1.1** *Animate Skeleton*: Raise bones. You channel dark energy into a set of bones within 30 feet, causing them to assemble into a skeletal minion. **Level** (number of skeletons animated). **Instant** (1 action): The skeleton performs a single task or attack (1d6 damage) before crumbling. **Active** (2+ actions): The skeleton remains animated throughout the encounter, has 3 hit points, and follows simple commands until destroyed or the scene ends.  
**6.1.2** *Black Sacrament*: Death ritual. You perform a ritual that sacrifices 1 hit point to empower your necromantic magic. Dark runes appear on your flesh, glowing with malevolent energy. **Level** (bonus necrotic damage dice). **Instant** (1 action): Your next spell this turn deals +1d6 necrotic damage. **Active** (2+ actions): All your necromantic spells deal +1d6 necrotic damage for the remainder of the encounter. This sacrifice cannot reduce you below 1 hit point.  
**6.1.3** *Blood Curse*: Sacrifice life. You cut yourself and speak a curse, taking 1d6 damage to place a hex on a target within 60 feet. Blood droplets float through the air toward the target, marking them with crimson stains. **Level** (number of targets affected). **Instant** (1 action): The target has disadvantage on their next roll. **Active** (2+ actions): The target has disadvantage on all rolls until the curse is triggered or the encounter ends.  
**6.1.4** *Blood Track*: Follow wounds. You sense the direction and approximate distance to any bleeding creature you've seen injured. The spell creates a faint red trail visible only to you, leading toward the wounded target. **Level** (tracking range in miles). **Instant** (1 action): Sense direction to one bleeding creature within the current area. **Active** (2+ actions): Track all bleeding creatures within 1 mile throughout the scene, with the trail growing brighter as you approach.  
**6.1.5** *Bone Puppet*: Animate dead. You animate a Small or smaller corpse within 30 feet, controlling it like a marionette. Spectral strings of dark energy connect your fingers to the animated corpse. **Level** (size of creature controlled up to Medium+1 per Level). **Instant** (1 action): The puppet performs one simple action or attack (1d6 damage) before collapsing. **Active** (2+ actions): The puppet remains under your control for the encounter, moving at half speed and following commands until concentration is lost.  
**6.1.6** *Bone Shield*: Skeletal armor. Bones from nearby corpses or the ground float around you in a protective orbit. When the shield absorbs damage, bone fragments scatter dramatically before reforming. **Level** (AC bonus and hits absorbed). **Instant** (1 action): Grants +2 AC against the next attack made against you. **Active** (2+ actions): Grants +2 AC against all attacks for the encounter, but only absorbs three hits before the bones are depleted.

**6.2.1** *Command Undead*: Control dead. You issue a command to an undead creature within 30 feet that has 2 Hit Dice or fewer. Glowing purple runes appear around the target's head as your will compels obedience. **Level** (Hit Dice of undead controlled). **Instant** (1 action): The target must obey a single-word command on its next turn ("flee," "grovel," "halt," "approach"). **Active** (2+ actions): The target obeys your verbal commands for the encounter or until it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw at the end of its turn.  
**6.2.2** *Comprehend Dead*: Dead languages. You can understand written languages left by the deceased, including ancient scripts and forgotten tongues. The text glows with faint spectral light as words rearrange into comprehensible meaning. **Level** (age of writings comprehended in centuries). **Instant** (1 action): Understand one piece of dead writing (epitaph, curse, final message). **Active** (2+ actions): Understand all dead languages throughout the scene, including continuous translation of newly discovered texts.  
**6.2.3** *Corpse Explosion*: Detonate dead. You cause a corpse within 60 feet to violently explode. Bone fragments and putrid flesh spray outward in a gruesome display, completely destroying the corpse. **Level** (explosive force becomes more devastating, necrotic energy spreads further, death field effects intensify). **Instant** (1 action): The explosion deals 1d6 necrotic damage to all creatures within 10 feet (Dex save for half). **Active** (2+ actions): The explosion creates a lingering death field that deals 1d6 necrotic damage to creatures entering or starting their turn in the 10-foot area.  
**6.2.4** *Dark Blessing*: Death's power. You invoke the favor of death itself as dark energy swirls around you and whispers from beyond guide your actions. Your eyes turn black as the blessing takes hold. **Level** (number of allies affected by blessing). **Instant** (1 action): Gain advantage on your next roll this turn. **Active** (2+ actions): Gain advantage on all rolls for the encounter, but each use leaves you feeling progressively colder and more hollow.  
**6.2.5** *Death Gate*: Portal to underworld. You create a 5-foot diameter portal to the realm of the dead, appearing as a swirling vortex of darkness. Living creatures within 10 feet feel an overwhelming urge to approach (Wisdom save to resist). **Level** (portal size in 5-foot increments and attraction range). **Instant** (1 action): The portal briefly opens, dealing 1d6 necrotic damage to living creatures touching it before snapping shut. **Active** (2+ actions): The portal remains open for the encounter, allowing undead passage and continuously threatening living creatures who approach.  
**6.2.6** *Death Touch*: Wither life. Your touch becomes infused with necrotic energy, causing living tissue to wither and decay. Your hand appears skeletal and cold. **Level** (withering effect becomes more potent and lasting, necrotic energy penetrates deeper, life force drain intensifies). **Instant** (1 action): Your next touch deals 1d6 necrotic damage and reduces max HP by the same amount until a long rest. **Active** (2+ actions): All your touch attacks throughout the encounter have this withering effect. Undead touched by this spell are healed for 1d6 hit points instead.

**6.3.1** *Death Ward*: Protect from necrotic. You shroud yourself or an ally within touch range in protective deathly energy. A faint aura of pale light surrounds the protected individual, visible only to those with magical sight. **Level** (number of targets protected). **Instant** (1 action): Grants resistance to the next source of necrotic damage or advantage on the next death saving throw. **Active** (2+ actions): Grants resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on death saves for the encounter, but dissipates if more than 10 necrotic damage is taken at once.  
**6.3.2** *Doom Mark*: Mark for death. You place a spectral skull mark on a creature within 60 feet that only you and other necromancers can see. The mark appears as a glowing sigil on the creature's forehead, pulsing ominously with each heartbeat. **Level** (death mark becomes more potent and persistent, can affect additional targets, necrotic resonance strengthens). **Instant** (1 action): Your next attack against the target deals +1d6 necrotic damage. **Active** (2+ actions): All your attacks against the target deal +1d6 necrotic damage for the encounter. Only one creature can bear your doom mark at a time.  
**6.3.3** *Drain Life*: Steal vitality. You siphon life force from a target within 30 feet. Dark tendrils of energy flow from the victim to you, visible as wisps of shadow. The target feels chill and weakness as their vitality transfers. **Level** (life drain becomes more efficient and powerful, energy transfer intensifies, vitality theft deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Deal 1d6 necrotic damage and heal yourself for half the damage dealt. **Active** (2+ actions): Each turn, automatically drain 1d6 life from the target and heal yourself for half, until they move out of range or break line of sight. No effect on undead or constructs.  
**6.3.4** *Fear Aura*: Death terror. You emanate an aura of supernatural dread in a 15-foot radius. The air grows cold and heavy, filled with whispers of mortality and doom, while your shadow writhes and reaches toward nearby creatures. **Level** (dread aura spreads further and becomes more oppressive, fear effects deepen, death whispers multiply). **Instant** (1 action): All creatures within 15 feet must save or be frightened until the end of their next turn. **Active** (2+ actions): The aura persists for the encounter, frightening creatures that enter for the first time or start their turn within range.  
**6.3.5** *Ghost Bind*: Trap spirits. You create spectral chains that bind an incorporeal undead creature within 30 feet. The ghostly chains appear as glowing links of pale energy wrapped around the spirit. **Level** (Hit Dice of spirits bound and number of targets). **Instant** (1 action): The target makes a Charisma save or is restrained until the end of its next turn. **Active** (2+ actions): The target is restrained for the encounter and cannot use incorporeal abilities or teleport until it succeeds on a Charisma save at the end of its turn.  
**6.3.6** *Grave Mist*: Create death fog. You conjure a 20-foot radius sphere of gray, cloying mist centered on a point within 60 feet. The mist heavily obscures the area while undead creatures remain unaffected and can see normally through the supernatural fog. **Level** (death fog spreads further and becomes more toxic, necrotic effects intensify, mist persistence improves). **Instant** (1 action): The mist appears briefly, dealing 1d6 necrotic damage to all living creatures within before dispersing. **Active** (2+ actions): The mist persists for the encounter, dealing 1d6 necrotic damage to living creatures starting their turn within, until dispersed by strong wind.

**6.4.1** *Life Sense*: Detect living. You can sense the location of living creatures within 60 feet, even through walls and barriers. Living beings appear as warm, glowing outlines in your vision, pulsing with each heartbeat. **Level** (life detection becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can sense subtler life signs, perception clarity improves). **Instant** (1 action): Sense all living creatures within 60 feet for a brief moment, getting a snapshot of their current positions. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously track all living creatures for the encounter, watching their movements in real-time. Cannot determine creature type, only that they live. Undead and constructs remain invisible.  
**6.4.2** *Mass Animation*: Raise army. You animate up to three Small corpses or sets of bones within 30 feet. Each animated creature has 1 hit point and deals 1d6 damage with simple attacks. **Level** (animation becomes more efficient and powerful, can raise more undead, animated creatures become more durable). **Instant** (1 action): The undead perform one coordinated attack or action before crumbling to dust. **Active** (2+ actions): The undead minions follow basic commands for the encounter, crumbling when reduced to 0 hit points or when the scene ends.  
**6.4.3** *Marble Madness*: Death's toys. You animate small bones within 60 feet to roll and bounce like marbles across the ground. The chattering bone fragments whisper fragments of their former lives as they skitter about. **Level** (bone animation spreads over wider areas, fragments become more disruptive, whispers grow more disturbing). **Instant** (1 action): Creatures in a 20-foot area must make a Dex save or fall prone as bones briefly animate underfoot. **Active** (2+ actions): The entire 60-foot area becomes difficult terrain for the encounter as bones continuously roll and trip creatures moving through.  
**6.4.4** *Pain Echo*: Share suffering. When you take damage, you can force a creature within 30 feet to share your pain. Dark energy connects you to the victim, creating a visible link of suffering. **Level** (number of creatures linked to share pain). **Instant** (1 action): The next time you take damage this turn, one target within 30 feet takes half that damage as psychic damage (no save). **Active** (2+ actions): For the encounter, each time you take damage, you can use your reaction to make one creature within 30 feet take half the damage as psychic damage.  
**6.4.5** *Plague Wind*: Spread death. You create a 30-foot cone of diseased wind that carries the essence of decay and sickness. The wind carries the stench of rotting flesh and grave soil. **Level** (diseased wind spreads further and becomes more virulent, decay effects intensify, sickness becomes more persistent). **Instant** (1 action): Creatures in the cone make a Con save or take 1d6 necrotic damage and are poisoned until the end of their next turn. **Active** (2+ actions): The wind lingers for the encounter, poisoning creatures that enter or start their turn in the area (save ends at end of turn).  
**6.4.6** *Psychometry*: Read death echoes. By touching an object or location where someone died, you experience visions of their final moments from the victim's perspective. You feel their emotions and pain but take no damage. **Level** (death echoes become clearer and more detailed, can perceive older deaths, emotional resonance deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Experience a 6-second vision of one death that occurred here recently. **Active** (2+ actions): Experience detailed death echoes from multiple victims over time, seeing up to several deaths that occurred in this location, with older deaths becoming progressively fainter.

**6.5.1** *Raise Dead*: Create undead. You target a humanoid corpse, raising it as a zombie under your control. Dark energy flows into the corpse as it lurches back to unholy life. **Level** (Hit Dice of corpse that can be raised). **Instant** (1 action): The zombie performs one action (attack, move, or simple task) before collapsing permanently. **Active** (2+ actions): The zombie follows your mental commands for the encounter with standard zombie statistics, then collapses and cannot be reanimated by this spell.  
**6.5.2** *Sniff*: Smell death. Your sense of smell becomes supernaturally acute for death-related scents. You can detect undead creatures within 60 feet by smell alone, even if hidden or invisible. Different types of death produce distinct odors you can identify. **Level** (death scent detection becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can distinguish subtler death traces, olfactory precision improves). **Instant** (1 action): Detect all undead within 60 feet and determine how one nearby corpse died. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously track undead throughout the encounter and analyze death scents in detail, determining time and manner of death for all corpses in the area.  
**6.5.3** *Soul Cage*: Trap essence. You create a spectral prison around a creature within 30 feet that has 10 hit points or fewer. The cage appears as a ribcage of spectral bones with dark energy bars between them. **Level** (soul cage becomes more powerful and inescapable, can trap stronger creatures, spiritual binding intensifies). **Instant** (1 action): The target makes a Charisma save or is restrained until the end of their next turn. **Active** (2+ actions): The target remains caged for the encounter, restrained and unable to use movement abilities, until they succeed on a Charisma save at the end of their turn.  
**6.5.4** *Soul Rend*: Tear spirit. You make a spectral claw attack against a creature within 30 feet, attempting to damage their very soul. The victim feels invisible claws tearing at their essence. **Level** (spiritual claws become sharper and more devastating, soul damage deepens, essence tearing becomes more thorough). **Instant** (1 action): Make a spell attack; on hit, deal 1d6 psychic damage and reduce max HP by the same amount until a long rest (Wisdom save negates HP reduction). **Active** (2+ actions): Your attacks for the encounter have spectral claws that automatically attempt to rend souls on successful hits.  
**6.5.5** *Speak with Dead*: Question spirits. You commune with the spirit of a corpse. The spirit answers truthfully but only knows what it knew in life, speaking in a hollow, echoing voice. The corpse's eyes glow with pale light during conversation. **Level** (number of questions and age of corpse in years). **Instant** (1 action): Ask one question of the dead and receive a brief answer before the spirit departs. **Active** (2+ actions): Conduct an extended conversation for the scene, asking multiple questions and receiving detailed responses until the spirit chooses to depart.  
**6.5.6** *Spirit Chains*: Bind ghost. Ethereal chains of pale energy wrap around an incorporeal creature within 30 feet, forcing it into a partially corporeal state. While bound, the creature loses incorporeal abilities and takes normal damage from non-magical attacks. **Level** (Hit Dice of spirits bound and chain strength). **Instant** (1 action): The target makes a Charisma save or becomes corporeal until the end of its next turn. **Active** (2+ actions): The spectral chains remain visible for the encounter, keeping the creature corporeal until it succeeds on a Charisma save at the end of its turn.

**6.6.1** *Spirit Form*: Become ghostly. Your body becomes translucent and partially incorporeal, allowing you to move through objects and creatures as difficult terrain. Your appearance becomes ghostly and pale with a faint spectral glow. **Level** (number of allies affected by spirit form). **Instant** (1 action): Become incorporeal until the end of your turn, gaining resistance to all damage except force and radiant. **Active** (2+ actions): Remain incorporeal for the encounter but cannot manipulate objects or cast spells with material components. Take 1d6 force damage if you end your turn inside an object.  
**6.6.2** *Truth Sense*: Death's truth. Death reveals deceptions to you through supernatural insight that cannot be fooled by illusions or enchantments. When someone lies, their words appear to drip with spectral blood only you can see. **Level** (range of truth detection in 30-foot increments). **Instant** (1 action): Automatically detect if the next statement made in your presence is a lie. **Active** (2+ actions): Detect all lies spoken in your presence for the encounter. The spell reveals falsehood but not the actual truth.  
**6.6.3** *Undead Sight*: See spirits. You can see invisible undead creatures and spirits within 60 feet as if they were visible. Your eyes become pale and clouded, giving you an unsettling appearance. You can also see spiritual auras where death occurred. **Level** (sight range in 60-foot increments and age of death auras). **Instant** (1 action): See all invisible undead within 60 feet for a brief moment and detect recent deaths in the immediate area. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously see invisible undead for the encounter and track incorporeal spirits, detecting hidden burial sites and old battlefields throughout the scene.  
**6.6.4** *Vampiric Touch*: Drain energy. Your touch drains life force, healing you when you deal damage to living creatures with melee attacks. Your hands glow with dark energy and feel unnaturally cold. **Level** (percentage of damage healed per 25%). **Instant** (1 action): Your next successful melee attack heals you for half the damage dealt to a living creature. **Active** (2+ actions): For the encounter, all your melee attacks against living creatures heal you for half the damage dealt. No effect against undead or constructs.  
**6.6.5** *Wizard Mark*: Death runes. You inscribe invisible necromantic runes on an object or surface within touch range. The mark appears as glowing skull symbols to you and other necromancers, carrying a faint aura of death magic that makes living creatures uneasy. **Level** (number of marks and tracking range in miles). **Instant** (1 action): Create a temporary mark that lasts for the current scene, allowing you to track the object's location. **Active** (2+ actions): Create a permanent mark that lasts for weeks, always showing you the direction and distance to marked objects within 1 mile.  
**6.6.6** *Perfect Death*: Ultimate end. You attempt to instantly slay a creature within 30 feet that has 15 hit points or fewer. Dark energy surrounds the victim as death itself reaches out to claim them. **Level** (death magic becomes more potent and irresistible, can affect stronger creatures, mortality compulsion intensifies). **Instant** (1 action): The target makes a Constitution save; on failure, they drop to 0 hit points and begin death saves immediately. On success, take 1d6 necrotic damage. **Active** (2+ actions): For the encounter, your attacks against creatures with 15 or fewer HP force Constitution saves or instant death. No effect on undead or constructs.
