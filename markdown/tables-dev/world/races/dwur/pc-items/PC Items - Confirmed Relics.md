# PC Items - Confirmed Relics

## The Three Keys of Making (PC-Owned Items)

### **1. The Resonance Crystal** ✓ (Acquired by Party)
*Fragment of Thurkaz's original vault-seal*

**Appearance:** Blue-white crystal that pulses with otherworldly energy  
**Relic Type:** Lesser Relic (Thurkaz fragment)  
**Corruption Level:** Minor - causes brief flashes of ancient memories

#### **Practical Powers:**
- **Harmonic Unlock:** Resonates with Dwur lock mechanisms, opens sealed doors
- **Echo Location:** Crystal glows brighter when near other Thurkaz relics
- **Ward Detection:** Pulses rapidly when approaching magical traps/seals
- **Memory Touch:** Touching to stone walls shows brief glimpses of past events
- **Frequency Match:** Can attune to specific magical frequencies for 10 minutes

#### **Vault Applications:**
- Opens the **Sleeper's Gate** (first seal)
- Activates **Resonance Hall** harmonic locks
- Detects hidden **memory triggers** in walls
- Guides party toward **Observatory of Hours**
- Warns of **dangerous First Light concentrations**

---

### **2. The Crystalline Prism** ✓ (From Iron Chest)
*Refined focusing lens for Thurkaz's sealed knowledge*

**Appearance:** Multi-faceted crystal that refracts light into impossible colors  
**Relic Type:** Lesser Relic (Thurkaz crafting tool)  
**Corruption Level:** Moderate - shows glimpses of forbidden knowledge

#### **Practical Powers:**
- **Light Splitting:** Divides single light source into multiple beams
- **True Sight:** Reveals invisible writing, hidden passages, illusions
- **Star Map Projection:** Projects astronomical calculations on surfaces  
- **Memory Focus:** Concentrates crystal visions into useful information
- **Seal Reading:** Translates Dwur runic seals and warnings

#### **Vault Applications:**
- Required for **Observatory of Hours** star charts
- Reveals hidden **astronomical mechanisms**
- Projects **binding post locations** when combined with maps
- Shows **true paths** through illusion chambers
- Focuses **Heart of First Light** safely (if found)

---

### **3. The Three Resonance Regulators** ✓ (From Iron Chest)
*Thurkaz's control mechanisms for dangerous vault energies*

**Appearance:** Metal spheres covered in Dwur runes and astronomical symbols  
**Relic Type:** Greater Relic Set (Vault control system)  
**Corruption Level:** High when all three used together

#### **Individual Sphere Powers:**
- **Sphere of Binding:** Temporarily seals dangerous magical effects
- **Sphere of Revealing:** Forces hidden mechanisms to activate
- **Sphere of Balancing:** Stabilizes unstable magical energies

#### **Combined Powers (All Three):**
- **Vault Mastery:** Can override any single Dwur magical system
- **Stellar Alignment:** Manipulates astronomical mechanisms
- **First Light Control:** Safely channels dangerous vault energies
- **Emergency Seal:** Can re-seal breached Thurkaz protections (once only)
- **Heart Activation:** Required to safely activate the Heart of First Light

#### **Vault Applications:**
- **Essential for final chamber** - Heart of First Light
- Override **Seven Circles of Warding** (one per use)
- Stabilize **collapsing reality** in deep chambers
- Control **Stellar Forge** without being consumed
- **Restart or permanently seal** the entire Vault system

---

### **4. The Warning Tablet** ✓ (From Iron Chest)
*Thurkaz's final message to future generations*

**Appearance:** Stone tablet with shifting Dwur runes  
**Relic Type:** Minor Relic (Divine warning)  
**Corruption Level:** None - protective rather than dangerous

#### **Practical Powers:**
- **Prophetic Guidance:** Runes change to show immediate dangers
- **Thurkaz Communication:** Provides cryptic hints from the Silent Smith
- **Protective Ward:** Grants +2 saves vs First Light corruption while held
- **True Warning:** Cannot be fooled by illusions or false information
- **Final Truth:** Reveals the real purpose/danger of any Vault chamber

#### **Vault Applications:**
- **Warns of imminent dangers** before entering rooms
- **Guides moral choices** about Vault's ultimate fate
- **Reveals true nature** of Heart of First Light before activation
- **Shows consequences** of different actions (restart/seal/destroy)
- **Thurkaz's blessing** for those who choose wisdom over greed

---

## **The Scroll Case Maps & Calculations**
*Practical navigation and puzzle-solving tools*

### **Mountain Pass Maps**
- **+2 to navigation** in Serpent's Teeth region
- **Shows safe routes** avoiding avalanche zones
- **Marks hidden entrances** to Vault system
- **Weather prediction** based on Dwur mountain lore

### **Astronomical Calculations**
- **Required for Observatory of Hours** puzzles
- **Predicts celestial conjunction** timing exactly
- **Shows when Vault powers** are strongest/weakest
- **Guides stellar alignment** mechanisms

### **Architectural Diagrams**
- **Reveals Vault layout** (partial, some areas missing)
- **Shows trap locations** and disarmament methods
- **Indicates structural weak points** for emergency escape
- **Maps the Seven Circles** and their connection points

## Using Multiple Items Together

### **Crystal + Prism Combination:**
- **Enhanced True Sight** - see through any Vault illusion
- **Perfect Navigation** - never get lost in shifting passages
- **Danger Prediction** - know exact nature of threats ahead

### **All Three Keys Active:**
- **Vault Mastery** - can solve any single puzzle/obstacle
- **Thurkaz's Favor** - protection from worst corruption effects
- **Ultimate Choice** - when reaching Heart of First Light, can choose to restart creation, seal it forever, or destroy it entirely

### **Corruption Interaction:**
Each item used reduces corruption from First Light gold by 1 stage (to minimum of none). The party's moral choices with these items determine whether Thurkaz aids or abandons them in the final chamber.
