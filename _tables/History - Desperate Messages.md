## Madness Indicators (Roll 1d20 when finding messages)

*Signs of declining mental state in writing*

1. **Repeated Words** - Same phrase carved dozens of times; obsessive compulsion
2. **Size Changes** - Letters get progressively larger/smaller; losing perspective
3. **Multiple Voices** - Different handwriting in same message; fractured personality
4. **Backwards Text** - Must read in mirror to understand; reality reversal
5. **Symbol Integration** - Words mixed with unknown symbols; external influence
6. **Blood Mixing** - Multiple blood types in same message; group madness
7. **Time Confusion** - Dates that don't make sense; temporal displacement
8. **Identity Loss** - Signature changes mid-message; forgetting self
9. **Circular Logic** - Messages that contradict themselves; cognitive dissonance
10. **Paranoid Delusions** - Messages about being watched; persecution complex
11. **Grandiose Claims** - Messages claiming divine knowledge; delusions of grandeur
12. **Perseveration** - Same word repeated endlessly; mental fixation
13. **Neologisms** - Made-up words or symbols; language breakdown
14. **Echolalia** - Repeating others' words; loss of original thought
15. **Word Salad** - Random words without meaning; thought disorder
16. **Flight of Ideas** - Rapid topic changes; manic thinking
17. **Tangential Speech** - Off-topic responses; loose associations
18. **Clang Associations** - Words chosen for sound, not meaning; thought disorder
19. **Perseveration** - Stuck on same topic; mental rigidity
20. **Circumstantiality** - Excessive detail without point; thought disorder

## The Secret (Hidden Pattern in Messages)

*When 3+ messages are found, roll 1d20*

1. **Past Life References** - Messages mention remembering "other deaths," "previous lives," "being here before"
2. **Identity Confusion** - "I was the Dwur," "I remember being," "which life is real?"
3. **Terrible Understanding** - The First Light shows all past incarnations - the weight of infinite memory drives people insane
4. **Reality Distortion** - Messages about things that shouldn't exist or events that couldn't happen
5. **Temporal Confusion** - References to events in wrong time periods or impossible chronologies
6. **Species Memory** - Remembering being different races or creatures
7. **Collective Consciousness** - Messages suggesting shared memories or group mind
8. **Cosmic Horror** - References to entities or concepts beyond human understanding
9. **Moral Weight** - Guilt over actions committed in past lives or alternate selves
10. **Existential Dread** - Questions about the nature of self and reality
11. **Memory Synchronization** - All bearers sharing the same memories
12. **Identity Fragmentation** - Multiple personalities emerging from past lives
13. **Temporal Echo** - Past events replaying in the present
14. **Spatial Distortion** - References to impossible spaces or locations
15. **Causality Breakdown** - Effects preceding causes or impossible sequences
16. **Consciousness Expansion** - Minds expanding beyond normal human limits
17. **Reality Softening** - Laws of nature becoming negotiable
18. **Creation Reversal** - Matter returning to energy or existence unraveling
19. **Soul Magnetism** - Dead refusing to stay buried or souls being drawn
20. **Memory Crystallization** - Thoughts becoming solid objects or memories made manifest
