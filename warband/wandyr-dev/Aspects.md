# Aspects

Aspects are Alignments, but per Muster.

Characters have multiple Alignments. Each has a level. This is simple in the core rules. For this variant, Alignments gain another layer of detail at Muster.

## Declaring an Aspect

At Muster, you may optionally choose one Trait and one Skill and assign them to one of your Alignments. This is your **Aspect** for the day — the facet of yourself you're leading with. For example, assign the trait "strong" and the skill "sword" to your "Fighter" Alignment: today you are the strong-armed swordsman.

## Sweet and Spicy

Aspects change *who narrates* twists, not how big they are:

- **Single Sweet/Spicy — the Guide narrates.** The world twists: the uniform is spotless, another guard glances over. One line, play moves on.
- **Double Sweet/Spicy — the player narrates, through their Aspect** (if it fits). The character asserts onto the world in the way they declared at Muster. The Trait colors *how* it happens ("strong" — you wrench the portcullis off its track); the Skill colors *what* it happens with ("sword" — the blade finds the gap in three quick beats).

**If the Aspect doesn't fit**, fall back to the core double rule: ask the Oracle a hopeful (double Sweet) or dire (double Spicy) question. Doubles are never wasted; the Aspect has first claim, not the only claim.

**Death keeps its exception.** Double Spicy on a dying character is still final — the Aspect narration becomes the manner of the end, not an escape from it.

## Design Notes

- **Declared fiction, randomized arrival.** A player can't assign "clever" and expect a standing bonus — the Aspect only speaks when doubles land. You choose at dawn who gets to write your big moments; the dice choose when.
- **No new machinery.** No extra dice, no charges, no table state. The randomness lives in dice already rolled; the declaration lives on your own sheet.
- **Frequency.** Doubles land ~2.8% per direction on a normal roll, ~7.4% double Sweet with Advantage — roughly once or twice per session per player, clustering when you act inside your competence. Your Aspect lights up where your Alignment already shines.
- **Authorship is the reward.** No mechanical rider on the double — narration rights are self-balancing and need no adjudication. (If doubles feel hollow in play, try adding the +1 from the alternate version below.)
- **Synergy with Character Advancement.** If double Sweet also grants an advancement claim, the sequence becomes: double Sweet → Aspect shapes the moment → advancement memorializes it. Aspects steer *where* characters grow.
- **Your defining trait cuts both ways.** On double Spicy, the Aspect is the *source* of the disaster — your fury carries you off the bridge. The trait that elevates you is the trait that undoes you.

## Alternate Version: Aspect Bonuses

Instead of (or in addition to) narration rights, the Aspect grants standing bonuses:

- **Trait**: Treat your Alignment as +1 if the trait is narrow and your roll is focused on that narrow slice. Declare the slice as a concrete phrase at Muster ("feats of raw strength, blade in hand") so the in-play check is binary.
- **Skill**: When rolling a d6 for effect with the assigned Skill, add +1.

*This version gives reliable, plannable bonuses but reintroduces a per-roll fit judgment for the Trait. The narration version above is fully self-adjudicating.*
