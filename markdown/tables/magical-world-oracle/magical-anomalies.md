# Magical Anomalies

Roll 2d6 for strange magical phenomena:

**1.1** Floating Islands - Land masses hover in air  
**1.2** Walking Trees - Plants move and speak  
**1.3** Talking Stones - Rocks communicate with people  
**1.4** Living Water - Rivers and lakes have consciousness  
**1.5** Breathing Earth - Ground inhales and exhales  
**1.6** Singing Wind - Air carries melodies

**2.1** Time Loops - Same events repeat endlessly  
**2.2** Reality Bubbles - Laws of physics change in areas  
**2.3** Memory Storms - Forgotten events become visible  
**2.4** Prophecy Echoes - Future glimpsed in reflections  
**2.5** Soul Winds - Spirits ride the breeze  
**2.6** Life Surges - All living things grow rapidly

**3.1** Magic Leaks - Spells escape their casters  
**3.2** Enchantment Bleed - Magical effects spread  
**3.3** Rune Storms - Magical symbols appear everywhere  
**3.4** Mana Pools - Raw magic collects in puddles  
**3.5** Spell Echoes - Magic repeats itself  
**3.6** Charm Residue - Bewitching effects linger

**4.1** Light Anomalies - Illumination behaves strangely  
**4.2** Shadow Creatures - Darkness takes physical form  
**4.3** Beast Transformations - Animals change shape  
**4.4** Craft Animations - Tools work themselves  
**4.5** Story Manifestations - Tales become real  
**4.6** Music Magic - Songs have physical effects

**5.1** Religious Miracles - Faith produces real effects  
**5.2** Heroic Resurrections - Dead warriors return  
**5.3** Merchant's Luck - Trade becomes supernatural  
**5.4** Scholar's Insight - Knowledge appears spontaneously  
**5.5** Warrior's Valor - Combat skills enhanced magically  
**5.6** Healer's Touch - Wounds heal miraculously

**6.1** Fey Intrusions - Otherworldly beings appear  
**6.2** Dragon Awakening - Ancient creatures stir  
**6.3** Undead Rising - Dead walk the earth  
**6.4** Elemental Storms - Natural forces rage  
**6.5** Shadow Invasion - Darkness spreads  
**6.6** Light Corruption - Illumination becomes dangerous
