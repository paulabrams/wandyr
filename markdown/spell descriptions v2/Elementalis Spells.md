# Elementalis (Elemental) Spells

*Primal forces, elements, and weather. The Baal inscribe dragon magic in burning runes carved in stone. Practitioners control weather, create barriers of ice and fire, command gravity, and shape elements.*

**2.1.1** *Control Weather* \- Command climate: Influence weather patterns in a small area to adjust conditions to your needs. **Level** (weather control becomes more precise and far-reaching, atmospheric sensitivity improves, climate influence deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief weather phenomenon like a gust of wind, temperature shift, or light precipitation that affects the immediate area. **Active** (2+ actions): Establish sustained weather control over a 100-foot radius, allowing you to gradually shift conditions like changing rain intensity, temperature, or wind direction. The effect works best with existing weather conditions rather than creating dramatic changes from nothing.

**2.1.2** *Crystal Growth* - Form gems: Encourage the formation of small crystals from existing mineral deposits you touch. **Level** (crystal quality improves, formation success rate increases, range of possible gem types expands). **Instant** (1 action): Create tiny crystal fragments suitable for spell components or simple decoration. **Active** (2+ actions): Produce larger, more valuable crystals worth 1-10 gold pieces, carefully shaping them from available mineral content. Higher levels yield more consistent results, better clarity, and may coax precious gems from mineral-rich sources. Rich mineral veins produce better results than common stone.

**2.1.3** *Dragon's Breath* \- Channel flame: Exhale a cone of elemental energy from your mouth in the manner of a dragon. **Level** (breath becomes more intense and dragonlike, cone widens slightly, secondary effects manifest). **Instant** (1 action): Release a 15-foot cone dealing 1d6+Level damage of your chosen element (fire burns, frost slows, lightning stuns). **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a continuous stream of elemental energy, allowing you to sweep the cone across multiple targets or sustain pressure on a single area. Higher levels produce more focused breath with enhanced elemental effects - fire may ignite surroundings, frost might create ice patches, lightning could arc between targets. You can choose fire, frost, or lightning when casting, and targets can dive for cover to reduce damage. The breath appears with appropriate draconic visual effects and sound.

**2.1.4** *Earth Shape* \- Mold stone: Make natural earth materials malleable like potter's clay for shaping. **Level** (material workability improves, harder substances become moldable, shaping precision increases). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly reshape a small amount of clay, mud, or soft stone into a simple form that hardens immediately. **Active** (2+ actions): Work with up to 10 cubic feet of material, carefully sculpting complex shapes and forms with your hands until satisfied. Higher levels allow working with denser stone, create more detailed sculptures, and maintain malleability for longer periods. The material hardens into its new permanent shape when you finish working. This spell works best on natural earth and stone but cannot affect worked masonry or metal.

**2.1.5** *Earthquake* \- Shake ground: Create tremors in the earth that destabilize the area around you. **Level** (tremor range gradually expands, seismic intensity deepens, ground disruption becomes more pronounced). **Instant** (1 action): Generate a single powerful shake in a 20-foot radius that can topple objects and cause creatures to stumble. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain continuous tremors that give creatures disadvantage on balance and fine motor actions while potentially triggering environmental hazards. The shaking can rattle doors, topple unstable objects, and cause people to lose their footing. The tremor is strong enough to trigger avalanches or collapses in already unstable areas.

**2.1.6** *Element Master* \- Complete control: Demonstrate precise control over small amounts of all four classical elements simultaneously. **Level** (number of elements controlled simultaneously, complexity of manipulations). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief but impressive display of elemental mastery that showcases your magical prowess. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain continuous control over fire, water, earth, and air at once, allowing complex manipulations and sustained demonstrations. You can maintain a flame in one hand, shape water with another, levitate small stones, and direct air currents all at once. This impressive display grants advantage on social interactions with those who witness it, emphasizing finesse over raw power.

**2.2.1** *Elemental Wall* \- Create barrier: Create a barrier of elemental energy to block passage or provide protection. **Level** (wall length increases by +5 feet per Level, durability +3 Hearts per Level spent). **Instant** (1 action): Raise a temporary wall 10 feet long and 5 feet high that provides immediate cover but dissipates quickly. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a stable elemental barrier that persists for the encounter, allowing you to reposition or reshape it as needed. You can choose ice (blocks movement), fire (deals 1d6 damage to those passing through), stone (solid barrier), or compressed air (deflects projectiles). The wall has 5 Hearts worth of durability and can be broken with sufficient force.

**2.2.2** *Fire Shield* \- Protective flames: Surround yourself with a warm aura of protective flame that wards off attackers. **Level** (flames burn hotter and more intensely, protective coverage expands, can shield additional allies). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief flare of protective fire that damages one creature striking you and provides momentary warmth and light. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a continuous fiery aura that consistently punishes melee attackers and provides ongoing warmth and illumination. The shield provides comfortable warmth in cold conditions and sheds light like a torch. Anyone who strikes you in melee combat takes 1d6 fire damage from the controlled flames that won't ignite your equipment.

**2.2.3** *Fog Cloud* \- Create mist: Generate a thick cloud of mist or steam that obscures vision in the targeted area. **Level** (mist spreads further and grows denser, duration extends, weather resistance improves). **Instant** (1 action): Create a burst of fog in a 20-foot radius that provides immediate concealment but disperses quickly. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a persistent fog cloud that you can move or reshape, providing ongoing tactical advantage. The fog heavily obscures vision but does not block sound or movement, and strong wind can disperse it. You can see through your own fog clearly, giving you an advantage against creatures blinded by it.

**2.2.4** *Force Wall* \- Energy barrier: Create an invisible barrier of compressed air that deflects physical objects and provides protection. **Level** (wall size increases by +5x5 feet per Level, strength increases). **Instant** (1 action): Generate a brief force barrier that can deflect a single attack or projectile before dissipating. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a stable wall 15 feet long and 10 feet high that consistently blocks attacks and can support weight. The wall can stop arrows, thrown weapons, and even charging creatures with a successful resistance roll. It provides partial cover against ranged attacks and can support weight like a solid surface.

**2.2.5** *Gravity Shift* \- Change gravity: Alter gravity's pull on objects and creatures to create tactical advantages. **Level** (gravitational control becomes more precise, affects larger masses, manipulation range gradually expands). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly affect gravity for objects under 50 pounds, causing them to float, fall upward, or become heavier for a moment. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain gravitational manipulation in a 10-foot radius, allowing sustained effects on movement and positioning. You can make objects float weightlessly, fall upward, or become three times heavier. Creatures can resist the effect but find their movement awkward and difficult, allowing for creative tactical solutions.

**2.2.6** *Heat Metal* \- Make objects burning: Make metal objects uncomfortably hot to force opponents to drop them or suffer ongoing discomfort. **Level** (number of objects affected, heat intensity and damage increase). **Instant** (1 action): Cause a single metal object within 30 feet to become briefly hot enough to force someone to drop it. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain intense heat on metal objects, causing ongoing discomfort and tactical disadvantage to those using them. The heat is not enough to cause serious burns but will make someone drop weapons, armor, or tools. The metal glows faintly red and radiates warmth, with heated armor causing disadvantage on actions.

**2.3.1** *Icy Touch* \- Spread ice: Touch any surface to create a layer of slippery ice that spreads outward from the contact point. **Level** (frost spreads more quickly and extensively, ice becomes thicker and more persistent, cold intensifies). **Instant** (1 action): Create a small patch of ice around the touched area that provides immediate but temporary hazard. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ice formation that spreads 10 feet in all directions and persists throughout the encounter. Creatures moving across the ice must make balance checks or risk falling prone. The ice is thin enough that it won't support heavy objects but makes movement treacherous for enemies and allies alike.

**2.3.2** *Increase Gravity* \- Triple weight: Dramatically increase the pull of gravity in a localized area to hinder movement and flight. **Level** (gravitational field spreads naturally, weight effects become more pronounced, control precision improves). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief gravitational surge that can force flying creatures to land and make everyone stumble. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain tripled gravity in a 15-foot radius, consistently hampering movement and preventing flight. Affected creatures move at half speed and have disadvantage on jumping or climbing attempts. Flying creatures must land or risk falling as their wings cannot support the increased weight, and dropped objects deal double damage.

**2.3.3** *Lightning Strike* - Call electricity: Call down bolts of lightning from the sky or conjure them from your fingertips. **Level** (electrical potency intensifies, lightning becomes more precise, may arc to additional targets). **Instant** (1 action): Strike a single target within 100 feet with lightning that deals 1d6 electrical damage and jumps to one nearby creature for half damage. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously channel electrical energy, allowing multiple strikes or sustained electrical attacks on targets within range. Metal armor provides no protection and may conduct the electricity to other touching metal objects. The flash briefly illuminates the area and creates a sharp crack of thunder.

**2.3.4** *Liquid Air* \- Make air swimmable: Transform air into a thick, viscous substance that changes how creatures move through it. **Level** (atmospheric transformation deepens, viscosity control improves, affected area expands organically). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly thicken the air in a small area, slowing movement and creating unusual tactical conditions. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a 20-foot cube of liquid air that allows swimming through the atmosphere and dramatically alters battlefield mobility. Creatures can "swim" through the air at their normal swimming speed while those who cannot swim sink slowly downward. The air remains breathable but feels heavy and humid, creating unique tactical opportunities.

**2.3.5** *Mist Form* \- Become vapor: Transform your body into a cloud of mist to escape danger or infiltrate secure areas. **Level** (duration increases, can include allies, mist density and resistance improve). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly become incorporeal mist to avoid a single attack or squeeze through a small opening before reforming. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain vapor form for extended infiltration, allowing you to pass through small openings and under doors. You move at half speed but can squeeze through any gap larger than one inch. Physical attacks pass harmlessly through you, but you cannot manipulate objects or cast other spells while strong winds can affect your movement.

**2.3.6** *Mountain's Strength* \- Earth power: Draw strength from the earth beneath your feet to enhance your physical capabilities. **Level** (strength multiplier increases, duration extends, additional physical enhancements). **Instant** (1 action): Gain a surge of earth-born power for a single feat of strength, breaking objects or overpowering opponents. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain doubled physical power as long as you remain in contact with natural earth or stone. Your carrying capacity doubles and you have advantage on feats of strength like breaking down doors or lifting heavy objects. You can punch through thin stone walls or bend iron bars with effort, but the power fades if you lose contact with the earth.

**2.4.1** *Rain Make* \- Create downpour: Summon a localized rainstorm that follows your movements and provides tactical advantages. **Level** (storm coverage expands naturally, rainfall becomes heavier and more sustained, weather effects intensify). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief but heavy downpour that can extinguish fires or provide immediate water in a small area. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a persistent rainstorm in a 50-foot radius that moves with you and affects the battlefield. The rain is heavy enough to extinguish normal fires and provide fresh drinking water. Visibility is reduced to 20 feet within the storm and the ground becomes muddy and slippery, creating ongoing environmental hazards.

**2.4.2** *Sculpt Elements* \- Shape materials: Reshape natural elemental materials into useful or artistic forms through careful manipulation. **Level** (material responsiveness improves, shaping precision increases, can work with larger quantities and denser substances). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly reshape a small amount of natural material (stone, metal, wood, ice, or clay) into a simple form. **Active** (2+ actions): Work with up to 50 cubic feet of material, carefully sculpting complex shapes and forms with concentration and precision. The shaping creates permanent changes to the material's form but not its essential properties. You cannot create moving parts or complex mechanisms, but can fashion tools, weapons, or artistic sculptures from naturally occurring materials.

**2.4.3** *Steam Form* \- Become vapor: Transform into a cloud of hot steam to move through spaces and damage enemies. **Level** (vapor form becomes more stable and controllable, heat effects intensify, transformation duration improves). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly become scalding steam to escape danger or move through a small opening, dealing damage to creatures you pass through. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain steam form for extended mobility, allowing you to pass through small spaces and rise with air currents. The steam is hot enough to deal 1d6 damage to creatures you pass through but dissipates if exposed to cold or strong winds. You can see and hear normally but cannot manipulate objects or speak clearly, though water-based attacks can restore dispersed vapor.

**2.4.4** *Stone Skin* \- Harden flesh: Transform your skin to have the texture and durability of stone for enhanced protection. **Level** (damage reduction improves, duration extends, can protect allies). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly harden your skin to resist a single attack, reducing damage from physical sources. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain stone-like skin that provides ongoing resistance to physical damage, reducing all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage by half. Your skin takes on a granite-like appearance and feels cool to the touch. The transformation makes you heavier and slightly slower, but provides excellent protection against conventional weapons throughout the encounter.

**2.4.5** *Stone Tell* \- Read earth memories: Touch natural stone to learn about events that occurred in its presence. **Level** (memory depth increases, clarity improves, range of accessible events expands). **Instant** (1 action): Gain a brief glimpse of recent significant events that happened near the stone you're touching. **Active** (2+ actions): Delve deeper into the stone's memories, accessing a fuller history of major events like battles, constructions, or gatherings over extended periods. You experience these memories as hazy visions showing shapes and movements but not fine details or conversations. Ancient stones may provide glimpses of events from centuries past, though these visions are often fragmented and require careful interpretation.

**2.4.6** *Storm Call* \- Summon thunder: Create an intense thunderstorm that brings lightning, rain, and chaos to the battlefield. **Level** (radius increases by +15 feet per Level, lightning frequency and damage increase). **Instant** (1 action): Generate a brief but violent storm burst with lightning strikes and thunder that affects the immediate area. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a persistent thunderstorm in a 30-foot radius with ongoing lightning strikes and environmental effects. Lightning flashes every few seconds, providing intermittent bright illumination and dealing 1d6 damage to one random creature in the area each round. The constant thunder makes communication difficult and masks other sounds while rain extinguishes fires and creates treacherous footing.

**2.5.1** *Summon Cube* \- Control earth: Raise a cube of solid earth or stone from the ground to create tactical advantages. **Level** (earth responds more readily, cubes become denser and more durable, control precision improves). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly raise a temporary earth cube that provides immediate cover or creates an obstacle before sinking back down. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a 5-foot cube of earth within 30 feet that you can reposition by concentrating on it. The cube can serve as cover, a platform, or an obstacle depending on your needs and can move up to 10 feet per round. The cube has 3 Hearts worth of durability and crumbles to normal earth when destroyed or when you cease maintaining it.

**2.5.2** *Summon Idol* \- Create statue: Shape earth, stone, or clay into a human-sized statue that can perform simple tasks. **Level** (idol size and intelligence increase, can create multiple idols). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly form a crude statue that can perform one simple action like pointing or gesturing before becoming inert. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain an animated idol that can perform ongoing simple tasks like standing guard, pointing directions, or holding objects. The idol understands basic commands in your language and has minimal intelligence but cannot fight or cast spells. The statue moves slowly and deliberately, obviously artificial but impressive to observers who aren't familiar with magic.

**2.5.3** *Thunder Call* \- Create sonic boom: Generate a deafening thunderclap that damages enemies and creates widespread noise. **Level** (thunder becomes more resonant and powerful, sonic effects spread further, sound intensity deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Create a single massive thunderclap at a point within 60 feet that deals 1d6 sonic damage and deafens creatures within 20 feet. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ongoing thunder effects, allowing multiple thunderclaps or sustained sonic pressure in the targeted area. The sound is loud enough to trigger avalanches, break fragile objects, and wake sleeping creatures within a quarter mile. You can choose to be immune to the effect when casting, making it useful for signaling or environmental manipulation.

**2.5.4** *Tide Control* \- Command waters: Control the flow and level of natural bodies of water to create tactical advantages. **Level** (water responds more readily to your will, control range expands naturally, tidal effects become more pronounced). **Instant** (1 action): Cause a sudden change in water level or current that can affect boats, create waves, or expose hidden areas briefly. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain control over water within 100 feet, allowing you to raise or lower levels by up to 5 feet and redirect currents. You can change the direction of currents or create small waves on rivers, lakes, ponds, and coastal areas. The effect cannot create water from nothing, but sudden changes can strand boats or flood low areas as needed.

**2.5.5** *Upwell* \- Create spring: Cause fresh water to bubble up from the ground to provide clean drinking water. **Level** (spring flow becomes more reliable and abundant, water quality improves, flow sustainability increases). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief upwelling of fresh water that provides immediate hydration or can be collected quickly. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a steady spring that produces 10 gallons per minute, creating a reliable water source for the group. The water is clean and safe to drink, appearing even in areas where no groundwater naturally exists. The spring creates a small pool that provides enough water for drinking, cooking, and basic cleaning needs while you maintain it.

**2.5.6** *Water Breathing* \- Breathe liquid: Grant yourself and others the ability to breathe water as easily as air. **Level** (duration extends, can affect +2 additional creatures per Level). **Instant** (1 action): Provide brief underwater breathing capability for emergency situations or short dives. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain water breathing for yourself and up to three other creatures, allowing extended underwater exploration and activity. This allows underwater exploration and swimming without concern for drowning. The spell doesn't grant swimming abilities or protection from water pressure, only the ability to extract oxygen from water while still being able to breathe air normally.

**2.6.1** *Water Shape* \- Control liquid: Command water to take shapes that serve tactical or practical purposes. **Level** (water becomes more responsive and malleable, shape complexity improves, formed structures become more stable). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly reshape a small amount of water into a simple form like a barrier or tool that holds briefly before flowing away. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain precise control over up to 100 gallons of water, shaping it into complex forms like walls, bridges, or stairs. The water maintains its new form as if it were a solid object but remains liquid to the touch. You can create structures that support weight and block passage, with the shaped water returning to natural flow when you stop maintaining concentration.

**2.6.2** *Web Weave* \- Spin natural nets: Create barriers of fibrous vines or thick spider webs to hinder enemy movement. **Level** (web coverage spreads organically, strands become stronger and more adhesive, entanglement effects improve). **Instant** (1 action): Rapidly grow a small area of webs or vines that can entangle one or two creatures briefly. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain extensive webbing that fills a 20-foot square area with persistent entangling effects. Creatures moving through the webs are slowed to quarter speed and may become entangled if they fail to break free. The webs are sticky and strong enough to hold human-sized creatures but can be cut or burned away, providing ongoing battlefield control.

**2.6.3** *Whirlwind* \- Create vortex: Generate a spinning column of air that can move across the battlefield and affect multiple targets. **Level** (vortex becomes more powerful and focused, wind intensity increases, control precision improves). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief whirlwind that strikes one area, dealing damage and scattering objects before dissipating. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a controllable whirlwind 10 feet wide and 30 feet tall that moves at your direction up to 30 feet per round. The whirlwind can pick up small objects, dust, and debris while creatures caught in the vortex take 1d6 damage and are lifted 10 feet before being deposited roughly. The wind is strong enough to extinguish fires and scatter loose papers.

**2.6.4** *Wind Shield* \- Air protection: Surround yourself with a swirling barrier of protective wind that deflects attacks and provides environmental benefits. **Level** (protection improves, can shield allies, wind strength increases). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief wind barrier that can deflect a single ranged attack or blow away small threats. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a constantly swirling wind shield that provides ongoing protection from projectiles and environmental hazards. The wind deflects arrows, thrown weapons, and other projectiles, providing partial cover against ranged attacks. Small flying creatures like insects are blown away, the constant air movement keeps you cool, and your scent is carried away for stealth.

**2.6.5** *Wind Walk* \- Float on breeze: Step onto currents of air and walk through the sky using wind magic. **Level** (altitude increases, speed improves, can carry allies). **Instant** (1 action): Take a brief aerial step or leap using wind currents to cross gaps or reach elevated positions. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the ability to walk on air currents at normal walking speed, ascending or descending gradually as needed. You cannot hover in one place without forward movement, and strong winds can affect your direction. You remain about 10-20 feet above the ground and must land gently when you cease maintaining the effect.

**2.6.6** *Zephyr* \- Ride the winds: Transform into a gust of wind for rapid movement and escape from danger. **Level** (speed multiplier increases, duration extends, can carry objects or allies). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly become wind to move quickly across short distances or escape immediate threats. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain wind form for extended travel, moving at ten times your normal speed and passing through openings larger than a few inches. Physical attacks cannot harm you in this form, but you cannot manipulate objects or cast other spells. You can reform when desired or when you stop maintaining the effect, appearing wherever the wind carries you.
