# Wåndyr Version History

## Changes: v0.5 to v0.7

### Sweet and Spicy: clarified intent that this is about "style, not substance"

**Old:** If any die rolls 6 the answer is more "sweet", and if any die rolls 1 the answer is more "spicy". Never remove a 1 or 6 from the roll.

*Example:* A character is bow-sniping a sentry and takes time to set up the shot, making it easy (6). If it ends up "spicy" another guard might notice, or if "sweet" the guard uniform is spotless.

**New:** When any die rolls a 6, the outcome is "Sweet," adding a stylistic flair. Double 6s indicate a bonus or benefit. Conversely, if any die rolls a 1, the outcome is "Spicy," also adding style. Double 1s signify a penalty or complication.

*Example:* Bowman snipes a sentry. A "spicy" result might mean another guard notices, while a "sweet" result could mean the guard's uniform is spotless.

The Guide can either describe these effects or allow the player to provide their own interpretation. Unless the side effect is straightforward and very minor, ask the Oracle if it happens. Players must spend a Heart to request a heroic stunt or a magical effect.

**Rationale:** The original examples were about a guard sniped with "sweet" leaving a spotless uniform. This is "style"...there isn't extra damage or other guards dying as collateral. However, players have had a ton of fun with sweet and spicy, sometimes getting a bit carried away (e.g. asking "does the arrow also kill the other guards?"). The new wording attempts to create space for this (since it's fun), but also creates room for an appropriate cost.

### Reframe Melee Combat broad penalty as a more specific bonus

**Old:** A character is "in Melee" if they are within reach (~10 feet) of an enemy who is making a melee attack. The character has Disadvantage on all rolls unless they attempt an attack with a close combat weapon (e.g. dagger, sword, spear, pistol).

**New:** When two opponents attempt melee attacks against each other, reduce hits to each other by 1 Heart.

**Rationale:** This preserves the intention that characters in melee should do melee, not other kinds of actions, but reframes it as a bonus rather than a severe un-fun penalty. This also gives players a tactical option to reduce from one foe.

### Initiative: minor edits to clarify intent

## Changes: v0.2 to v0.5

- Assets renamed to Skills (to avoid confusion)
- Magic Trait costs a Skill
- Party Level system added: boosts Hearts, affects Oracle
- Magic expanded: sentient items, Arcana usage rules, magic components
- Named items can be used once per Muster for free
- Movement rate quantified; accessing Baggage takes time
- Songs grant morale Hearts (max 2 per character)

## Changes: v0.1 to v0.2

- Magic system added: spell schools, spellbooks, Arcana
- Magic-users defined by Traits; spells use Hearts
- Names mechanic introduced: earn XP to name deeds, songs, items
- Group music and comforts improve camp healing (up to 2d6 + 4 Hearts)
- Turn Clock introduced to track time and spell durations
