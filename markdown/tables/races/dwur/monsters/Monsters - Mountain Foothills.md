# Random Encounters - Mountain Foothills

*Roll 1d20. Check every 4 hours travel, encounter on 6.*

1. **Brown Bears** (1d2) - HD 4, AC 6; protecting territory, fishing streams
2. **Wolf Pack** (2d4) - HD 2+2, AC 7; coordinated hunters, howl warnings
3. **Owlbear** (1) - HD 5, AC 5; territorial, unpredictable mood
4. **Giant Eagles** (1d2) - HD 4, AC 7; soar overhead, protect nesting grounds
5. **Mountain Lions** (1d3) - HD 3+2, AC 6; stalk party, attack weakest member
6. **Dwur Patrol** (1d4+2) - HD 1+1, AC 4; armed scouts, may be friendly
7. **Bandit Hunters** (1d6) - HD 1, AC 7; human/halfling trappers and guides
8. **Stone Giant** (1) - HD 9, AC 4; ancient guardian, throws boulders
9. **Griffon** (1) - HD 7, AC 5; hunts in high peaks, fierce predator
10. **Hill Giant** (1) - HD 8, AC 4; crude and aggressive, seeks easy prey
11. **Bulezau Pack** (1d3) - HD 3, AC 6; demonic creatures drawn to conflict
12. **Treant** (1) - HD 8, AC 2; ancient forest guardian, suspicious
13. **Mountain Goats** (2d4) - HD 2, AC 7; sure-footed, flee from danger
14. **Giant Spiders** (1d3) - HD 3, AC 6; web-spinners, ambush predators
15. **Mountain Troll** (1) - HD 6, AC 4; regenerates, fears fire
16. **Giant Vultures** (1d4) - HD 3, AC 6; carrion eaters, circle overhead
17. **Mountain Dwarves** (1d6) - HD 1+1, AC 4; prospectors, may trade
18. **Giant Boars** (1d3) - HD 4, AC 5; aggressive, charge when threatened
19. **Mountain Nymph** (1) - HD 3, AC 5; nature spirit, tests travelers
20. **Giant Badgers** (1d2) - HD 3, AC 6; burrowing creatures, territorial

## Weather Events (Roll when encounter = 1-3)

- **Mountain Storm** (40%) - Heavy rain/snow, visibility limited
- **Fog Bank** (30%) - Heavy concealment, easy to get lost
- **Sudden Blizzard** (20%) - Extreme cold, seek shelter immediately
- **Clear Weather** (10%) - Perfect visibility, +2 to spot distant features
