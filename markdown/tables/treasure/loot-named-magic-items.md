# Named Magic Items

1. *Dawnbreaker* - An elven sword that glows white in the presence of evil creatures  
2. *Nightcleaver* - A companion blade with similar properties  
3. *Whisper* - A clever little blade that hums when danger approaches  
4. *The Ring of Shadows* - A simple iron band that renders its wearer unseen but draws dark forces  
5. *The Heart of Winter* - A white gem that glows with inner frost and inspires loyalty  
6. *The Cloak of Dusk* - A gray-green cloak that shifts colors with its surroundings  
7. *The Boots of Featherfall* - Leather boots that leave no tracks and silence footsteps  
8. *The Star-Map* - A map with silver writing visible only under starlight  
9. *The Crown Key* - An ancient key that unlocks doors only at specific hours  
10. *The Raven's Circlet* - A silver band allowing the wearer to speak with corvids  
11. *The Stone of Lost Tales* - A river-smooth stone that reveals forgotten stories  
12. *The Wayfinder's Staff* - A blackthorn staff that warms when pointed toward shelter  
13. *The Mistwalker's Ring* - A ring that helps its wearer walk safely through fog and water  
14. *The Sky-friend's Token* - A silver feather that can call for aid from above  
15. *The Shadow's Heart* - A dark pearl that helps its bearer move unseen  
16. *The Songbird's Chain* - A necklace granting understanding of bird speech  
17. *The Wyrm's Doom* - A black arrow destined to find a dragon's heart  
18. *The Starweave Coat* - Armor light as silk but stronger than steel  
19. *The Watchful Purse* - A leather purse that shrieks when touched by thieves  
20. *The Ring of Wild Shape* - A bronze ring bearing the mark of a great beast
