# Weapon and Armor Quality

Roll 2d6 for the quality level of weapons and armor:

**1.1** Poor Quality - Damaged, rusted, or poorly made (-1 to effectiveness)  
**1.2** Common Quality - Standard craftsmanship, no bonus  
**1.3** Good Quality - Well-made, slightly better than standard (+1)  
**1.4** Fine Quality - Excellent craftsmanship, noticeable improvement (+1)  
**1.5** Superior Quality - Masterwork, significantly better (+2)  
**1.6** Exceptional Quality - Rare masterpiece, exceptional performance (+2)

**2.1** Worn - Used but functional, slight penalty (-1)  
**2.2** Standard - Average quality, no bonus or penalty  
**2.3** Sturdy - Well-constructed, reliable (+1)  
**2.4** Balanced - Perfectly weighted and balanced (+1)  
**2.5** Masterwork - Expert craftsmanship (+2)  
**2.6** Legendary - One-of-a-kind quality (+2)

**3.1** Damaged - Cracked, bent, or broken (-1)  
**3.2** Basic - Simple but functional, no bonus  
**3.3** Refined - Carefully crafted, minor improvement (+1)  
**3.4** Tempered - Specially treated for better performance (+1)  
**3.5** Artisan - Skilled craftsperson's work (+2)  
**3.6** Pristine - Perfect condition, flawless work (+2)

**4.1** Inferior - Substandard materials or workmanship (-1)  
**4.2** Regular - Normal quality, standard performance  
**4.3** Enhanced - Improved design or materials (+1)  
**4.4** Fortified - Strengthened for durability (+1)  
**4.5** Elite - High-end craftsmanship (+2)  
**4.6** Perfected - Ultimate expression of the craft (+2)

**5.1** Defective - Flawed construction (-1)  
**5.2** Typical - Common quality, expected performance  
**5.3** Improved - Better than average (+1)  
**5.4** Reinforced - Additional strength or protection (+1)  
**5.5** Premium - Top-tier quality (+2)  
**5.6** Immaculate - Perfect in every detail (+2)

**6.1** Broken - Severely damaged, barely functional (-1)  
**6.2** Standard - Normal quality, no special features  
**6.3** Upgraded - Enhanced performance (+1)  
**6.4** Optimized - Perfectly suited for purpose (+1)  
**6.5** Exceptional - Outstanding quality (+2)  
**6.6** Transcendent - Beyond normal limits (+2)

## Quality Guidelines

### Weapons

- **Daggers/Small Weapons**: Rarely above +1 quality
- **Swords/Medium Weapons**: Common +1, rare +2
- **Polearms/Heavy Weapons**: Often +1, sometimes +2
- **Ranged Weapons**: +1 common, +2 rare
- **Exotic Weapons**: Variable quality based on complexity

### Armor

- **Light Armor (Leather)**: Rarely above +1 quality
- **Medium Armor (Chain)**: Common +1, rare +2
- **Heavy Armor (Plate)**: Often +1, sometimes +2
- **Shields**: +1 common, +2 rare
- **Specialized Armor**: Quality depends on purpose

### Quality Effects

- **-1**: Reduced effectiveness, penalties to use
- **+0**: Standard performance, no bonus
- **+1**: Improved effectiveness, minor bonuses
- **+2**: Significant improvement, notable advantages

### Cost Modifiers

- **-1 Quality**: 50% of base cost
- **+0 Quality**: Base cost
- **+1 Quality**: +100 gp to base cost
- **+2 Quality**: +1,000 gp to base cost
