# Relics - The First Being

## The Entity Behind the Gold

*The otherworldly being whose essence became the First Light*

### **Thurkaz, the Silent Smith** (Dwur Voiceless Keeper)

- **Domain:** Forbidden memories, dangerous secrets, protective binding

## The Heart of First Light (Legendary Relic)

*The original core of the Star Dreamer's being*

**Form:** Basketball-sized sphere of pure Star Gold
**Location:** Heart of the Stellar Forge chamber
**Power:** Complete access to universal memory - every thought, every experience, every identity that ever existed
**Corruption:** Instant total consciousness dissolution - the wielder becomes everyone and therefore no one

## Protection From Relic Corruption

*Methods to resist the Star Dreamer's influence*

- **Iron Binding:** Wrap relics in cold iron (reduces effect by half)
- **Salt Circles:** Contained within pure salt (blocks minor effects)
- **Running Water:** Constant contact weakens connection
- **Concentrated Will:** Strong sense of self provides resistance
- **Memory Anchors:** Physical reminders of current identity
- **Group Support:** Companions remind you who you are now

## Signs of Star Dreamer Awakening

*What happens if too many relics gather in one place*

1. **Memory Storms** - Random experiences flood area
