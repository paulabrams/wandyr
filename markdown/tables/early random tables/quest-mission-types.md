# Mission Types

Roll 2d6, first die is row (1-6), second die is column (1-6)

**1.1** Escort valuable cargo through dangerous territory **1.2** Investigate mysterious disappearances in village **1.3** Retrieve stolen artifact from thieves' den **1.4** Deliver urgent message to distant ally **1.5** Rescue kidnapped noble from bandits **1.6** Eliminate monster threatening trade routes

**2.1** Explore newly discovered ancient ruins **2.2** Map uncharted wilderness for cartographer **2.3** Establish trade relations with distant people **2.4** Find cure for mysterious plague **2.5** Locate source of corrupting magical influence **2.6** Discover fate of missing expedition

**3.1** Mediate dispute between feuding families **3.2** Gather evidence of noble's corruption **3.3** Infiltrate criminal organization **3.4** Protect witness from assassination attempts **3.5** Expose false prophet deceiving followers **3.6** Prevent war between neighboring kingdoms

**4.1** Recover family heirloom lost to gambling **4.2** Clear family name of false accusations **4.3** Find missing heir to noble house **4.4** Avenge murdered mentor or teacher **4.5** Break curse affecting loved one **4.6** Restore honor lost through cowardice

**5.1** Prevent prophesied catastrophe from occurring **5.2** Gather components for powerful ritual **5.3** Seal dimensional rift before demons emerge **5.4** Defeat ancient evil awakening from slumber **5.5** Restore balance to disrupted magical forces **5.6** Fulfill divine mandate from chosen deity

**6.1** Establish new settlement in wilderness **6.2** Build alliance between diverse peoples **6.3** Create trade network spanning continents **6.4** Found school for magical education **6.5** Establish temple in unconsecrated land **6.6** Unite scattered tribes under single banner
