# Wåndyr Update Briefing: v0.4 → v1.1

## Major System Overhauls

### 1. **Character Creation - Simplified**

- **OLD**: Magic school costs a Skill; non-human costs a Skill; Traits/Skills have Levels (0+)
- **NEW**:
  - Magic school is just a Skill (no cost)
  - Non-human is just a Trait (no cost)
  - No individual Trait/Skill Levels - replaced by Alignment system
- *Why*: Simpler character creation, less bookkeeping

### 2. **Experience & Advancement - Complete Redesign**

- **OLD**:
  - Variable XP costs (3rd Trait = 3 XP, 5th Skill = 5 XP)
  - Individual Trait/Skill Levels (1d6+Level to advance)
  - Names must be earned, then cost XP equal to count
- **NEW**:
  - **Flat 3 XP** for all advancements (Traits, Skills, Bynames, Spells, Arcana, Relics, Knacks, Songs, Recipes)
  - No individual Levels - replaced by Alignment
  - Bynames can be earned or given
- *Why*: Much simpler, easier to remember, encourages diverse advancement

### 3. **Level System → Alignment System**

- **OLD**:
  - Party Level (starts at 0, costs 2,000gp+ to advance)
  - Individual Trait/Skill Levels (0+)
  - Party Level adds Hearts at Muster from home base
- **NEW**:
  - **Alignment** replaces all Level systems
  - Alignment = (relevant Advancements ÷ 3, rounded down)
  - Used for magic schools, fighting styles, factions, otherworldly beings
  - Determines maximum spell level, Knack usage, etc.
  - Party Leader chooses Alignment at Muster for extra Hearts
- *Why*: More flexible, character-specific, no gold cost

### 4. **Hearts System - Simplified**

- **OLD**: 2d6 Hearts (same as v1.1, but Party Level could add more from home base)
- **NEW**: Always **2d6 Hearts** at Muster (no Party Level bonus)
- *Why*: Consistent starting Hearts, simpler

### 5. **Magic System - Spell Levels & Control**

- **OLD**:
  - Casting cost = Spell Level in Hearts
  - Magic-users understand magic up to their Level
  - Meddling beyond level = additional risks (d6 Hearts)
  - Magic Components offset cost by 1 Heart per slot
- **NEW**:
  - **Spell Level** set by Guide based on what you're trying to do
  - Pay Level in Hearts (use Arcana Hearts first)
  - Maximum Level = Alignment with magic school
  - Roll Oracle for **control** (not success/failure)
  - No Magic Components mentioned
- **Arcana Hearts**: NEW! Gain 1 per Arcana item (up to Alignment), used for casting and against hostile magic
- *Why*: More structured, clearer limits, better risk/reward

### 6. **Spell Duration - Removed**

- **OLD**: Instant or Active (persists until Turn Clock runs out, can extend for 1 Heart)
- **NEW**: No duration system - effects are narrative
- *Why*: Simpler, less bookkeeping

### 7. **Spellbooks - Simplified**

- **OLD**:
  - 1st level = 1 slot, 1 spell
  - 2nd level = 2 slots, 1 2nd + 2 1st level spells
  - 3rd level = 3 slots, 1 3rd + 2 2nd level spells
- **NEW**: Most spellbooks have a single spell (no mention of multi-level books)
- *Why*: Simpler, more focused

### 8. **Spell Scrolls - Removed**

- **OLD**: 5 per slot, crumble when used
- **NEW**: Not mentioned
- *Why*: Simplified magic items

### 9. **Arcana - Redesigned**

- **OLD**:
  - Enchanting costs XP (spell level, increases if total exceeds item slots)
  - Words move to Arcana, leaving spell fragment
  - Can cast from Arcana if equipped and used visibly
  - Weapon Arcana can cast on hit instead of damage
- **NEW**:
  - Transfer known spell to Arcana for 3 XP
  - Words vanish from book, appear on item
  - Grants +1 to caster level when casting those spells
  - Arcana Hearts system (see above)
  - No mention of weapon Arcana casting on hit
- *Why*: Clearer process, more valuable, better integration

### 10. **New Magic School: Canting**

- **NEW**: 7th magic school - Thieves' Cant magic
- 36 spells focused on deception, manipulation, thievery
- Examples: *Mark the Gull*, *Budge*, *Darkmans Cloak*, *Prince of Thieves*
- *Why*: Expands magic options for roguish characters

### 11. **Morale System - Completely New**

- **OLD**:
  - Comforts: +1 or +2 Hearts during Muster (10gp or 100gp)
  - Music/Songs: +1 Heart for Name, +1 for Skill during Muster
- **NEW**: Comprehensive morale system with four pillars:
  - **Leadership**: Party Leader gains extra Hearts = Alignment Level, can absorb damage for companions
  - **Hospitality**: Share provisions (1 Heart/hour), Comforts (+1 or +2 Hearts), Host role
  - **Music**: Performance (1 Heart/hour) + Bard role (Action to restore 1 Heart)
  - **Animal Companions**: NPC Action to restore 1 Heart per Muster
- *Why*: Emphasizes journey and companionship themes

### 12. **Combat - Rounds vs Turns**

- **OLD**: Everything in "Turns" (variable time)
- **NEW**:
  - **Rounds** (10 seconds) for combat
  - **Turns** (10 minutes) for exploration
  - Turn Clock paused during combat
- *Why*: Better distinction between fast-paced action and exploration

### 13. **Combat - "At the Same Time"**

- **NEW**: If targeted, you can act simultaneously
  - "Right of way" determines order
  - Weapon attacks > everything else
  - Melee > missiles
  - Reach matters at distance, smaller weapon in close quarters
- *Why*: More dynamic combat

### 14. **Death System - Redesigned**

- **OLD**:
  - 0 Hearts = injured (recover in Camp)
  - Below 0 = critical (needs magic healing, dies when Turn Clock clears)
- **NEW**: **Offscreen vs Onscreen death**
  - **Offscreen**: Cut away immediately, uncertain fate, can return via Invocation (unless Double Spicy)
  - **Onscreen**: One final heroic action (requires Sweet), then Offscreen
- *Why*: More narrative control, less mechanical death

### 15. **Saving Throws - Removed**

- **OLD**: Oracle roll to avoid "worst half" of harmful effects
- **NEW**: Not mentioned (likely handled by Oracle rolls)
- *Why*: Simpler, less special cases

### 16. **Melee Rules - Simplified**

- **OLD**: Within 1" (10 feet) = "in Melee", Disadvantage on ALL rolls if not using close combat weapon
- **NEW**: Reduce melee damage by 1 Heart if you melee attack or parry that foe this round
- *Why*: Simpler, less punitive

### 17. **Invocations - Streamlined**

- **OLD**:
  - Lose d6 Hearts (half on Sweet, double on Spicy)
  - Must offer 1-6 XP to Name, ask Oracle how spent
- **NEW**:
  - Lose d6 Hearts first
  - Roll Oracle for response (Sweet = likes, Spicy = dislikes)
  - Guide rolls on secret Trait table for the being
  - If you already have the Trait = harmony (no cost)
  - Otherwise, being spends 3 XP on that Trait
- *Why*: Clearer process, more predictable costs

### 18. **Relics - Enhanced**

- **OLD**: Conduits for alignment, can make Invocations on behalf of possessors
- **NEW**:
  - "Ark" mechanic: +1 Alignment for similar Invocations
  - "Saint" mechanic: Can bless companions at Muster (1 extra Heart each)
  - Blessing tied to rumors the being likes
- *Why*: Makes Relics more meaningful, encourages party cooperation

### 19. **Artifacts → Sentient Items**

- **OLD**: "Artifacts" with True Names, bound souls
- **NEW**: "Sentient Items" - same concept, clearer terminology
  - Alignment growth when carrying
  - Should take Traits reflecting attachment
- *Why*: Clearer terminology

### 20. **Insight System - Updated**

- **OLD**: 6 options (TRAIT, ITEM, SKILL, NAME, ASSIST, EFFORT)
- **NEW**: 6 options with changes:
  - NAME → **BYNAME** (titles, epithets, named items)
  - ASSIST → **FEAT** (you've done this before)
  - Still has: TRAIT, SKILL, ITEM, EFFORT
- *Why*: Better reflects new advancement system

### 21. **Sweet & Spicy - Enhanced**

- **OLD**:
  - 6 = Sweet, 1 = Spicy
  - Player asks question on Sweet, Guide on Spicy
- **NEW**:
  - 6 = Sweet (good surprises), 1 = Spicy (not-so-good surprises)
  - Interpret yourself or ask Oracle
  - **Double Sweet/Spicy**: Ask Oracle a question (hopeful on Sweet, dire on Spicy)
  - Double Sweet can lead to learning a **Knack** (3 XP)
- *Why*: More player agency, rewards exceptional moments

### 22. **Attempting the Extraordinary**

- **NEW**: System for heroic feats, spells, invocations
  - Guide assigns Level based on what you're trying to do
  - Pay Level in Hearts
  - Maximum Level = relevant Alignment
  - Roll Oracle for success
  - "Extraordinary Alignment": Guide may temporarily improve Alignment by +1
- *Why*: Unified system for extraordinary actions

### 23. **Rumors & Locations Tables**

- **NEW**: Full d66 tables for rumors and locations
- Rumor updates: Guide combines results as rumors evolve
- Location updates: Can combine results when visiting
- *Why*: More tools for emergent play

### 24. **Equipment Pricing - Detailed**

- **NEW**: Clear pricing guide
  - Common: 1gp/slot
  - Armaments: 5gp/slot
  - Niche: 10gp/slot
  - Masterwork: +100gp (+1), +1,000gp (+2)
  - Elevated: +10gp (good), +100gp (rich), +1,000gp (noble)
- *Why*: Easier for Guide to price items

### 25. **Baggage - Clarified**

- **OLD**: Suffers in dice rolls (via Insight), Guide worries about safety
- **NEW**:
  - Dropping Baggage = 1 Round (10 sec)
  - Accessing items = 1 Turn (10 min)
  - Also counts if hands occupied with non-item-slot items
  - Movement: 40 feet (20 feet with Baggage)
- *Why*: Clearer trade-offs, specific timing

### 26. **Move - Simplified**

- **OLD**: 12" Move (1" = 10 feet tactical, 1" = 1 mile road), cut in half per impediment
- **NEW**: 40 feet (20 feet with Baggage), cut in half per impediment
- *Why*: Simpler, consistent units

### 27. **Item Slots - Expanded Examples**

- **OLD**: Basic examples (weapons, bows, shields, armor)
- **NEW**: Comprehensive examples including:
  - Clothing, Light, Musical Instruments, Hospitality, Animals
  - More detailed slot costs
- *Why*: Better guidance for players

### 28. **Camp vs Home Base**

- **OLD**: "Camping" ends session, return to "home base"
- **NEW**:
  - "Camp" = temporary or return to prior camp
  - "Home Base" = permanent camp (cave, tower, tavern, etc.)
  - Fast-travel between camps
  - Can leave treasure behind for pickup
- *Why*: Clearer distinction, more options

### 29. **Party Leader - New Role**

- **NEW**: Elect a Party Leader at Muster
  - Chooses one Alignment, gains extra Hearts = that Level
  - Can spend Hearts to absorb damage for companions
  - Cannot pay for spells or invocations
- *Why*: Encourages leadership, party support

### 30. **Monsters - Alignment-Based**

- **OLD**: Compared to Party Level
- **NEW**: Compared to **Alignment Level** (use Party Leader's when working together)
- *Why*: Consistent with new Alignment system

### 31. **Spell Lists - Numbering Change**

- **OLD**: 1.1.1, 1.1.2 format
- **NEW**: 1.1, 1.2 format (simpler)
- *Why*: Cleaner presentation

### 32. **Magic School Names**

- **OLD**: Elementalis, Umbrakala
- **NEW**: Elementale, Umbrakallo
- *Why*: Consistent naming

### 33. **Character Examples - Updated**

- **OLD**: Some examples have magic schools as Traits with quotes
- **NEW**: Magic schools as Skills, cleaner format
- *Why*: Consistent with new rules

### 34. **Turn Clock - Enhanced**

- **OLD**: Advance by 1 at start of Turn or time-consuming actions, roll Insight, clear at 6
- **NEW**:
  - Guide tosses die into bowl each time you do something time-consuming
  - When bowl full (6 dice), hour passed
  - Empty and roll dice - 1's indicate spicy complications
  - Also roll when doing something reckless (but put dice back)
- *Why*: More visual, clearer timing

### 35. **Area of Effect**

- **NEW**: Rules for area attacks
  - Each target rolls defense
  - Each enemy rolls when you attack with area effect
- *Why*: Handles dragonbreath, fireballs, etc.

### 36. **Knacks**

- **NEW**: Signature moves learned through Double Sweet
  - Cost 3 XP
  - Future use limited by Alignment
  - May need extraordinary circumstances to repeat
- *Why*: Rewards exceptional moments, creates signature abilities

### 37. **Songs and Recipes**

- **NEW**: Explicit advancement options
  - Songs: music that stays with you
  - Recipes: dishes that taste like home
  - Each costs 3 XP
  - Limited Hearts per Muster (Item Slots + Songs/Recipes)
- *Why*: Encourages roleplay, supports morale theme

### 38. **Bynames - Expanded**

- **OLD**: Personal bynames, titles, epithets, story/song names
- **NEW**:
  - Can name items after significant deeds (3 XP)
  - Can be given byname (title, epithet, honorific)
  - Usually assigned rather than chosen
  - Also possible for animal companions
- *Why*: More options, encourages storytelling

### 39. **Learning Spells**

- **OLD**: Cast with Sweet, pay 1 XP, gain as Name, no longer need book
- **NEW**: Cast with Sweet, eligible to learn for 3 XP, can cast without book (known spell)
- *Why*: Consistent with flat XP costs

### 40. **Magic Items - Clarified**

- **OLD**: Magic "plus" items, Power Words (Arcana), Artifacts
- **NEW**:
  - Magic items have magical features (discoverable through use)
  - Masterwork items (+1, +2)
  - Elevated items (good, rich, noble)
  - Arcana vs Magic Items distinction clarified
- *Why*: Clearer categorization

## What Stays the Same

- Core Oracle mechanics (2d6, 8+ = Yes)
- Sweet & Spicy system (enhanced but same core)
- Character creation basics (2 Traits, 2 Skills)
- 10 Item Slots
- Magic school structure (6 schools + new Canting)
- Spell lists (same spells, different numbering)
- Basic combat flow (attack, defend)
- Turn Clock concept (enhanced)
- Exploration focus
- Sandbox play style

## Migration Notes

- **Existing characters**:
  - Convert Trait/Skill Levels to Alignments
  - Magic school Trait → Magic school Skill
  - Recalculate XP costs (everything now 3 XP)
- **Existing spells**: Still work the same, but now use Spell Level system
- **Existing Arcana**: Now grant Arcana Hearts, use new enchanting rules
- **Party Level**: Convert to relevant Alignments
- **Magic Components**: No longer mentioned - may need to adapt or remove
- **Spell Scrolls**: No longer exist - convert to spellbooks if needed
- **Individual Levels**: All replaced by Alignment system

## Summary

v1.1 represents a major simplification and streamlining of the game:

- **Removed**: Individual Trait/Skill Levels, Party Level costs, Magic Components, Spell Scrolls, Saving Throws, complex spellbook levels
- **Added**: Alignment system, Morale system, Arcana Hearts, Canting school, Rumors/Locations tables, Equipment pricing, Knacks, Songs/Recipes
- **Simplified**: XP costs (flat 3 XP), Magic system (Spell Levels), Combat (Rounds vs Turns), Death (Offscreen/Onscreen)
- **Enhanced**: Sweet & Spicy, Insight, Invocations, Relics, Turn Clock

The game is now more focused on character growth through Alignment, party cooperation through Morale, and narrative control through simplified mechanics.
