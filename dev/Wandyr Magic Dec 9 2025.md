# Wåndyr Magic Rules

## Magic Casting

1. Declare spell name and intended effect
2. Guide assigns spell level (1-8, based on OSR equivalents)
3. Pay Hearts equal to spell level (Arcana Hearts first, then your own)
4. Ask the Oracle: "Do I control the magic?"
5. YES: Works as intended. NO: Does not work as intended (see Oracle below)

## Oracle

Ask "Do I control the magic?". On YES, you control the spell and it works as intended. On NO, you do not control the spell and something else happens. This can include complications, backlash, or collateral damage.

## Caster Level

Your "spell alignment" or "caster level" is your Alignment with the magic spell.

Casting a spell below your caster level is safer than a spell at your caster level. Attempting to cast a spell above your caster level is fraught with peril.

## Alignment

Count all Advancements (Traits, Skills, Names, etc.) relevant to the spell and divide by 3 (round down).

For example, if casting "peace aura" and you had a trait "peaceful", a skill "Vitae", and the spell "peace aura" itself, you would have 3 advancements, which puts you at caster level 1.

Generally for most spells you can simply determine your Alignment with the magic school. However, in some cases you might find you have some advancements that are relevant to the spell that cross magic schools. This allows you to take on the role of a "specialist" caster, e.g. a pyromancer will surely have some Elementale spells but might also find a fire spell in Vitae that is also relevant.

## Spellbooks

Spellbooks contain spells, and allow you to cast them even if you have not "learned" them yet. Spells in spellbooks have a fixed spell level, and this is the minimum spell level to cast them from the book.

## Learning Spells

Learning a spell costs 3 XP. You must have cast the spell with Sweet success at least once. Afterwards you do not need the spellbook to cast the spell, and are free to cast the spell at any spell level up and including to your caster level.

## Arcana

Arcana are magic items worn or held openly and ostentatiously—obvious wizard bling. Hidden Arcana do not function.

Arcana provide Arcana Hearts (1 per item slot) for casting spells or absorbing damage from hostile magic. Arcana Hearts replenish at Muster (must be equipped).

## Enchanting Arcana

You can transfer a Known Spell onto an Arcana by paying 3 XP. The spell's power words noticeably shape the item's appearance/bling. Your caster level is treated as 1 higher when casting that spell from the Arcana. This allows you to reach into the next spell level.
