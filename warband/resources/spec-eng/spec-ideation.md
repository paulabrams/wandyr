# Spec Starter

Your ideas are important. Always write this by hand before you bring in an AI. It's best to use pencil and paper to force a clean separation.

## Objective

What am I actually trying to accomplish? The outcome, not the task. One sentence.

## Why Now

Why does this matter? What happens if this ships? What if it doesn't? How does it connect to your principles and goals?

## Priority Queue

What are the pieces? Components, subtasks, dependencies.

What does "done" look like? The finished output. Specific.

## Constraint Architecture + Failure Modes

What does "wrong" look like? The subtle failure modes.

## Risks and Open Questions

What's the hard part? Where am I least certain?

## Additional Context

What do I already know that I haven't written down? Institutional knowledge, unwritten rules.
