## Rumor Reliability
- **True** (40%) - Accurate information, can be trusted
- **Partial** (35%) - Contains truth but incomplete/distorted  
- **Unknown** (15%) - Uncertain, may or may not be true
- **False** (10%) - Completely wrong, based on misunderstanding
