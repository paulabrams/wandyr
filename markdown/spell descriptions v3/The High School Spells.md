# The High School Spells

*Nobility, wisdom, and divine authority. The ancient magic of the High Elves and White Wizards, written in celestial silver script that glows with inner light. These spells channel the power of righteousness, embody the wisdom of ages, and manifest the authority of lawful good.*


**5.1.1** *Authority* - Project aura of natural authority that compels recognition. **Instant** Create a brief moment of commanding presence that forces one creature within 30 feet to acknowledge your authority for their next action. **Active** Maintain a sustained aura of leadership throughout the encounter, making it difficult for others to oppose your reasonable requests or ignore your guidance. The effect works best on those who respect legitimate authority and lawful order. **Level** Additional creatures affected.

**5.1.2** *Blessing* - Call upon higher powers to grant divine favor. **Instant** Grant advantage on one immediate action as divine light briefly surrounds yourself or an ally within 30 feet. **Active** Maintain continuous divine favor throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all actions and resistance to fear and despair. The blessing manifests as a subtle golden glow and a sense of divine purpose. **Level** Additional targets blessed.

**5.1.3** *Celestial Light* - Create light that banishes darkness and reveals truth. **Instant** Create a burst of pure white light that illuminates a 30-foot radius and dispels magical darkness. **Active** Maintain a sustained beacon of celestial light that not only illuminates but also reveals hidden creatures and objects, making illusions and disguises less effective. The light feels warm and comforting to good creatures but uncomfortable to evil ones. **Level** Light intensity and range increase.

**5.1.4** *Command* - Speak with voice of authority to compel obedience. **Instant** Issue a single, simple command that one creature within 30 feet must obey if they can hear and understand you. **Active** Maintain the power of command throughout the encounter, allowing you to issue reasonable orders that lawful creatures find difficult to resist. The command must be something the target could reasonably do and not obviously harmful to them. **Level** Additional targets affected.

**5.1.5** *Divine Protection* - Create protective barrier of divine energy. **Instant** Absorb one incoming attack or harmful effect with a flash of golden light on yourself or an ally. **Active** Maintain a sustained protective aura that reduces damage from evil creatures and provides resistance to harmful magic throughout the encounter. The protection is strongest against undead, demons, and other evil beings. **Level** Additional creatures protected.

**5.1.6** *Holy Presence* - Project aura of divine authority and moral certainty. **Instant** Create a brief surge of holy energy that causes evil creatures within 20 feet to flinch or hesitate. **Active** Maintain a sustained holy presence that makes evil creatures uncomfortable and less effective while bolstering the courage and resolve of good creatures. The aura manifests as a subtle golden glow and a sense of divine purpose. **Level** Holy presence becomes more intense and far-reaching.

**5.2.1** *Judgment* - Divine insight: Gain supernatural insight into the moral character and intentions of others. **Instant** Receive a brief flash of insight about one creature's true nature and current intentions within 30 feet. **Active** Maintain continuous moral perception throughout the encounter, allowing you to sense deception, hidden evil, or noble intentions in those around you. The insight manifests as a subtle aura of color around creatures - gold for good, red for evil, gray for neutral. **Level** judgment becomes more penetrating and detailed.

**5.2.2** *Law Binding* - Enforce order: Create magical restraints that bind creatures to their word or prevent harmful actions. **Instant** Create a brief magical constraint that prevents one harmful action or enforces one promise made by a creature within 30 feet. **Active** Maintain sustained magical law enforcement throughout the encounter, preventing violence, theft, or other harmful actions while encouraging honorable behavior. The binding is strongest against those who respect law and order. **Level** additional creatures bound.

**5.2.3** *Noble Aura* - Project dignity: Radiate an aura of nobility and high birth that commands respect and attention. **Instant** Create a brief moment of commanding presence that forces all creatures within 20 feet to acknowledge your noble status. **Active** Maintain a sustained aura of nobility throughout the encounter, making it easier to influence others, gain access to restricted areas, and command respect from those who recognize social hierarchy. **Level** noble aura becomes more compelling and far-reaching.

**5.2.4** *Purify* - Cleanse corruption: Remove evil influences and magical corruption from objects, creatures, or areas. **Instant** Purge one instance of evil magic or corruption from a target within 30 feet with a flash of cleansing light. **Active** Maintain a sustained purifying field that continuously removes evil influences and magical corruption throughout the encounter. The purification is most effective against curses, evil enchantments, and demonic influences. **Level** purification becomes more thorough and extensive.

**5.2.5** *Righteous Fury* - Divine anger: Channel righteous anger into enhanced combat abilities. **Instant** Gain advantage on one attack against an evil creature as divine light guides your strike. **Active** Maintain sustained righteous fury throughout the encounter, gaining advantage on all attacks against evil creatures and dealing additional damage to undead, demons, and other evil beings. The fury manifests as a golden glow around your weapons and armor. **Level** righteous fury becomes more intense and effective.

**5.2.6** *Sacred Ground* - Consecrate area: Bless an area to make it holy and resistant to evil influences. **Instant** Create a brief surge of holy energy that purges evil from a 20-foot radius area. **Active** Maintain a sustained consecration that makes the area resistant to evil magic, undead, and demonic influences throughout the encounter. Good creatures feel strengthened and protected within the sacred ground. **Level** sacred ground becomes more extensive and powerful.

**5.3.1** *Smite Evil* - Divine strike: Channel divine power into a devastating attack against evil creatures. **Instant** Deal 2d6 additional damage to an evil creature with your next attack as divine light guides your strike. **Active** Maintain the power to smite evil throughout the encounter, dealing additional damage to evil creatures with each attack. The smite is most effective against undead, demons, and creatures of pure evil. **Level** smite damage increases by 1d6 per level.

**5.3.2** *Turn Undead* - Repel undead: Use divine authority to drive away undead creatures. **Instant** Force one undead creature within 30 feet to flee from you for one round with a display of holy power. **Active** Maintain the power to turn undead throughout the encounter, keeping undead creatures at bay and preventing them from approaching within 30 feet. The turning is most effective against mindless undead and weaker undead creatures. **Level** additional undead affected and turning becomes more powerful.

**5.3.3** *Virtue* - Enhance character: Temporarily enhance the moral character and resolve of yourself or an ally. **Instant** Grant advantage on one saving throw or ability check as divine virtue strengthens the target. **Active** Maintain sustained virtue throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all saving throws and resistance to fear, charm, and other mind-affecting effects. The virtue manifests as a subtle golden glow and increased moral resolve. **Level** additional creatures affected.

**5.3.4** *Wisdom* - Divine insight: Gain supernatural wisdom and insight into complex situations. **Instant** Receive a brief flash of divine wisdom that provides insight into one immediate problem or decision. **Active** Maintain sustained divine wisdom throughout the encounter, gaining advantage on all Intelligence and Wisdom checks and providing guidance on moral and ethical decisions. The wisdom manifests as a sense of clarity and understanding. **Level** wisdom becomes more profound and comprehensive.

**5.3.5** *Word of Law* - Divine command: Speak with the authority of divine law to compel obedience. **Instant** Issue a single command that all creatures within 30 feet must obey if they can hear and understand you. **Active** Maintain the power of divine law throughout the encounter, allowing you to issue commands that lawful creatures find impossible to resist. The commands must be reasonable and not obviously harmful to the targets. **Level** additional creatures affected.

**5.3.6** *Zeal* - Divine fervor: Channel divine zeal into enhanced abilities and resistance. **Instant** Gain advantage on one action and resistance to one harmful effect as divine zeal strengthens you. **Active** Maintain sustained divine zeal throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all actions and resistance to fear, exhaustion, and other debilitating effects. The zeal manifests as increased energy and determination. **Level** zeal becomes more intense and far-reaching.

**5.4.1** *Atonement* - Divine forgiveness: Absolve sins and restore moral purity to a willing creature. **Instant** Remove one curse or evil influence from a willing creature within touch range with a surge of divine forgiveness. **Active** Maintain a sustained process of atonement throughout the encounter, gradually removing evil influences and restoring the target's moral purity. The atonement is most effective on creatures who genuinely seek redemption. **Level** atonement becomes more thorough and effective.

**5.4.2** *Consecrate* - Bless objects: Imbue objects with divine power to make them holy and effective against evil. **Instant** Bless one weapon or object to make it more effective against evil creatures for one attack or use. **Active** Maintain sustained consecration throughout the encounter, making weapons deal additional damage to evil creatures and protective items more effective against evil magic. **Level** additional objects consecrated.

**5.4.3** *Divine Favor* - Heavenly blessing: Call upon divine favor to enhance your abilities and protect you from harm. **Instant** Gain advantage on one action and resistance to one harmful effect as divine favor surrounds you. **Active** Maintain sustained divine favor throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all actions and resistance to harmful magic and evil influences. The favor manifests as a golden glow and increased confidence. **Level** divine favor becomes more powerful and comprehensive.

**5.4.4** *Exorcism* - Banish evil: Drive evil spirits and demonic influences from possessed creatures or cursed areas. **Instant** Attempt to banish one evil spirit or demonic influence from a creature or area within 30 feet with a display of divine power. **Active** Maintain a sustained exorcism throughout the encounter, gradually weakening and eventually banishing evil influences. The exorcism is most effective against weaker evil spirits and demonic influences. **Level** exorcism becomes more powerful and effective.

**5.4.5** *Holy Weapon* - Divine armament: Imbue a weapon with divine power to make it more effective against evil. **Instant** Bless one weapon to deal additional damage to evil creatures for one attack. **Active** Maintain sustained divine blessing throughout the encounter, making the weapon deal additional damage to evil creatures and providing protection against evil magic. **Level** additional weapons blessed.

**5.4.6** *Protection from Evil* - Divine ward: Create a protective barrier that shields against evil creatures and harmful magic. **Instant** Create a brief protective barrier that blocks one attack or harmful effect from an evil source. **Active** Maintain a sustained protective barrier throughout the encounter that provides resistance to attacks from evil creatures and harmful magic. The protection is strongest against undead, demons, and other evil beings. **Level** protection becomes more comprehensive and effective.

**5.5.1** *Divine Power* - Heavenly might: Channel divine power to enhance your physical and magical abilities. **Instant** Gain advantage on one action and deal additional damage with one attack as divine power flows through you. **Active** Maintain sustained divine power throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all actions and dealing additional damage to evil creatures. The power manifests as increased strength and a golden glow. **Level** divine power becomes more intense and far-reaching.

**5.5.2** *Holy Aura* - Divine radiance: Project an aura of divine power that protects allies and harms evil creatures. **Instant** Create a brief surge of holy energy that damages evil creatures and heals good creatures within 20 feet. **Active** Maintain a sustained holy aura throughout the encounter that continuously damages evil creatures and provides protection to good creatures. The aura manifests as a golden glow and a sense of divine presence. **Level** holy aura becomes more powerful and extensive.

**5.5.3** *Judgment Day* - Divine reckoning: Call upon divine judgment to punish evil and reward good. **Instant** Create a brief display of divine judgment that damages evil creatures and heals good creatures within 30 feet. **Active** Maintain sustained divine judgment throughout the encounter, continuously punishing evil and rewarding good. The judgment manifests as divine light that harms evil and heals good. **Level** judgment becomes more powerful and comprehensive.

**5.5.4** *Sacred Bond* - Divine connection: Create a magical bond between yourself and allies that shares divine protection and strength. **Instant** Create a brief connection that allows you to share one beneficial effect with an ally within 30 feet. **Active** Maintain a sustained sacred bond throughout the encounter that allows you to share divine protection, healing, and strength with allies. The bond manifests as golden threads of light connecting you to your allies. **Level** additional allies bonded.

**5.5.5** *Word of God* - Divine command: Speak with the authority of the divine to command reality itself. **Instant** Issue a single command that affects reality itself, such as "be still" to stop movement or "be healed" to restore health. **Active** Maintain the power of divine command throughout the encounter, allowing you to issue commands that affect reality and compel obedience from all creatures. The commands must be reasonable and not obviously harmful. **Level** divine command becomes more powerful and far-reaching.

**5.5.6** *Zealotry* - Divine fervor: Channel divine zeal into overwhelming power and determination. **Instant** Gain advantage on all actions for one round and deal additional damage to evil creatures as divine zeal overwhelms you. **Active** Maintain sustained divine zeal throughout the encounter, providing advantage on all actions and dealing massive additional damage to evil creatures. The zealotry manifests as overwhelming energy and determination. **Level** zealotry becomes more intense and comprehensive.

**5.6.1** *Divine Ascension* - Heavenly transformation: Temporarily ascend to a higher state of being, gaining divine abilities and protection. **Instant** Transform into a divine being for one round, gaining advantage on all actions and resistance to all harmful effects. **Active** Maintain divine ascension throughout the encounter, gaining supernatural abilities and divine protection. The ascension manifests as a golden glow and increased size and power. **Level** ascension becomes more powerful and comprehensive.

**5.6.2** *Holy Wrath* - Divine anger: Channel divine wrath into overwhelming power against evil. **Instant** Unleash a burst of divine wrath that damages all evil creatures within 50 feet and heals all good creatures. **Active** Maintain sustained divine wrath throughout the encounter, continuously damaging evil creatures and protecting good ones. The wrath manifests as divine fire and lightning that harms evil and protects good. **Level** wrath becomes more powerful and extensive.

**5.6.3** *Judgment of the Gods* - Divine reckoning: Call upon the judgment of the gods to punish evil and reward good on a massive scale. **Instant** Create a massive display of divine judgment that affects all creatures within 100 feet, damaging evil and healing good. **Active** Maintain sustained divine judgment throughout the encounter, creating a zone where evil is punished and good is rewarded. The judgment manifests as divine light and thunder that affects all within range. **Level** judgment becomes more powerful and far-reaching.

**5.6.4** *Sacred Covenant* - Divine pact: Create a sacred covenant that binds you and your allies to divine protection and strength. **Instant** Create a brief covenant that provides divine protection and strength to all allies within 50 feet. **Active** Maintain a sustained sacred covenant throughout the encounter that provides divine protection, healing, and strength to all allies. The covenant manifests as golden bonds of light connecting all allies. **Level** covenant becomes more powerful and extensive.

**5.6.5** *Word of Creation* - Divine authority: Speak with the authority of creation itself to command reality and existence. **Instant** Issue a single command that affects reality itself on a massive scale, such as "be at peace" to stop all conflict or "be restored" to heal all wounds. **Active** Maintain the power of divine creation throughout the encounter, allowing you to issue commands that affect reality and existence itself. The commands must be reasonable and not obviously harmful. **Level** divine authority becomes more powerful and comprehensive.

**5.6.6** *Zeal of the Righteous* - Divine fervor: Channel the zeal of the righteous into overwhelming divine power and authority. **Instant** Transform into an avatar of divine righteousness for one round, gaining overwhelming power and authority over all creatures. **Active** Maintain the zeal of the righteous throughout the encounter, gaining divine power and authority that affects all creatures within range. The zeal manifests as overwhelming divine presence and power. **Level** zeal becomes more powerful and comprehensive.
