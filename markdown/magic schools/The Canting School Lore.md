## The Canting School

### Origins and Philosophy
The Canting School emerges from the shadowed underworld of the Canting Crew - thieves, rogues, beggars, and all manner of vagabonds who live by their wits in society's margins. Unlike the grand academies with their towers and ancient lineages, the Canting School thrives in taverns, thieves' dens, and hidden meeting places where the fraternity of rogues gather to share their secrets and flash the patter (speak in cant).

This "hedge magic" was born from pure necessity - the craft of wandering rogues, criminal leaders, and all the masterless folk who must survive by their cunning when the world of honest men excludes them. The Canting School teaches that true power lies not in book learning, but in understanding the secret language of the streets and the hidden networks that connect every tavern, back alley, and criminal sanctuary.

The Canting School emphasizes adaptability, resourcefulness, and the value of appearing unremarkable. A true practitioner can walk through any social gathering - from noble courts to criminal dens - blending seamlessly while gathering intelligence and influence. The ultimate goal is to become a living network, connected to every level of society while beholden to none, wielding the kind of practical power that comes from knowing everyone's secrets.

### Practitioners and Titles
**Cony-Catchers**: Entry-level practitioners who work as confidence tricksters and small-time criminals. They learn to read marks (victims), flash the cant (speak thieves' language), and work simple lay (criminal schemes). Often serve as bulks (decoys) or stalls (accomplices) in larger operations.

**Foist and Nip**: Specialists in pickpocketing and sleight of hand who have mastered the art of the dive (theft) and the lift (stealing purses). They speak fluent thieves' cant and can fence (sell stolen goods) through established networks of receivers and upright men.

**Rufflers and Hookers**: Advanced practitioners who operate as enforcers, safecrackers, and house-breakers. They maintain networks among the Abraham men (vagrant criminals) and can organize complex lay (criminal operations) involving multiple members of the canting crew.

**Upright Men**: Masters who lead criminal organizations and maintain the laws of the underworld. These practitioners control territories, settle disputes between rogues, and coordinate the activities of entire brotherhoods. They are fluent in all forms of cant and maintain contacts with corrupt officials.

**The Arch-Rogue**: The legendary highest rank, individuals who supposedly control criminal activity across entire regions. Their existence is whispered of in cant but never proven, known only through the complex webs of favor and obligation that span the criminal underworld.

### Dress and Appearance
Cove School practitioners favor practical, unremarkable clothing that helps them blend into whatever environment they find themselves in. They excel at dressing the part - appearing as merchants when dealing with traders, laborers when working the docks, or servants when infiltrating noble households. Their garments often contain hidden pockets, reinforced sections, and carefully concealed tools.

Many practitioners develop distinctive but subtle marks of their trade - callused hands that speak of honest labor despite their magical abilities, a keen eye for evaluating both goods and people, or the particular bearing that comes from being comfortable in any social situation. Advanced practitioners often carry multiple sets of identification and can shift their apparent social class as easily as changing clothes.

Their most characteristic feature is their network of small, practical magical items - coins that always land on the desired side, keys that fit common locks, ink that fades after a set time, or papers that display different text to different readers. These tools mark them as serious practitioners to those who know what to look for.

### Sacred Components and Materials
**Canting Coins**: Special tokens used to identify members of the brotherhood and seal underworld contracts. Each bears cant symbols that reveal the holder's rank and affiliations. When two rogues exchange these coins while speaking the proper cant phrases, they create binding agreements honored throughout the criminal underworld.

**Flash Papers**: Documents written in thieves' cant that appear as meaningless scribbles to those who cannot "flash the patter." These papers carry encoded messages between practitioners and update their text based on who reads them, showing different information to upright men, law officers, and members of the canting crew.

**Budge Barrels**: Small containers that appear to hold ordinary goods but actually conceal stolen items, tools, or contraband. These magical storage devices can only be opened by those who know the proper cant phrases and hand signals of the brotherhood.

**Mort's Masks**: Disguise items that help practitioners assume different roles within society. These include false papers, makeup, padding, and clothing that transforms rogues into convincing merchants, servants, or even law officers when activated with cant phrases.

**Darkmans Maps**: Charts written in cant that reveal the hidden criminal geography of cities - safe houses (budgets), fencing operations (receiving houses), corrupt officials (bent beaks), and the territories of different criminal organizations. Only those fluent in cant can read these maps.

**Brotherhood Marks**: Tokens, tattoos, or jewelry that identify a practitioner's rank and affiliations within the canting crew. These marks are invisible to outsiders but clearly recognizable to other members of the brotherhood, allowing for instant recognition and establishing proper criminal etiquette.

### Traditions and Practices
Every practitioner begins their day with "Taking the Air," making rounds through their territory to check in with stalls (informants), collect tribute from smaller rogues, and gather intelligence about potential marks and opportunities. During these rounds, they exchange cant phrases with other members of the brotherhood to confirm identities and share information.

The most sacred tradition is "The Upright Court," irregular gatherings where members of the canting crew come together to settle disputes, plan major operations, and initiate new members into the brotherhood. These meetings are held in rotating ken (safe houses) and conducted entirely in thieves' cant, with elaborate rituals that reinforce criminal hierarchy and brotherhood bonds.

Advanced practitioners undertake "Playing the Gonnof," assuming false identities for extended periods to infiltrate legitimate society. This dangerous practice requires mastering multiple forms of cant, maintaining complex cover stories, and never breaking character even under suspicion. Discovery means not just death, but being branded a "nose" (informer) and losing all protection of the brotherhood.

The brotherhood maintains a vast network of information through "The Patter," an oral tradition where cant phrases, criminal intelligence, and territorial knowledge are passed down through generations of rogues. This spoken archive includes detailed knowledge of every city's criminal geography, corrupt officials, safe houses, and the genealogies of criminal families stretching back centuries.

Initiation into the brotherhood involves learning "The Canting Academy," a formal education in thieves' cant that includes hundreds of specialized terms, proper criminal etiquette, and the secret histories of the underworld. Only those who can speak fluent cant and demonstrate their worth through successful criminal operations are accepted as full members of the canting crew.
