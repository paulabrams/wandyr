# Umbrakalla (Shadow Gates) Spells

*Shadows, thresholds, and dimensional paths. The secret magic of the Alu, written in darkness and void. Practitioners create Kalla gates, walk between shadows, craft dimensional spaces, and traverse the boundaries between realms.*

**5.1.1** *Befuddle* \- Shadow memories: Thread shadowy confusion through a creature's mind within 30 feet.  
**Level** (number of targets affected by shadow confusion)  
**Instant** (1 action): Target forgets their intended action this round, becoming disoriented and losing planned attacks or spells.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain shadow-threads in the target's consciousness, causing ongoing confusion and memory gaps. While active, the target has disadvantage on concentration and complex actions as shadow-thoughts continuously disrupt their focus.  
**5.1.2** *Body Swap* \- Shadow exchange: Exchange positions with a willing creature within 60 feet through shadow-paths.  
**Level** (teleportation distance and number of simultaneous connections)  
**Instant** (1 action): Both participants dissolve into darkness and reform in each other's locations, requiring dim lighting at both positions.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Create stable shadow-tunnels between your positions, allowing repeated position swapping throughout the encounter. Either participant can trigger the exchange as a quick action.  
**5.1.3** *Cloak of Shadows* \- Dark protection: Wrap yourself in living darkness that adapts to threats.  
**Level** (number of damage types protected against simultaneously)  
**Instant** (1 action): Gain resistance to the next attack against you as shadows briefly absorb the impact.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a flowing shroud of protective darkness. Choose one damage type; the cloak grants resistance to that type while active. The shadows visibly writhe and shift when protecting against attacks, and you can change the protected damage type each round.  
**5.1.4** *Dark Anchor* \- Fix dimensional point: Mark a location with shadow energy as a dimensional reference.  
**Level** (number of anchors placed and dimensional stability)  
**Instant** (1 action): Place a temporary anchor at your current position that aids dimensional magic for this encounter.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Create a stable shadow-anchor that exists partially in the shadow realm. You sense its direction and distance, and it serves as a beacon for other dimensional magic. Other Umbrakalla practitioners can sense and use your anchor if permitted.  
**5.1.5** *Darksight* \- See in darkness: Draw upon shadow realm perception to see clearly in darkness.  
**Level** (vision range and ability to pierce magical concealment)  
**Instant** (1 action): Gain perfect vision in darkness for this round, seeing as clearly as in daylight. Your eyes briefly glow with silver light.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain enhanced vision that penetrates natural and magical darkness. Your eyes show a faint silver glow visible to magical sight. This shadow-sight reveals the true shapes of things hidden by darkness but not invisible objects.  
**5.1.6** *Deep Shadow* \- Create pure darkness: Generate impenetrable shadow that blocks all light and vision.  
**Level** (radius of darkness and intensity of shadow effects)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a brief 10-foot radius sphere of absolute darkness that blocks vision for one round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a 20-foot radius sphere of magical darkness at a point within 60 feet. Even darkvision cannot penetrate this shadow, though you see normally within it. Sounds are muffled and temperature drops noticeably while active.

**5.2.1** *Dimension Door* \- Create portal: Tear doorways through the shadow realm for dimensional travel.  
**Level** (portal stability and dimensional reach improve, shadow realm navigation becomes more precise)  
**Instant** (1 action): Open a brief portal to travel up to 60 feet to any visualized location. The portal closes immediately after passage.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Create a stable doorway of writhing darkness that allows travel up to 100 feet. The portal remains open, allowing multiple uses and passage of allies. Each use involves floating through the cold void between dimensions.  
**5.2.2** *Disassemble* \- Shadow-split form: Scatter your physical form into shadow fragments.  
**Level** (fragmentation becomes more controlled and extensive, shadow form stability improves, dispersal range naturally expands)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly become insubstantial to avoid one attack, dissolving into shadow mist before immediately reforming.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a disassembled shadow-fragment form. You're immune to physical attacks but vulnerable to bright light, can move through small openings, and resist most effects. Cannot take physical actions while scattered, but can reassemble at will or when forced by bright light.  
**5.2.3** *Gate Sense* \- Detect portals: Attune your senses to dimensional disturbances and shadow realm whispers.  
**Level** (dimensional awareness becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can perceive subtler portal traces, shadow whispers become clearer)  
**Instant** (1 action): Detect any active portals or dimensional rifts within 100 feet, sensing their direction and nature.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain extended awareness of dimensional disturbances. Sense portals, gates, and rifts within a wide area, detecting their direction, distance, and activity status. Kalla gates appear more clearly than other openings, and you hear subtle whispers guiding your attention to anomalies.  
**5.2.4** *Hatred* \- Dark emotion: Plant shadow-born animosity in a creature's mind within 30 feet.  
**Level** (number of targets affected and shadow emotion intensity)  
**Instant** (1 action): Target feels sudden hostility toward a designated creature, compelling them to focus attacks on that target this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain an unnatural hatred that compels the target to prioritize the designated enemy. While active, attacks against the hated target deal 1d6 additional damage. The emotion feels genuine but unnaturally intense, potentially raising suspicion.  
**5.2.5** *Ice Heart* \- Freeze with shadow: Channel shadow-cold into a creature's vital essence through touch.  
**Level** (damage intensity and shadow-cold area spread)  
**Instant** (1 action): Deal 1d6 cold damage as supernatural chill penetrates the target's core, slowing them this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain shadow-frost within the target's essence. They take ongoing cold damage each round and move at half speed. Their breath becomes visible and frost forms at the touch point. This shadow-cold resists normal warmth and requires magical heat to fully dispel.  
**5.2.6** *Kalla Gate* \- Create pocket realm: Open dimensional pockets accessible through shadow-portals.  
**Level** (pocket dimensions become more spacious and stable, storage capacity expands organically, shadow realm connections strengthen)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a small temporary pocket that can store one item until the end of the encounter.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a stable dimensional pocket roughly 5 feet square that holds up to 100 pounds of non-living material. Access the pocket by reaching into any sufficiently large shadow. Only you and designated allies can open the gate, providing secure storage throughout the scene.

**5.3.1** *Kallascript* \- Write gate runes: Inscribe shadow-runes that trigger dimensional connections.  
**Level** (number of connected runes and dimensional link complexity)  
**Instant** (1 action): Write a simple shadow-mark that creates a brief portal to another visible location when activated.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Inscribe complex Kallascript runes that remain dormant until triggered by touch or command words. When activated, they create gates connecting to other runes you've inscribed. The shifting shadow-marks are invisible in bright light but clearly visible in darkness.  
**5.3.2** *Masquerade* \- Shadow dance: Weave shadows to alter your appearance.  
**Level** (shadow disguises become more convincing and sophisticated, illusion stability improves, light resistance increases)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly shift shadows around yourself to confuse observers about your identity this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain shadow-woven disguise that changes your general appearance to resemble another humanoid. The illusion affects only visual appearance, not voice or mannerisms. Works best in dim lighting where shadows naturally flow, but bright light may reveal inconsistencies.  
**5.3.3** *Miniaturize* \- Shrink into shadows: Compress your form using shadow manipulation.  
**Level** (size compression becomes more dramatic and stable, shadow integration improves, miniaturization control increases)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly shrink to squeeze through a small opening or hide behind minimal cover this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain reduced size at half your normal dimensions. Equipment shrinks proportionally and movement speed decreases, but you hide more easily and slip through smaller spaces. Take half damage from area effects but deal reduced damage with physical attacks.  
**5.3.4** *Mind Shatter* \- Shadow thoughts: Send shadow-tendrils into a creature's mind within 30 feet.  
**Level** (number of targets and shadow tendril intensity)  
**Instant** (1 action): Deal 1d6 psychic damage and disrupt concentration, visible as darkness streaming toward the target's head.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain shadow-assault on the target's thoughts. They take psychic damage each round and suffer ongoing concentration disruption. The continuous mental fragmentation makes it difficult to form coherent plans or maintain focus on complex tasks.  
**5.3.5** *Night Sphere* \- Create darkness: Conjure supernatural darkness that moves with you.  
**Level** (darkness becomes deeper and more encompassing, shadow realm effects intensify, supernatural obscurement improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a brief 10-foot radius sphere of darkness around yourself that blocks light this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a 15-foot radius sphere of supernatural darkness centered on you. The sphere moves as you do, blocking normal and magical light while allowing you clear vision within. Creatures inside navigate by sound and touch, and the darkness feels cold with faint shadow realm whispers.  
**5.3.6** *Nightmare Seed* \- Dark dreams: Plant shadow-memory fragments in a creature's mind.  
**Level** (number of targets and nightmare intensity/duration)  
**Instant** (1 action): Implant a brief nightmare vision that causes momentary disorientation and fear.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Plant a growing seed of shadow-dreams that affects the target's rest. When they next sleep, vivid nightmares of shadow, loss, and dimensional horror prevent restful sleep, causing fatigue. The dreams fade upon waking but leave lingering unease throughout the following day.

**5.4.1** *Ooze Form* \- Shadow substance: Transform into fluid shadow-substance.  
**Level** (form fluidity and shadow substance control)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly become liquid shadow to flow through a crack or avoid an attack this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ooze form, allowing you to flow through small openings and along surfaces. You're immune to physical attacks but vulnerable to light damage, cannot use equipment or cast spells, and move at half speed. The transformation feels like melting into cool darkness.  
**5.4.2** *Phantom Coach* \- Shadow transport: Summon a vehicle of crystallized shadow.  
**Level** (shadow vehicle becomes more substantial and swift, can carry more passengers, ethereal construction improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a brief shadow-platform that can carry you and one ally across a gap or obstacle.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain an elegant carriage of dark glass and shadow with ethereal horses. Carries up to 4 passengers at double walking speed over difficult terrain including water. Provides weather protection but dissolves if heavily damaged. The horses leave no tracks.  
**5.4.3** *Plane Shift* \- Change reality: Shift into a parallel shadow-dimension.  
**Level** (dimensional shift depth and shadow-plane interaction)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly phase into the shadow-plane to avoid attacks or pass through obstacles this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain existence in the shadow-dimension where you're invisible and intangible to the normal world but can observe clearly. Cannot affect the physical realm but move freely through obstacles. The shadow-plane feels colder with muted colors and distant whispers.  
**5.4.4** *Realm Weave* \- Connect spaces: Create passages through the shadow realm linking distant locations.  
**Level** (dimensional weaving becomes more sophisticated and stable, can maintain more passages, shadow realm navigation improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Open a brief tunnel through shadow to quickly move between two shadowed areas within 100 feet.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain invisible passages linking locations within 500 feet. Both endpoints must be in shadow or darkness, travel takes one round regardless of distance, and up to 6 creatures can use each passage. Connections appear as subtle shadow darkening to those sensitive to dimensional magic.  
**5.4.5** *Shadow Bind* \- Trap in darkness: Animate shadows to restrain a creature within 30 feet.  
**Level** (number of targets bound and shadow restraint strength)  
**Instant** (1 action): Shadows briefly coil around the target, hindering their movement this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain shadow-bonds that hold the target immobile. They can struggle to break free, but the bonds tighten in response to movement. Bright light weakens the bindings significantly. The shadows feel cold and substantial like chains of solid darkness.  
**5.4.6** *Shadow Clone* \- Create dark duplicate: Craft a duplicate from shadow material.  
**Level** (number of clones and shadow duplicate complexity)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a brief shadow-image that confuses enemies about your location this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a semi-solid duplicate that follows mental commands. The clone performs simple tasks and has basic combat abilities but takes double damage from light attacks. It shares your memories and personality but lacks magical abilities and equipment, dissolving into shadow wisps when destroyed.

**5.5.1** *Shadow Meld* \- Merge with darkness: Become one with an existing shadow.  
**Level** (shadow size compatibility and dimensional hiding depth)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly meld with a shadow to hide from immediate detection.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain perfect concealment within a shadow while remaining aware of surroundings. Cannot move, act, or be detected by normal means, but can observe everything nearby. Emerge instantly at will in any adjacent unoccupied space. The merging feels like sinking into cool liquid darkness.  
**5.5.2** *Shadow Step* \- Travel through dark: Transport through the shadow realm to areas of darkness.  
**Level** (shadow travel becomes more fluid and far-reaching, can bring more companions, dimensional transition improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Teleport up to 30 feet to any visible shadow or darkness, creating brief shadow wisps at both points.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain enhanced shadow-stepping abilities. Travel up to 60 feet to any darkness you can see, bringing equipment and one willing creature. Each journey feels like floating through a cold, whispering void but takes no physical time.  
**5.5.3** *Shadow Time* \- Pause in darkness: Step partially outside normal time flow while in shadow.  
**Level** (temporal displacement intensity and shadow realm time manipulation)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly experience time slowing around you, allowing perfect observation of events this round.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain partial temporal displacement while in shadow or darkness. Experience everything moving extremely slowly while you move freely within shadowed areas. Cannot interact with the slowed world but observe events unfolding with perfect clarity and memory.  
**5.5.4** *Shuffle* \- Shadow displacement: Rapidly exchange positions with nearby shadows.  
**Level** (shadow displacement becomes more rapid and disorienting, can maintain more positions, flickering precision improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Flicker between shadows to confuse attackers, gaining improved defense against the next attack.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain constant flickering between shadows within 30 feet, creating multiple false images. Attackers have difficulty targeting your true location as you appear in several places at once. Requires dim lighting and multiple shadows to function effectively.  
**5.5.5** *Smoke Form* \- Become shadow: Transform into sentient shadow-smoke.  
**Level** (smoke density control and dimensional dispersal area)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly become smoke to pass through a small opening or avoid an attack.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain smoke form, allowing you to float and pass through openings. Immune to physical damage but take double from wind or light attacks. Move at normal speed in any direction and speak in whispers. The transformation leaves you feeling temporarily insubstantial even after returning to solid form.  
**5.5.6** *Space Fold* \- Bend dimensions: Fold space to bring distant locations within reach.  
**Level** (spatial folding becomes more dramatic and stable, dimensional manipulation improves, reality distortion deepens)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly fold space to interact with something up to 60 feet away as if adjacent, creating visible ripples.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain spatial distortion around yourself. Repeatedly fold space to interact with objects or creatures up to 100 feet away as if they were adjacent. Each fold creates disorienting ripples and sensations of compressed distance for observers.

**5.6.1** *Threshold Walk* \- Pass through doors: Phase through barriers by shifting into the shadow realm.  
**Level** (barrier thickness penetrated and dimensional phasing stability)  
**Instant** (1 action): Pass through one barrier up to 1 foot thick, experiencing a moment of cold void.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the ability to phase through barriers up to 3 feet thick during movement. Works on doors, walls, and gates, though magical barriers or dimensional wards block passage. Cannot carry other creatures through, and each passage brings a chill from the shadow realm.  
**5.6.2** *Twilight Veil* \- Walk between worlds: Envelope yourself in dimensional mist.  
**Level** (dimensional existence balance and twilight realm control)  
**Instant** (1 action): Briefly become translucent to avoid an attack or pass through an obstacle.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain existence partially in both physical and shadow realms. You're translucent and can pass through creatures and objects, though bright light forces you fully physical. Can interact with either dimension but not both simultaneously. The veil appears as clinging twilight fog.  
**5.6.3** *Void Gate* \- Open dark portal: Tear gateways to the shadow realm.  
**Level** (void portals become more stable and impressive, shadow realm connections strengthen, dimensional tearing improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Open a small, brief portal to the shadow realm for quick passage or to retrieve something stored there.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a stable gateway 6 feet tall and 3 feet wide connecting to the shadow realm. Creatures can pass through safely, though the shadow realm has its own dangers. The gate appears as absolute darkness surrounded by crackling shadow-energy.  
**5.6.4** *Void Shield* \- Dark protection: Manifest a shield of solidified void-energy.  
**Level** (shield size and void-energy absorption capacity)  
**Instant** (1 action): Create a brief void-barrier that blocks one attack with exceptional effectiveness.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a circular shield of void-energy on your arm that absorbs significant damage before dissolving. Grants advantage against ranged attacks and flickers with dark energy when blocking damage. The weightless shield feels substantial and cold to the touch.  
**5.6.5** *Void Step* \- Brief teleport: Step through micro-portals to the void between dimensions.  
**Level** (void stepping becomes more fluid and responsive, dimensional transitions quicken, void mastery improves)  
**Instant** (1 action): Teleport up to 30 feet silently through the void, leaving only a brief absence of light at your departure.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain enhanced void-stepping abilities allowing repeated short-range teleportation. Each step is silent with perfect concealment during transit. Can bring small equipment but not other creatures. This fundamental technique becomes more fluid and responsive while active.  
**5.6.6** *Perfect Shadow* \- Complete control: Gain command over shadows within 60 feet.  
**Level** (shadow mastery becomes more comprehensive and powerful, can create more complex constructs, darkness control deepens)  
**Instant** (1 action): Shape nearby shadows into one simple tool, weapon, or barrier that lasts for this encounter.  
**Active** (2+ actions): Maintain absolute control over all shadows within 60 feet, shaping them into multiple tools, weapons, or barriers at will. Shadow-crafted items function as normal equipment but dissolve in bright light. Create weapons dealing 1d6 damage, protective barriers, or functional tools. Shadows respond instantly to thought and maintain complex shapes while active.
