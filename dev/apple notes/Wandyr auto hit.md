# Wandyr auto hit?

What if attacks automatically "hit" unless someone uses their action to parry?

Players would have Soak rather than roll on the Oracle "did I avoid the hit?".

Monsters auto-roll damage, and the player rolls defense as Soak?

Soak could be on the same scale as Oracle, or it could be Soak Dice based on Armor instead of armor providing Hearts.

Perhaps the soak dice need to match the exact number rolled? So if the damage die is a 5 then the soak die needs to be a 5 to match?

## Example

Two orcs shoot at you… GM rolls 2d6: 3 and 4

Player rolls 3 Soak dice for their Muster, with 3, 4, 6. So they avoid both hits. However, if a roll had been a 1 they would not have avoided it.

Armor plus adds to the soak die, so the only way to stop a hit from an Ogre might be to have nice armor.
