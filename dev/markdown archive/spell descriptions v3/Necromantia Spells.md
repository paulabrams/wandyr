# Necromantia (Death) Spells

*Death, spirits, and decay. The forbidden magic of the Avathars, inscribed in blood and bone. Practitioners bind spirits, animate dead, drain life force, and manipulate the boundary between life and death.*


**6.1.1** *Animate Skeleton* - Raise bones: Channel dark energy into a set of bones to create a skeletal minion. **Instant** Animate bones within 30 feet to perform a single task or attack (1d6 damage) before crumbling. Dark energy assembles the skeleton briefly for one action. **Active** Maintain an animated skeleton throughout the encounter that has 3 hit points and follows simple commands until destroyed or the scene ends. The skeletal minion remains under your control. **Level** number of skeletons animated.
**6.1.2** *Black Sacrament* - Death ritual: Sacrifice 1 hit point to empower your necromantic magic. **Instant** Perform a ritual that makes your next spell this turn deal +1d6 necrotic damage. Dark runes appear on your flesh, glowing with malevolent energy. **Active** Maintain empowered necromantic magic for the remainder of the encounter, making all your necromantic spells deal +1d6 necrotic damage. This sacrifice cannot reduce you below 1 hit point. **Level** bonus necrotic damage dice.
**6.1.3** *Blood Curse* - Sacrifice life: Cut yourself and speak a curse to hex a target. **Instant** Take 1d6 damage to place a hex on a target within 60 feet, giving them disadvantage on their next roll. Blood droplets float through the air toward the target, marking them with crimson stains. **Active** Maintain a sustained hex that gives the target disadvantage on all rolls until the curse is triggered or the encounter ends. The cursed target remains marked with your blood magic. **Level** number of targets affected.
**6.1.4** *Blood Track* - Follow wounds: Sense the direction and distance to bleeding creatures you've seen injured. **Instant** Sense direction to one bleeding creature within the current area. The spell creates a faint red trail visible only to you, leading toward the wounded target. **Active** Track all bleeding creatures within 1 mile throughout the scene, with the trail growing brighter as you approach. You can follow multiple blood trails simultaneously. **Level** tracking range in miles.
**6.1.5** *Bone Puppet* - Animate dead: Control a Small or smaller corpse like a marionette. **Instant** Animate a corpse within 30 feet to perform one simple action or attack (1d6 damage) before collapsing. Spectral strings of dark energy connect your fingers to the animated corpse. **Active** Maintain control over the puppet for the encounter, moving it at half speed and commanding it until concentration is lost. The spectral strings remain visible throughout. **Level** size of creature controlled up to Medium+1 per Level.
**6.1.6** *Bone Shield* - Skeletal armor: Bones float around you in a protective orbit. **Instant** Grant +2 AC against the next attack made against you. Bones from nearby corpses or the ground briefly orbit your body defensively. **Active** Maintain +2 AC against all attacks for the encounter, but only absorbs three hits before the bones are depleted. When the shield absorbs damage, bone fragments scatter dramatically before reforming. **Level** AC bonus and hits absorbed.

**6.2.1** *Command Undead* - Control dead: Issue a command to an undead creature within 30 feet. **Instant** Force an undead creature with 2 Hit Dice or fewer to obey a single-word command on its next turn ("flee," "grovel," "halt," "approach"). Glowing purple runes appear around the target's head as your will compels obedience. **Active** Maintain control over the target for the encounter, making it obey your verbal commands until it succeeds on a Wisdom saving throw at the end of its turn. The purple runes persist throughout your control. **Level** Hit Dice of undead controlled.
**6.2.2** *Comprehend Dead* - Dead languages: Understand written languages left by the deceased. **Instant** Understand one piece of dead writing (epitaph, curse, final message). The text glows with faint spectral light as words rearrange into comprehensible meaning. **Active** Understand all dead languages throughout the scene, including continuous translation of newly discovered texts. Works on ancient scripts and forgotten tongues from deceased writers. **Level** age of writings comprehended in centuries.
**6.2.3** *Corpse Explosion* - Detonate dead: Cause a corpse within 60 feet to violently explode. **Instant** Make a corpse explode, dealing 1d6 necrotic damage to all creatures within 10 feet (Dex save for half). Bone fragments and putrid flesh spray outward in a gruesome display, completely destroying the corpse. **Active** Create a lingering death field that deals 1d6 necrotic damage to creatures entering or starting their turn in the 10-foot area throughout the encounter. The explosion leaves behind necrotic energy. **Level** explosive force becomes more devastating, necrotic energy spreads further, death field effects intensify.
**6.2.4** *Dark Blessing* - Death's power: Invoke the favor of death itself. **Instant** Gain advantage on your next roll this turn as dark energy swirls around you and whispers from beyond guide your actions. Your eyes turn black as the blessing takes hold. **Active** Maintain advantage on all rolls for the encounter, but each use leaves you feeling progressively colder and more hollow. The dark blessing persists throughout the scene. **Level** number of allies affected by blessing.
**6.2.5** *Death Gate* - Portal to underworld: Create a 5-foot diameter portal to the realm of the dead. **Instant** Briefly open a portal that deals 1d6 necrotic damage to living creatures touching it before snapping shut. The portal appears as a swirling vortex of darkness. **Active** Maintain an open portal for the encounter, allowing undead passage and continuously threatening living creatures who approach. Living creatures within 10 feet feel an overwhelming urge to approach (Wisdom save to resist). **Level** portal size in 5-foot increments and attraction range.
**6.2.6** *Death Touch* - Wither life: Infuse your touch with necrotic energy. **Instant** Make your next touch deal 1d6 necrotic damage and reduce max HP by the same amount until a long rest. Your hand appears skeletal and cold as necrotic energy flows through it. **Active** Maintain the withering effect on all your touch attacks throughout the encounter. Undead touched by this spell are healed for 1d6 hit points instead. Living tissue withers and decays at your touch. **Level** withering effect becomes more potent and lasting, necrotic energy penetrates deeper, life force drain intensifies.

**6.3.1** *Death Ward* - Protect from necrotic: Shroud yourself or an ally in protective deathly energy. **Instant** Grant resistance to the next source of necrotic damage or advantage on the next death saving throw to someone within touch range. A faint aura of pale light surrounds the protected individual, visible only to those with magical sight. **Active** Maintain resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on death saves for the encounter, but the ward dissipates if more than 10 necrotic damage is taken at once. The protective aura persists throughout. **Level** number of targets protected.
**6.3.2** *Doom Mark* - Mark for death: Place a spectral skull mark on a creature within 60 feet. **Instant** Mark a target so your next attack against them deals +1d6 necrotic damage. The mark appears as a glowing sigil on the creature's forehead, pulsing ominously with each heartbeat, visible only to you and other necromancers. **Active** Maintain the doom mark for the encounter so all your attacks against the target deal +1d6 necrotic damage. Only one creature can bear your doom mark at a time. **Level** death mark becomes more potent and persistent, can affect additional targets, necrotic resonance strengthens.
**6.3.3** *Drain Life* - Steal vitality: Siphon life force from a target within 30 feet. **Instant** Deal 1d6 necrotic damage and heal yourself for half the damage dealt. Dark tendrils of energy flow from the victim to you, visible as wisps of shadow. **Active** Each turn, automatically drain 1d6 life from the target and heal yourself for half, until they move out of range or break line of sight throughout the encounter. The target feels chill and weakness as their vitality transfers. No effect on undead or constructs. **Level** life drain becomes more efficient and powerful, energy transfer intensifies, vitality theft deepens.
**6.3.4** *Fear Aura* - Death terror: Emanate an aura of supernatural dread in a 15-foot radius. **Instant** Force all creatures within 15 feet to save or be frightened until the end of their next turn. The air grows cold and heavy, filled with whispers of mortality and doom. **Active** Maintain the aura for the encounter, frightening creatures that enter for the first time or start their turn within range. Your shadow writhes and reaches toward nearby creatures throughout the effect. **Level** dread aura spreads further and becomes more oppressive, fear effects deepen, death whispers multiply.
**6.3.5** *Ghost Bind* - Trap spirits: Create spectral chains that bind an incorporeal undead creature. **Instant** Force an incorporeal undead within 30 feet to make a Charisma save or be restrained until the end of its next turn. The ghostly chains appear as glowing links of pale energy wrapped around the spirit. **Active** Maintain restraint for the encounter, preventing the target from using incorporeal abilities or teleporting until it succeeds on a Charisma save at the end of its turn. The spectral chains persist throughout. **Level** Hit Dice of spirits bound and number of targets.
**6.3.6** *Grave Mist* - Create death fog: Conjure a 20-foot radius sphere of gray, cloying mist. **Instant** Create mist centered on a point within 60 feet that briefly deals 1d6 necrotic damage to all living creatures within before dispersing. The mist heavily obscures the area. **Active** Maintain the mist for the encounter, dealing 1d6 necrotic damage to living creatures starting their turn within, until dispersed by strong wind. Undead creatures remain unaffected and can see normally through the supernatural fog. **Level** death fog spreads further and becomes more toxic, necrotic effects intensify, mist persistence improves.

**6.4.1** *Life Sense* - Detect living: Sense the location of living creatures within 60 feet. **Instant** Sense all living creatures within 60 feet for a brief moment, getting a snapshot of their current positions. Living beings appear as warm, glowing outlines in your vision, pulsing with each heartbeat. **Active** Continuously track all living creatures for the encounter, watching their movements in real-time even through walls and barriers. Cannot determine creature type, only that they live. Undead and constructs remain invisible. **Level** life detection becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can sense subtler life signs, perception clarity improves.
**6.4.2** *Mass Animation* - Raise army: Animate up to three Small corpses or sets of bones. **Instant** Animate corpses within 30 feet to perform one coordinated attack or action before crumbling to dust. Each animated creature has 1 hit point and deals 1d6 damage with simple attacks. **Active** Maintain undead minions that follow basic commands for the encounter, crumbling when reduced to 0 hit points or when the scene ends. The necromantic energy sustains them throughout. **Level** animation becomes more efficient and powerful, can raise more undead, animated creatures become more durable.
**6.4.3** *Marble Madness* - Death's toys: Animate small bones within 60 feet to roll and bounce like marbles. **Instant** Force creatures in a 20-foot area to make a Dex save or fall prone as bones briefly animate underfoot. The chattering bone fragments whisper fragments of their former lives as they skitter about. **Active** Make the entire 60-foot area become difficult terrain for the encounter as bones continuously roll and trip creatures moving through. The animated bones persist throughout the scene. **Level** bone animation spreads over wider areas, fragments become more disruptive, whispers grow more disturbing.
**6.4.4** *Pain Echo* - Share suffering: Force a creature within 30 feet to share your pain when you take damage. **Instant** Make one target within 30 feet take half the damage as psychic damage (no save) the next time you take damage this turn. Dark energy connects you to the victim, creating a visible link of suffering. **Active** Maintain the pain link for the encounter so each time you take damage, you can use your reaction to make one creature within 30 feet take half the damage as psychic damage. The dark connection persists. **Level** number of creatures linked to share pain.
**6.4.5** *Plague Wind* - Spread death: Create a 30-foot cone of diseased wind carrying decay and sickness. **Instant** Force creatures in the cone to make a Con save or take 1d6 necrotic damage and be poisoned until the end of their next turn. The wind carries the stench of rotting flesh and grave soil. **Active** Make the wind linger for the encounter, poisoning creatures that enter or start their turn in the area (save ends at end of turn). The diseased wind persists throughout the scene. **Level** diseased wind spreads further and becomes more virulent, decay effects intensify, sickness becomes more persistent.
**6.4.6** *Psychometry* - Read death echoes: Touch an object or location where someone died to experience their final moments. **Instant** Experience a 6-second vision of one death that occurred here recently from the victim's perspective. You feel their emotions and pain but take no damage. **Active** Experience detailed death echoes from multiple victims over time, seeing up to several deaths that occurred in this location throughout the encounter, with older deaths becoming progressively fainter. The visions continue as you explore. **Level** death echoes become clearer and more detailed, can perceive older deaths, emotional resonance deepens.

**6.5.1** *Raise Dead* - Create undead: Target a humanoid corpse and raise it as a zombie. **Instant** Animate a corpse to perform one action (attack, move, or simple task) before collapsing permanently. Dark energy flows into the corpse as it lurches back to unholy life. **Active** Maintain zombie control for the encounter with standard zombie statistics, then it collapses and cannot be reanimated by this spell. The zombie follows your mental commands throughout. **Level** Hit Dice of corpse that can be raised.
**6.5.2** *Sniff* - Smell death: Make your sense of smell supernaturally acute for death-related scents. **Instant** Detect all undead within 60 feet and determine how one nearby corpse died. You can detect undead creatures by smell alone, even if hidden or invisible. **Active** Continuously track undead throughout the encounter and analyze death scents in detail, determining time and manner of death for all corpses in the area. Different types of death produce distinct odors you can identify. **Level** death scent detection becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can distinguish subtler death traces, olfactory precision improves.
**6.5.3** *Soul Cage* - Trap essence: Create a spectral prison around a creature within 30 feet with 10 hit points or fewer. **Instant** Force the target to make a Charisma save or be restrained until the end of their next turn. The cage appears as a ribcage of spectral bones with dark energy bars between them. **Active** Keep the target caged for the encounter, restrained and unable to use movement abilities, until they succeed on a Charisma save at the end of their turn. The spectral prison persists throughout. **Level** soul cage becomes more powerful and inescapable, can trap stronger creatures, spiritual binding intensifies.
**6.5.4** *Soul Rend* - Tear spirit: Make a spectral claw attack against a creature within 30 feet to damage their soul. **Instant** Make a spell attack; on hit, deal 1d6 psychic damage and reduce max HP by the same amount until a long rest (Wisdom save negates HP reduction). The victim feels invisible claws tearing at their essence. **Active** Enhance your attacks for the encounter with spectral claws that automatically attempt to rend souls on successful hits. The spiritual damage persists throughout. **Level** spiritual claws become sharper and more devastating, soul damage deepens, essence tearing becomes more thorough.
**6.5.5** *Speak with Dead* - Question spirits: Commune with the spirit of a corpse. **Instant** Ask one question of the dead and receive a brief answer before the spirit departs. The corpse's eyes glow with pale light during conversation. **Active** Conduct an extended conversation for the scene, asking multiple questions and receiving detailed responses until the spirit chooses to depart. The spirit answers truthfully but only knows what it knew in life, speaking in a hollow, echoing voice. **Level** number of questions and age of corpse in years.
**6.5.6** *Spirit Chains* - Bind ghost: Wrap ethereal chains around an incorporeal creature within 30 feet. **Instant** Force the target to make a Charisma save or become corporeal until the end of its next turn. Ethereal chains of pale energy wrap around the spirit, forcing it into a partially corporeal state. **Active** Maintain visible spectral chains for the encounter, keeping the creature corporeal until it succeeds on a Charisma save at the end of its turn. While bound, the creature loses incorporeal abilities and takes normal damage from non-magical attacks. **Level** Hit Dice of spirits bound and chain strength.

**6.6.1** *Spirit Form* - Become ghostly: Make your body translucent and partially incorporeal. **Instant** Become incorporeal until the end of your turn, gaining resistance to all damage except force and radiant. Your appearance becomes ghostly and pale with a faint spectral glow. **Active** Remain incorporeal for the encounter but cannot manipulate objects or cast spells with material components. You can move through objects and creatures as difficult terrain but take 1d6 force damage if you end your turn inside an object. **Level** number of allies affected by spirit form.
**6.6.2** *Truth Sense* - Death's truth: Let death reveal deceptions through supernatural insight. **Instant** Automatically detect if the next statement made in your presence is a lie. When someone lies, their words appear to drip with spectral blood only you can see. **Active** Detect all lies spoken in your presence for the encounter. The spell reveals falsehood but not the actual truth, and cannot be fooled by illusions or enchantments. **Level** range of truth detection in 30-foot increments.
**6.6.3** *Undead Sight* - See spirits: See invisible undead creatures and spirits within 60 feet as if they were visible. **Instant** See all invisible undead within 60 feet for a brief moment and detect recent deaths in the immediate area. Your eyes become pale and clouded, giving you an unsettling appearance. **Active** Continuously see invisible undead for the encounter and track incorporeal spirits, detecting hidden burial sites and old battlefields throughout the scene. You can also see spiritual auras where death occurred. **Level** sight range in 60-foot increments and age of death auras.
**6.6.4** *Vampiric Touch* - Drain energy: Make your touch drain life force, healing you when you damage living creatures. **Instant** Make your next successful melee attack heal you for half the damage dealt to a living creature. Your hands glow with dark energy and feel unnaturally cold. **Active** Enhance all your melee attacks against living creatures for the encounter to heal you for half the damage dealt. No effect against undead or constructs. The dark energy persists throughout. **Level** percentage of damage healed per 25%.
**6.6.5** *Wizard Mark* - Death runes: Inscribe invisible necromantic runes on an object or surface within touch range. **Instant** Create a temporary mark that lasts for the current scene, allowing you to track the object's location. The mark appears as glowing skull symbols to you and other necromancers. **Active** Create a permanent mark that lasts for weeks, always showing you the direction and distance to marked objects within 1 mile. The runes carry a faint aura of death magic that makes living creatures uneasy. **Level** number of marks and tracking range in miles.
**6.6.6** *Perfect Death* - Ultimate end: Attempt to instantly slay a creature within 30 feet with 15 hit points or fewer. **Instant** Force the target to make a Constitution save; on failure, they drop to 0 hit points and begin death saves immediately. On success, take 1d6 necrotic damage. Dark energy surrounds the victim as death itself reaches out to claim them. **Active** Enhance your attacks for the encounter so attacks against creatures with 15 or fewer HP force Constitution saves or instant death. No effect on undead or constructs. **Level** death magic becomes more potent and irresistible, can affect stronger creatures, mortality compulsion intensifies.
