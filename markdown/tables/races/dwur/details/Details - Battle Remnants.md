# Random Details - Battle Remnants

*Roll 1d20 for evidence of ancient conflicts.*

1. **Broken Weapons** - Dwur axe, blade snapped; fought something very hard
2. **Scorch Marks** - Walls blackened by fire; dragon breath or magical attack
3. **Arrow Shafts** - Embedded in wooden doors; besieged from corridor
4. **Dried Blood** - Dark stains on stone; old battle, many casualties
5. **Shattered Armor** - Dwur mail, rent open; claws or massive impact
6. **Bone Fragments** - Mixed species remains; Dwur and enemy bones
7. **Spell Components** - Scattered reagents; magic battle, ritual interrupted
8. **Barricade Remains** - Furniture piled up; desperate last stand
9. **Claw Marks** - Deep gouges in stone; large creature attack
10. **Frozen Corpse** - Dwur warrior, preserved; died during magical winter
11. **Melted Metal** - Weapons fused to wall; extreme heat, fire magic
12. **Shadow Burns** - Dark outlines on walls; victims vaporized by shadow magic
13. **Shattered Shields** - Wooden fragments; defensive battle
14. **Poison Stains** - Green residue on stone; chemical warfare
15. **Lightning Scars** - Forked burns on walls; electrical magic
16. **Frost Damage** - Cracked stone from cold; ice magic attack
17. **Acid Burns** - Pitted stone surfaces; corrosive attack
18. **Sonic Damage** - Shattered glass, cracked stone; sound magic
19. **Teleportation Residue** - Sparkling dust; dimensional magic
20. **Time Distortion** - Aged and fresh damage mixed; temporal magic
