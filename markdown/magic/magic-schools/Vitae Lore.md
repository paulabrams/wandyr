## The Hallow School of Vitae

### Origins and Philosophy

Vitae magic springs from the ancient understanding that all life is interconnected through growth, harmony, and the eternal cycle of nature. In the deep forests of Dorthonia, where the Hallow Elves first learned to commune with the living essence of existence, they discovered that true life magic requires understanding the harmony between all living things. The earliest practitioners learned that healing demands compassion, growth requires nurturing, and power comes from embracing the natural cycles of birth, renewal, and peaceful coexistence.

The school teaches that magic flows through the living essence of all things - from the smallest plant to the mightiest beast - and that true mastery comes from understanding that all life shares the same vital harmony. Practitioners believe that by embracing the natural cycles and understanding the peaceful nature of existence, they can channel the raw power of life force to heal, protect, and maintain the balance of nature. This philosophy celebrates the beauty and harmony of life while using this understanding for beneficial purposes.

### Practitioners and Titles

**The Hallow**: The most common practitioners, these are Hallow Elves who have learned to channel the living power of nature through understanding the natural cycles of growth, harmony, and peaceful coexistence. They serve as healers, protectors, and guardians, maintaining harmony with the natural world.

**Life Wardens**: Masters who specialize in healing magic and the connection between all living things, able to use life force to heal others or protect the natural world. Their spellbooks are written in living ink that grows and changes like vines.

**Growth Shapers**: Advanced practitioners who can perceive and nurture the vital currents that flow through all living things - from the sap in trees to the blood in veins. They often become advisors to tribal leaders or guardians of sacred groves.

**Peace Wardens**: Vitae specialists who focus on protective magic, using natural harmony and peaceful rituals to ward against threats while maintaining the natural balance. They often patrol the borders between Dorthonia and other realms.

**Wood Elves and Kith**: While Hallow Elves are the primary practitioners, Wood Elves and Kith have also learned to channel Vitae magic through their deep connection to nature. They bring their own unique perspectives and traditions to the school.

### Dress and Appearance

Vitae practitioners favor natural materials and earth tones, often decorated with living plants and natural elements. Their robes are typically woven from plant fibers and natural materials, dyed with living pigments that shift color with the seasons. Many wear living accessories: crowns of living flowers, belts of braided vines, or cloaks lined with fur that still carries the warmth of life.

Their spell components are kept in leather pouches that smell of earth, growing things, and fresh life. Senior practitioners often bear natural markings that resemble living patterns, and their eyes sometimes take on a green or amber tint from years of channeling life energy through harmony with nature.

### Sacred Components and Materials

**Living Ink**: The signature material of Vitae magic, this ink is brewed from crushed berries, spring water blessed by moonlight, and living plant extracts. When used to inscribe spells, the ink continues to grow and change, with words that can rewrite themselves as the caster's life force changes.

**Heartwood Staves**: Carved from trees that lived naturally, these staves retain the tree's life memory and amplify nature magic. The most prized are made from trees that lived through multiple centuries and are often decorated with living moss and natural elements.

**Life Dew**: Collected at dawn from plants that have been nurtured with care, these dewdrops serve as focusing elements for healing and growth spells. Each type of plant imparts different properties - rose dew for emotional healing, oak dew for strength.

**Harmony Stones**: Small river stones that have been buried in fertile soil and blessed by natural cycles for a full year. They become infused with concentrated life energy and are used to anchor longer-lasting Vitae effects.

**Living Charms**: Carved from living wood or crafted from natural materials, these charms correspond to different aspects of Vitae magic - oak for strength, willow for grace, pine for protection.

### Traditions and Practices

Vitae magic-users often begin each day with the "Life Meditation," touching the earth and extending their consciousness into the root networks of nearby plants while feeling the harmony of all living things. They maintain personal gardens where they grow their spell components and perform peaceful rituals to test new magical techniques.

During the changing of seasons, Vitae practitioners gather for "Harmony Ceremonies" where they share knowledge, perform group healing rituals, and renew their vows to protect the natural world through peaceful coexistence. These gatherings often take place in hidden clearings marked by standing stones covered in living ogham runes that grow and change with the seasons.

Many Vitae magic-users undertake pilgrimages to the Worldtree, a massive oak said to grow at the heart of Dorthonia, whose roots supposedly extend to every forest in the known world. Touching its bark while offering peaceful intentions is believed to grant deeper understanding of life's harmonious nature and the power of natural growth.
