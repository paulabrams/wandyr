# Weather Tables

## Instructions

**Tier 1: Climate Zone** is determined by the map or setting (same as terrain tables).

**Tier 2: Weather Pattern** is rolled (d6) at the start of each day or watch. Use the table matching the hex's climate zone. Interpret results through the current season.

**Tier 3: Weather Shift** is rolled (d6) when time passes or the GM wants to check for change. Results reference the Weather Pattern scale (1 = best, 6 = worst).

**Tier 4: Weather Condition** is a GM tool. Roll (d6) when you want to add environmental texture beyond the basic pattern.

**Tier 5: Weather Event** is a GM tool. Roll (d6) when you want something notable or atmospheric to occur.

### Example

Climate zone is Temperate, season is Autumn. Morning begins.

- **Tier 2 (Weather Pattern):** 3 → Overcast

*Grey skies, a chill in the air.*

Later that afternoon, the GM rolls for shift:

- **Tier 3 (Weather Shift):** 4 → Weather worsens one step

*The overcast sky darkens to light rain.*

## Tier 1: Climate Zone (by Latitude)

1. Polar
2. Boreal
3. Temperate
4. Subtropical
5. Tropical
6. Equatorial

## Tier 2: Weather Pattern (d6 per Climate Zone)

### Polar

1. Clear and frigid
2. Overcast and bitter
3. Light snow
4. Heavy snow
5. Blizzard
6. Ice fog

*Seasonal: Summer brings constant daylight and brief thaw. Winter brings constant darkness.*

### Boreal

1. Clear and cold
2. Overcast
3. Light snow or rain
4. Heavy snow or rain
5. Freezing rain
6. Dense fog

*Seasonal: Summer is short but mild. Winter is long and brutal. Spring/Autumn bring mud and slush.*

### Temperate

1. Clear skies
2. Partly cloudy
3. Overcast
4. Light rain
5. Heavy rain
6. Thunderstorm

*Seasonal: Spring brings rain, Summer warmth, Autumn fog and chill, Winter snow or frost.*

### Subtropical

1. Clear and hot
2. Hazy
3. Dry and windy
4. Light rain
5. Heavy rain
6. Dust storm or dry lightning

*Seasonal: Wet season and dry season rather than four seasons.*

### Tropical

1. Clear and humid
2. Hazy and still
3. Afternoon showers
4. Steady rain
5. Torrential downpour
6. Tropical storm

*Seasonal: Monsoon (wet) and dry seasons. Daily rain cycle common.*

### Equatorial

1. Hot and humid
2. Steamy
3. Afternoon thunderstorm
4. Steady rain
5. Torrential downpour
6. Brief clearing

*Seasonal: Little variation. Rain almost daily, usually afternoon.*

## Tier 3: Weather Shift (d6)

1. Weather holds steady
2. Weather holds steady
3. Weather improves one step
4. Weather worsens one step
5. Weather worsens dramatically
6. Sudden change (roll new Weather Pattern)

## Tier 4: Weather Condition (GM tool)

1. Fog or mist
2. High winds
3. Bitter cold or oppressive heat
4. Humidity or dryness
5. Unusual calm
6. Strange light (red sun, green sky, etc.)

## Tier 5: Weather Event (GM tool)

1. Rainbow or sun break
2. Aurora or strange glow
3. Ominous clouds
4. Sudden temperature shift
5. Unseasonal weather
6. Calm before the storm
