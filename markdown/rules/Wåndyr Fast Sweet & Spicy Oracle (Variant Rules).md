
# Wåndyr Fast Sweet & Spicy Oracle (Variant Rules)

## Sweet & Spicy Simplified
- **Single Sweet or Spicy** (one 6 or one 1): Affects *style*, not substance. Adds tone—luck, flair, tension—but doesn't change the scope of the action.
- **Double Sweet or Double Spicy** (two or more 6s or 1s): Affects *substance*. The benefiting side may ask a clarifying question about a new detail, advantage, or complication.

## Exceeding a Normal Action
To go beyond a normal action (like hitting multiple targets or pulling off a big stunt), a player must:
- Declare it as a **called shot** before rolling, or
- Propose it *after* rolling **Double Sweet**

If the overreach succeeds, the player pays a **Hearts cost**—but only if the action goes beyond normal and actually works.

## Clarifying Examples

### Normal Actions (no Hearts cost)
- Disarming or tripping the target you’re attacking
- Jumping across a small gap
- Snatching an item from someone in reach

### Overreaching Actions (Hearts cost if successful)
- Attacking multiple enemies at once
- Pulling several foes from a tree with a chain
- Leaping across a collapsing bridge while grabbing someone else

These overreaching moves must be either called shots *before* rolling, or proposed *after* rolling **Double Sweet**.

## Called Shot Outcomes

### A focused effect (disarm, trip, distract)
- Sweet: Clean, stylish success.
- Double Sweet: Bonus or extra effect.
- Spicy: Twist—caught, exposed, or off balance.

### A broad effect (multi-target, area move)
- Sweet: Only main target affected.
- Double Sweet: All targets hit with flair.
- Spicy: Backfires—exposed or overwhelmed.

### A utility move (jump gap, grab item)
- Sweet: Works neatly.
- Double Sweet: Extra reach or great timing.
- Spicy: Fail—drop, miss, or fall.

*This variant keeps play fast and sharp, while giving big rolls room to shine.*
