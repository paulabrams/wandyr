# Random Hazards - Environmental

*Roll 1d20 for natural and mining-related hazards.*

1. **Bad Air Pocket** - Entering area; CON save or unconscious; WIS 12 to detect
2. **Unstable Floor** - Weight/movement; DEX save or fall 1d6x5 feet; Investigation 13
3. **Icicle Fall** - Noise/vibration; 2d6 piercing damage; listen for creaking
4. **Slippery Ice** - Walking on ice; DEX save or fall prone; go slow, use spikes
5. **Cave-in** - Structural damage; 4d6 crushing, blocks passage; STR 15 to dig out
6. **Freezing Draft** - Air current; CON save or cold damage; thick clothing helps
7. **Flooded Tunnel** - Water flow; drowning risk, STR to swim; check for water sounds
8. **Toxic Minerals** - Touching/breathing; poison damage over time; hold breath, wash hands
9. **Unstable Shaft** - Climbing/rope use; DEX save or equipment fails; inspect before use
10. **Echo Chamber** - Speaking loudly; noise travels, alerts monsters; whisper only
11. **Magnetic Stone** - Metal equipment; metal items pulled/stick; use non-metal tools
12. **Temperature Shock** - Room transition; CON save or fatigue; gradual adjustment
13. **Gas Pocket** - Disturbing ground; flammable/poisonous gas release; test with torch
14. **Rock Slide** - Loud noise/vibration; falling stones cause 3d6 damage; move quietly
15. **Underground River** - Breaking through wall; sudden water flow; check for moisture
16. **Crystal Growth** - Sharp mineral formations; 1d4 piercing damage; careful movement
17. **Pressure Plate** - Stepping on stone; triggers various effects; look for seams
18. **Acid Pool** - Falling into liquid; 2d6 acid damage per round; test with metal
19. **Radiation Zone** - Prolonged exposure; CON save or illness; use protective gear
20. **Void Chamber** - Entering space; gravity shifts, items float; secure everything

## Environmental Conditions

- **Darkness** - Torches/lanterns essential
- **Cold** - Risk of frostbite in deep areas  
- **Altitude** - Thin air in high chambers
- **Dampness** - Equipment rust, disease risk
- **Silence** - Sound carries unusually far
