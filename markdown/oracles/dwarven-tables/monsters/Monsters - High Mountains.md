# Random Encounters - High Mountains

*Roll 1d20. Check every 6 hours travel, encounter on 6.*

1. **Abominable Snowman** (1) - HD 6, AC 6; barghest of the Matterhorn, territorial
2. **Drägä (Ice Dragon)** (1) - HD 9*, AC 2; ancient glacier dweller, distant signs only
3. **Roc** (1) - HD 12, AC 4; massive bird, circles high peaks
4. **Fire Giant Patrol** (1d2+1) - HD 11+3, AC 4; scouts from deeper mountain strongholds
5. **Frost Salamanders** (1d3) - HD 8, AC 3; cold-breathing lizards, ice attacks
6. **White Dragon Wyrmling** (1) - HD 4*, AC 4; young but cunning ice predator
7. **Yeti Pack** (1d4) - HD 3, AC 6; savage mountain dwellers, fear fire
8. **Mountain Storm Giant** (1) - HD 15, AC 2; commands weather, hurls lightning
9. **Ancient Mountain Spirit** (1) - HD 6*, AC 3; tests mortals who climb sacred peaks
10. **Crystal Drakes** (1d2) - HD 5*, AC 4; gem-scaled dragons, breath prismatic light
11. **Ice Troll** (1) - HD 8, AC 4; regenerates in cold, immune to fire
12. **Mountain Roc** (1) - HD 10, AC 3; smaller than roc, more aggressive
13. **Frost Giant Scout** (1) - HD 8, AC 4; lone giant, hunting for food
14. **Snow Leopard** (1d2) - HD 4, AC 5; stealthy mountain predators
15. **Avalanche Elemental** (1) - HD 6*, AC 2; living snowstorm, causes avalanches
16. **Mountain Chimera** (1) - HD 9, AC 4; goat-lion-snake, breathes cold
17. **Ice Golem** (1) - HD 7, AC 3; animated ice construct, immune to cold
18. **Mountain Basilisk** (1) - HD 6, AC 3; stone-gazing lizard, petrifies victims
19. **Frost Wraith** (1) - HD 5*, AC 4; undead ice spirit, drains warmth
20. **Mountain Kraken** (1) - HD 12*, AC 1; tentacled horror in mountain lakes

## Extreme Weather (Roll when encounter = 1-4)

- **Avalanche Zone** (30%) - DEX save DC 18 or buried (suffocation)
- **Whiteout Blizzard** (25%) - Cannot see 5 feet, CON saves vs cold
- **Crevasse Field** (25%) - Hidden ice cracks, fall 2d6x10 feet
- **Altitude Sickness** (20%) - CON save or exhausted until descent
