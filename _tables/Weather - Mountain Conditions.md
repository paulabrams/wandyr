# Random Weather - Mountain Conditions

*Roll 1d20 for weather during mountain travel.*

1. **Clear Skies** - Perfect visibility, +2 navigation; 1d6 hours duration
2. **Light Snow** - -1 visibility, tracks visible; 2d4 hours duration
3. **Heavy Fog** - 20ft visibility, easy to get lost; 1d4 hours duration
4. **Mountain Storm** - Rain/snow, -2 all rolls; 1d3 hours duration
5. **Sudden Blizzard** - 5ft visibility, extreme cold; 1d6 hours duration
6. **High Winds** - Difficult speech, flying debris; 2d6 hours duration
7. **Temperature Drop** - Sudden cold, CON saves needed; 1 hour duration
8. **Ice Storm** - Slippery surfaces, 1d3 cold damage; 1d4 hours duration
9. **Avalanche Risk** - Unstable snow, noise = danger; ongoing duration
10. **Aurora Display** - Beautiful lights, supernatural feel; night only
11. **Strange Calm** - Unnatural stillness, ominous; 1d8 hours duration
12. **Starlight Bleed** - Mountain glows faintly, magic active; night only
13. **Thunder Echoes** - Distant rumbling, sound carries far; 1d4 hours duration
14. **Frost Mist** - Freezing fog, visibility 10ft; 2d6 hours duration
15. **Hail Storm** - Pelting ice, 1d4 bludgeoning damage; 1d3 hours duration
16. **Wind Shear** - Sudden gusts, flying creatures grounded; 1d6 hours duration
17. **Rain Shadow** - Dry area, no precipitation; 1d8 hours duration
18. **Mountain Breath** - Warm updrafts, updraft currents; 2d4 hours duration
19. **Crystal Rain** - Falling ice crystals, magical properties; 1d4 hours duration
20. **Vault Weather** - Reality distortion, impossible conditions; variable duration

## Seasonal Modifiers

- **Spring** - Add +2 to fog and storm rolls
- **Summer** - Subtract -2 from snow/ice rolls  
- **Autumn** - Add +1 to wind and temperature drop
- **Winter** - Add +3 to all cold/snow effects

## Survival Requirements

- **Shelter** needed during storms
- **Warm clothing** prevents cold damage
- **Navigation tools** essential in poor visibility
- **Food/water** consumption doubles in extreme cold
