# Thaumaturgy (Control) Spells

*Physical laws, mechanisms, and force. The engineering magic of the Dwur, written in foundational runes that command reality. Practitioners control objects, manipulate energy, bind powers, and impose their will upon matter.*

**3.1.1** *Adhere* - Make sticky: Create a powerful adhesive bond between two objects. **Instant** Create a quick-setting bond that supports up to 25 pounds and breaks easily with deliberate effort, useful for temporary holds or emergency repairs on objects you touch. **Active** Maintain a powerful bond supporting up to 100 pounds that requires significant force to break, perfect for securing climbing routes or permanent repairs throughout the encounter. The spell works on most materials except liquids or gases. **Level** bond strength and weight capacity.
**3.1.2** *Animate Object* - Objects obey: Imbue one small object with limited mobility and purpose. **Instant** Make the object perform one simple task like opening a door, picking up an item, or making a single movement before becoming inert again. **Active** Grant continuous animation allowing the object to follow verbal commands throughout the encounter, performing tasks like sweeping, carrying items, or operating simple mechanisms at walking speed. Cannot attack or perform complex actions. **Level** object size and movement speed.
**3.1.3** *Astral Prison* - Crystal stasis: Encase one willing creature or object in a protective crystal shell. **Instant** Create a brief crystal barrier that blocks the next incoming attack or harmful effect, then shatters. The crystal provides immediate protection against one threat. **Active** Maintain complete temporal stasis where the target is immune to all damage, aging, disease, and magical effects while remaining aware but unable to act throughout the encounter. The crystal can be broken by dealing 10 points of damage from outside. **Level** crystal durability and target size.
**3.1.4** *Command* - Force obedience: Speak a single word that compels one creature within 30 feet to obey. **Instant** Force immediate compliance with one simple command like "stop," "drop," or "kneel" that must be completed this turn. **Active** Maintain ongoing compulsion where the target continues following the command until you issue a new one or they complete a complex task like "guard this door" or "follow me" throughout the encounter. Commands causing obvious harm are resisted. **Level** number of targets and command complexity.
**3.1.5** *Elasticity* - Control form: Grant one object the properties of rubber or clay. **Instant** Briefly make the object flexible enough for one specific use like squeezing through a gap, cushioning a single fall, or bending around an obstacle before returning to normal. **Active** Maintain continuous elasticity allowing the object to stretch up to twice its length or compress to half size repeatedly throughout the encounter, perfect for creating adaptive tools or navigating changing terrain. **Level** flexibility range and object size.
**3.1.6** *Filch* - Teleport items: Transport small objects directly to your hand. **Instant** Teleport one unattended object weighing less than 5 pounds from within 30 feet. Must be clearly visible and unsecured. **Active** Allow continuous retrieval of multiple objects throughout the encounter, can affect loosely held items, and extends range to 60 feet. Each teleportation is silent and subtle, creating brief flashes of light. **Level** object weight and range.

**3.2.1** *Force Push* - Directional power: Project invisible force in a 15-foot cone. **Instant** Create a single powerful burst that pushes targets 10 feet away, dealing 1d6 bludgeoning damage and potentially knocking them prone against obstacles. **Active** Maintain a continuous pressure wave that pushes creatures 5 feet each turn they remain in the area, knocks over objects, extinguishes flames, and creates an ongoing barrier of force throughout the encounter. **Level** force strength and cone size.
**3.2.2** *Haste* - Control speed: Accelerate one willing creature's movements. **Instant** Grant one burst of speed allowing double movement and one extra action this turn only, with no fatigue afterward. Enhanced speed affects all physical activities but not mental processes. **Active** Maintain continuous acceleration doubling movement speed and granting an extra action each turn throughout the encounter, but causes fatigue when the effect ends. The sustained speed enhancement persists until concentration breaks. **Level** acceleration becomes more pronounced and sustainable, can affect additional targets, temporal control improves.
**3.2.3** *Hover* - Control levitation: Lift yourself or one willing creature up to 3 feet off the ground. **Instant** Provide brief levitation for one specific purpose like avoiding a trap, crossing a small gap, or negating fall damage from a 10-foot drop. **Active** Maintain stable hovering throughout the encounter, allowing horizontal movement at normal speed over difficult terrain and obstacles while remaining 3 feet aloft. The levitation persists with concentration. **Level** number of targets and height.
**3.2.4** *Invisible Tether* - Bind objects: Create an invisible force connection between two objects within 60 feet. **Instant** Form a temporary tether that lasts only until one object moves more than 30 feet from the other, useful for quick connections or emergency links. **Active** Maintain a persistent tether supporting up to 100 pounds of tension throughout the encounter, where moving one object pulls the other along unless anchored. Becomes visible as a silver line when stressed. **Level** force connections become stronger and more versatile, can maintain multiple tethers, binding precision improves.
**3.2.5** *Knock* - Open locks: Force open locked mechanisms using controlled magical pressure. **Instant** Attempt to open one simple lock immediately with a loud clicking sound audible up to 100 feet away. May fail against complex or magical locks. **Active** Apply continuous pressure that can overcome reinforced locks, magical wards, and complex mechanisms throughout the encounter, working through multiple layers of security while maintaining the grinding noise. **Level** lock complexity and noise control.
**3.2.6** *Leap* - Control jumping: Enhance one creature's jumping ability. **Instant** Provide one enhanced jump with triple normal distance and height (up to 30 feet horizontal or 15 feet vertical), propelled by a burst of magical energy. **Active** Maintain continuous enhancement allowing multiple enhanced jumps throughout the encounter, perfect for battlefield mobility or scaling obstacles with repeated magical propulsion. The enhancement persists for the entire scene. **Level** jumping enhancement becomes more powerful and graceful, can affect multiple targets, gravitational manipulation improves.

**3.3.1** *Lock* - Seal door: Magically secure one door, window, chest, or similar closure. **Instant** Create a basic magical lock equivalent to a simple mechanism that lasts until opened or forced. **Active** Maintain a powerful seal equivalent to a masterwork lock that resists picking and force throughout the encounter, manifesting as glowing runes that dim but flare when threatened. Only designated creatures can open it normally. **Level** lock strength and number of portals.
**3.3.2** *Magic Suppressor* - Null magic: Create a 10-foot radius zone where magical effects are dampened. **Instant** Create a brief suppression pulse that weakens one ongoing magical effect in the area, reducing its effectiveness for a few moments. **Active** Maintain a continuous suppression field that reduces spell duration by half and diminishes effectiveness of all magic within throughout the encounter. The shimmer moves with you as center point. **Level** suppression field becomes more powerful and far-reaching, magic dampening intensifies, anti-magic precision improves.
**3.3.3** *Masterscript* - Write command runes: Inscribe runic commands on any surface that trigger when conditions are met. **Instant** Create simple trigger runes that activate once when touched or approached, producing basic effects like light, sound, or a brief message before fading. **Active** Inscribe persistent runes with complex trigger conditions that can activate multiple times and produce varied effects like alarms, barriers, or detailed messages throughout the encounter. The runes remain active for the entire scene. **Level** rune complexity and number of triggers.
**3.3.4** *Shrink Item* - Reduce size: Reduce one non-living object to one-quarter its normal size. **Instant** Briefly shrink the object for immediate concealment or transport, returning to normal size once used or after a few minutes. **Active** Maintain the size reduction throughout the encounter, allowing continued carrying and concealment. The object retains original weight but fits in small spaces and cannot function until restored. **Level** size reduction becomes more dramatic and stable, can affect multiple objects, dimensional compression improves.
**3.3.5** *Sort* - Organize items: Automatically arrange loose objects within a 15-foot radius according to your mental criteria. **Instant** Quickly sort a few specific items you're looking for to the top of piles or into your reach, useful for finding something immediately. **Active** Methodically organize all unattended objects under 10 pounds by size, color, material, function, or value into neat arrangements throughout the encounter. The organization persists as you maintain focus. **Level** organization becomes more sophisticated and far-reaching, can handle heavier objects, sorting precision improves.
**3.3.6** *Spider Climb* - Control climbing: Grant yourself or one willing creature the ability to walk on walls and ceilings. **Instant** Provide sticky grip for one specific climbing action like scaling a wall, crossing a ceiling, or repositioning to an advantageous surface before adhesion fades. **Active** Maintain continuous wall-climbing ability allowing normal movement in any orientation throughout the encounter, perfect for combat positioning and exploration. The adhesion persists as long as you concentrate. **Level** number of targets and surface adhesion.

**3.4.1** *Strengthen* - Reinforce material: Double the structural integrity and hardness of one object. **Instant** Briefly reinforce the object for one specific use, like blocking a single powerful attack or supporting extra weight for one action. **Active** Maintain continuous reinforcement with double structural integrity and resistance to physical damage throughout the encounter. Weapons gain +1d6 damage while the metallic sheen persists. **Level** reinforcement strength and number of objects.
**3.4.2** *Teleport* - Move objects: Transport objects weighing up to 20 pounds within 100 feet. **Instant** Teleport one object immediately to a clearly visualized destination with brief light flashes at both points. **Active** Allow continuous teleportation of multiple objects throughout the encounter, useful for repositioning equipment, tools, or supplies rapidly across the battlefield or workspace. **Level** teleportation becomes more efficient and far-reaching, can transport heavier objects, dimensional precision improves.
**3.4.3** *Time Slip* - Control time: Accelerate your perception of time relative to others. **Instant** Grant one extra action immediately while everyone else appears briefly frozen, allowing quick repositioning or a crucial action. **Active** Maintain slightly accelerated time throughout the encounter, granting an extra minor action each turn like drawing weapons, opening doors, or activating devices while others act normally. **Level** time acceleration and number of actions.
**3.4.4** *Unravel* - Counter spells: Disrupt magical effects within 60 feet by unweaving their foundational energies. **Instant** Attempt to dispel one ongoing magical effect, automatically ending equal or lower level magic and having a chance against more powerful spells. **Active** Maintain continuous dispelling pressure that weakens multiple magical effects in the area and provides resistance to new magical attacks targeting you or nearby allies throughout the encounter. **Level** dispelling becomes more potent and far-reaching, can unravel stronger magic, counter-magic precision improves.
**3.4.5** *Augment Object* - Enhance item: Improve one tool, weapon, or piece of equipment with magical properties. **Instant** Provide a brief enhancement for one specific use, like advantage on one skill check or +2 to one attack roll. **Active** Maintain continuous enhancement throughout the encounter - weapons gain +1 to attack and damage, armor provides extra protection, tools grant advantage on relevant checks. Glowing lines trace the object's features. **Level** enhancement strength and number of objects.
**3.4.6** *Control Device* - Command mechanisms: Take direct control of one mechanical device within 30 feet through magical command. **Instant** Remotely operate the device for one specific action like pulling a lever, opening a valve, or activating a mechanism. **Active** Maintain continuous remote control throughout the encounter, allowing complex operation of machinery, clockwork, or mechanical systems with perfect skill and timing as if physically present. **Level** mechanical control becomes more sophisticated and far-reaching, can command complex devices, precision operation improves.

**3.5.1** *Animate Tool* - Give purpose: Imbue one tool or simple weapon with autonomous purpose. **Instant** Make the tool perform one specific task like driving a nail, sweeping an area, or digging a hole before becoming inert again. **Active** Maintain continuous animation allowing the tool to work independently throughout the encounter, performing repetitive tasks at laborer speed while following your commands and adapting to changing instructions. **Level** number of tools and work efficiency.
**3.5.2** *Binding Circle* - Contain force: Create a circular barrier of magical force with a 10-foot radius. **Instant** Form a temporary barrier that lasts until someone breaks through it (15 hit points) or you move away, useful for blocking immediate threats. **Active** Maintain a persistent translucent wall (25 hit points) that regenerates damage and prevents passage while allowing sound and light through throughout the encounter, perfect for controlling battlefield positioning. **Level** barrier strength and radius.
**3.5.3** *Density Control* - Alter mass: Change the density of one object, making it half or twice as heavy while maintaining size and shape. **Instant** Briefly alter density for one specific purpose like making a thrown weapon heavier for impact or an object lighter for easier manipulation. **Active** Maintain continuous density control throughout the encounter, allowing tactical switching between light and heavy states for transport, combat, or environmental interaction. **Level** density multiplier and number of objects.
**3.5.4** *Enhance Power* - Boost magic: Amplify magical effects with geometric energy patterns around your hands. **Instant** Enhance the very next spell you cast by 50% effectiveness - more damage, longer duration, or larger area by 5 feet. **Active** Maintain amplification throughout the encounter, boosting all spells you cast but preventing use of other enhancement magic while active. The energy patterns persist for the entire scene. **Level** amplification strength and number of spells.
**3.5.5** *Mend* - Repair objects: Restore broken objects with golden threads of energy weaving pieces together. **Instant** Quickly repair minor damage like torn clothing, small cracks, or simple breaks, sufficient for immediate use. **Active** Methodically restore complex damage, broken mechanisms, or multiple objects throughout the encounter, handling extensive repairs that would normally require skilled craftsmanship. **Level** repair complexity and number of objects.
**3.5.6** *Object Memory* - Read item history: Touch objects to perceive psychic impressions of their past. **Instant** Briefly glimpse the most recent significant event the object experienced, providing quick flashes of who last handled it or dramatic moments it witnessed. **Active** Maintain extended reading throughout the encounter, revealing detailed history with sensory overlays showing multiple events, emotions, and interactions over recent time. **Level** memory depth and number of objects.

**3.6.1** *Reality Anchor* - Stabilize space: Establish a 20-foot radius zone where physical laws become rigid and unchangeable. **Instant** Create a brief stabilization that prevents one specific reality-altering effect like teleportation or shapechanging from occurring in the area. **Active** Maintain continuous reality anchor with geometric boundary patterns, suppressing all teleportation, shapechanging, and dimensional effects while making the air feel more solid and stable throughout the encounter. **Level** anchor radius and stability strength.
**3.6.2** *Rune Lock* - Seal with power: Inscribe a master rune that seals doors, containers, or portals with magical force. **Instant** Create a basic runic seal that requires the correct command word or designated key object to open, lasting until opened or forced. **Active** Maintain a powerful master rune that glows steadily and pulses when threatened, dealing 1d6 damage to intruders and resisting dispelling attempts throughout the encounter. **Level** runic sealing becomes more powerful and sophisticated, can maintain multiple seals, magical security intensifies.
**3.6.3** *Shape Material* - Change form: Reshape up to 20 cubic feet of stone, metal, wood, or other solid material into any form you can imagine. **Instant** Quickly form simple shapes like basic tools, barriers, or repairs with rough detail, completing the transformation immediately. **Active** Allow detailed sculpting throughout the encounter with incredible precision, creating complex tools, art objects, or architectural features while maintaining concentration. **Level** material manipulation becomes more refined and extensive, can shape denser substances, precision crafting improves.
**3.6.4** *Magic Circle* - Create workspace: Establish a 15-foot radius ritual circle with intricate runic patterns that glow and pulse with magical activity. **Instant** Create a temporary circle that enhances the next spell cast within it, providing advantage or stabilizing the effect against dispelling. **Active** Maintain a persistent workspace throughout the encounter where all spells require less effort, gain resistance to dispelling, and magical research or crafting receives significant bonuses. **Level** ritual circle becomes more potent and far-reaching, magical enhancement intensifies, runic complexity improves.
**3.6.5** *Thaumaturgic Seal* - Master rune: Place a runic seal on any surface that triggers protective effects when disturbed. **Instant** Create a simple trigger seal that activates once when approached within 10 feet, producing a basic effect like an alarm or warning before fading. **Active** Establish a persistent master rune with complex trigger conditions that can activate multiple times throughout the encounter, creating barriers, alarms, or messages when specific circumstances are met. **Level** seal complexity and trigger sensitivity.
**3.6.6** *Perfect Control* - Complete mastery: Achieve absolute command over all thaumaturgic forces within a 30-foot radius. **Instant** Briefly command all objects in the area for one coordinated action, like simultaneously operating multiple devices or repositioning dozens of items at once. **Active** Maintain complete mastery throughout the encounter, allowing continuous manipulation of multiple objects, machinery, and materials with perfect precision as invisible hands respond to your will - the pinnacle of Dwur engineering magic. **Level** control radius and precision mastery.
