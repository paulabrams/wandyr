# Morale: Music and Comforts

**When you muster with a music instrument or comforts in item slots, you give your party Morale (morale Hearts)**

1 Morale heart per slot of music instrument (requires a music skill)

1 Morale heart per slot of comforts (no skill required, but is consumed during the muster unless you have a cooking skill)

Slots remain occupied during muster or needs to be repaid with character hearts.

## Songs as Names

Keep Morale (insight) once per song per muster even when out of morale
