# Wandyr Traits

## New Characters

Characters start with four Traits. Each trait must be a single word. Traits influence starting inventory.

Example:
*Lindon: brave, strong, poleax, armor*

Traits are used for Insight in two ways:

1. **TRAIT** - Use a helpful Trait. Strong helps open stuck doors or crack heads.
2. **MASTERY** - Use a helpful Trait if one has Mastery (see below)

## Mastery

Beginner characters are novices, no more accomplished than normal people.

Traits have Ranks, starting at 1. So new characters have four Traits each at Level 1.

Insight MASTERY grants an Advantage if the character is better than the opponent or situation.

When fighting, the Trait Level must *beat* the Hit Dice of the monster. So to novice characters, 1 HD skeletons are scary.

## Gaining Mastery

Characters can spend XP to increase Trait levels.

Example after Lindon has played for awhile:
*Lindon: brave 3, strong 3, poleax 2, armor 2*

The XP cost is current Rank plus d6. So improving a level 1 Trait is d6+1, and improving a level 2 Trait is d6+2, and so on.

The player can roll the XP cost before spending XP, but that remains locked for that Trait level. Not all paths are easily followed.

### Approaches

Fate Accelerated utilizes a system of Approaches (Careful, Clever, Flashy, Forceful, Quick, Sneaky) to define *how* a character attempts actions, rather than listing specific skills. These Approaches are rated numerically and added to dice rolls when a character takes an action (Overcome, Create Advantage, Attack, Defend). Aspects, which are descriptive phrases about the character, further define their capabilities and challenges. This system demonstrates a successful model for characterization and task resolution without traditional stats or skills.

**Cons:** ambiguous when someone is doing it? seems like it would force people to use specific wording? am I going to tell the player they didn't qualify?

### Option: groups

Each Trait is a keyword within a category of Body, Mind, or Spirit.

- Body: strong, longsword, shield
- Mind: tactician
- Spirit: brave

**Pros:** gets people to think about their physicality vs inner character
**Cons:** not always clear which category
