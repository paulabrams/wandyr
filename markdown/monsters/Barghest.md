# Barghest

The Barghest of the matterhorn is found in high mountain passes and ice caves. Territorial and aggressive. Uses breath to create low-visibility conditions before attacking. Nine feet tall, with a thick, shaggy coat of white fur, sharp claws and teeth.

Level 4; Heart 36; Move 40'; Attacks 2 claws + 1 bite (deadly); SA blinding breath, snow surprise; SD cold immunity

- An exhale of the barghest's foetid blinding breath can create low-visibility conditions, such as extinguishing torches or creating blizzard whiteout conditions.
- Surprises on 1-5 in d6 in snowy conditions.
- Immune to cold.
