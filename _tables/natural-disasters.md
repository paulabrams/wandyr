# Natural Disasters

Roll 2d6 for natural disasters that reshape the landscape:

**1.1** Earthquake - Ground splits, revealing ancient ruins  
**1.2** Volcanic Eruption - Lava flows create new terrain  
**1.3** Tsunami - Coastal settlements destroyed  
**1.4** Hurricane - Winds reshape the landscape  
**1.5** Drought - Water sources dry up  
**1.6** Flood - Rivers overflow their banks

**2.1** Landslide - Mountains shift, revealing treasures  
**2.2** Avalanche - Snow buries mountain passes  
**2.3** Forest Fire - Ancient groves burn to ash  
**2.4** Sandstorm - Desert expands rapidly  
**2.5** Ice Storm - Frozen wasteland created  
**2.6** Lightning Storm - Fires spread across plains

**3.1** Sinkhole - Ground collapses into caverns  
**3.2** Rockfall - Cliffs crumble, blocking paths  
**3.3** Mudslide - Hills become impassable  
**3.4** Hailstorm - Crops destroyed, animals injured  
**3.5** Tornado - Buildings and trees uprooted  
**3.6** Blizzard - Snow isolates communities

**4.1** Geyser Eruption - Hot springs create new features  
**4.2** Cave Collapse - Underground passages sealed  
**4.3** River Change - Waterways find new courses  
**4.4** Coast Erosion - Beaches disappear  
**4.5** Glacier Movement - Ice reshapes mountains  
**4.6** Coral Bleaching - Ocean life dies

**5.1** Meteor Impact - Crater creates new landmark  
**5.2** Gas Leak - Toxic fumes poison area  
**5.3** Acid Rain - Vegetation withers  
**5.4** Dust Storm - Visibility reduced to zero  
**5.5** Heat Wave - Crops fail, animals suffer  
**5.6** Cold Snap - Unseasonable frost kills plants

**6.1** Magical Storm - Reality warps in area  
**6.2** Time Rift - Area ages rapidly  
**6.3** Dimensional Tear - Otherworldly creatures emerge  
**6.4** Life Surge - Area becomes overgrown  
**6.5** Death Touch - Area becomes barren  
**6.6** Balance Restored - Area returns to natural state
