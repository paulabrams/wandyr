# Random Tricks - Illusions & Puzzles

*Roll 1d20 for non-harmful but confusing challenges.*

1. **False Treasure** - Illusory coins/gems vanish when touched; magic detection reveals truth
2. **Mirror Maze** - Reflective surfaces create confusing paths; follow wall with hand
3. **Echoing Voices** - Sounds of Dwur workers from empty rooms; investigation shows no source
4. **Shifting Walls** - Passages seem to move/change; map carefully, illusion only
5. **Gravity Well** - Items fall upward in certain areas; secure everything before entering
6. **Time Distortion** - Clocks/timekeeping becomes unreliable; use biological rhythms
7. **False Doors** - Solid stone painted to look like exits; physical examination reveals truth
8. **Riddle Lock** - Door asks questions in Old Dwur; knowledge of Dwur culture helps
9. **Phantom Light** - Dancing lights lead explorers astray; follow fixed landmarks instead
10. **Memory Gap** - Brief confusion about recent events; compare notes with companions
11. **Sound Maze** - Audio illusions create false directions; use visual landmarks
12. **Color Confusion** - Hues shift and change unexpectedly; trust shape over color
13. **Size Distortion** - Objects appear larger/smaller than reality; measure with tools
14. **Perspective Trap** - Distance and depth become unreliable; use rope to measure
15. **Shadow Play** - Moving shadows suggest danger; light reveals empty space
16. **Temperature Trick** - Areas feel hot/cold without reason; ignore thermal sensations
17. **Smell Illusion** - Aromas suggest nearby food/danger; focus on visual evidence
18. **Touch Deception** - Surfaces feel different than they appear; trust sight over touch
19. **Balance Distortion** - Gravity seems to shift direction; use level tools
20. **Reality Echo** - Actions seem to repeat moments later; maintain clear timeline

## Puzzle Types

- **Mechanical** - Gears, levers, counterweights
- **Musical** - Play correct sequence of notes
- **Mathematical** - Dwur number systems
- **Astronomical** - Star chart alignment
- **Linguistic** - Old Dwur riddles/wordplay
