# Wåndyr 1.5 — Must-Haves

*The non-negotiables for Wåndyr 1.5. Wåndyr has the right bones; this is what "perfecting" it means. Source ideas linked per item.*

**Unifying principle — make the implicit explicit.** Every must-have below is the same move applied to a different subsystem: replace GM fiat and hidden state with transparent, player-legible rules. If a change makes a hidden ruling visible, it's on-mission; if it adds fidelity for its own sake, it isn't. (See [[Alignment as Insight]] design tenets: *"Difficulty is Transparent."*)

## Must-haves

- **Alignment / Approaches as the leveled stat.** Proficiency = count of relevant advancements ÷ 3. Use the four **Approaches** (Sword / Craft / Heart / Arcana) as the presentation, driven by the table question *"How do you approach this?"*
  - *The pivot:* this is the defining break from both predecessors. Legends modified rolls with the **Legend Die (TEACUP** — Theme/Effort/Asset/Combo/Ult/Prep**)**; Wåndyr with the **Insight die (TRAIT/SKILL/ITEM/BYNAME/FEAT/EFFORT)**. The same idea twice — a rotating per-roll d6 you checked every throw to ask *"does one of these six categories apply right now?"* That ephemeral, GM-adjudicated, looked-up-every-roll state is replaced by **one standing number on your sheet.** The old die-faces don't vanish, they're absorbed: Trait, Skill, Byname, and Feat become the advancements you *count* into the level; Effort survives in Sweet & Spicy pricing; Item adjusts the Difficulty.
  - *Why:* it's the core character-power axis, it lives on the sheet (no per-roll lookup), and Approaches is the most Hobbit framing (Craft-primary, Sword a last resort). Approaches doesn't replace Alignment — it *is* the four-bucket version of it. See [[Wandyr Approaches]], [[Alignment as Insight]].

- **Defined spell effects.** Every spell has a fixed, stated effect — *two-word title + two-word subtitle* — utility/control, not scalable damage. Scope scales globally with Arcana level (Knave's "INT" = your Arcana level); casting costs Hearts. — *Why:* fixed effects + no scalable damage stop players grinding spells to nerf boss monsters, and the two-word constraint filters out the fiddly, optimizable spells. The subtitle must *define* the effect, not just flavor it. See [[Knave Spellbook]], [[Wåndyr 1.5 — Unified Resolution (Experiment)]].

- **Facts — a free knowledge layer.** A list of known truths (about the character and the world) kept independently of the advancement system. They cost nothing and grant no dice. — *Why:* they fuel Plan-A cleverness and give the Guide hooks; the old **Dark Secret** becomes an optional Fact (fixing why nobody took it), and the Shadow is just your 0-level Approach. The line: knowledge is a Fact (free); capability is a Skill or Edge (earned).

- **Edges as asterisks.** Any special ability is an asterisk (footnote) on an existing Trait, Skill, or Asset — never its own slot. Grades: **Knack** (mundane; free / Advantage on the repeat) and **Secret** (potent or magical; costs Hearts to use). The Asset **Signature** is just an Edge on an item. — *Why:* keeps the sheet lean and ties every ability to something already on it. See [[Wåndyr 1.5 — Unified Resolution (Experiment)]].

- **High Concept = pure roleplay, zero crunch.** One line stating who the character is. It's a Fact: it never adds to a die. — *Why:* it earns *skipped rolls* (Plan A), which beat any bonus, and keeping it crunchless preserves the gap between who you say you are and how you actually roll. "Who you are" never rolls; "how you handle this" (Approach) does.

- **Traits vs Skills — keep them split.** **Traits** = how you play (adjectives: fierce, sneaky). **Skills** = what you know (classable nouns: greatsword, lockpicks, a magic school). — *Why:* the core lesson from Legends, whose single broad "Traits" slot tried to be qualities + skills at once and became too broad to mean anything.

- **Plan A is free cleverness; the dice are Plan B.** No roll without a named cost. When a clever, well-described action removes the uncertainty, it just works — no roll. — *Why:* the Hobbit ethos. Cleverness is the toll-free road; combat is a last resort.

- **One-minute combat round.** One action + free, unlimited defense, resolved as the *shape of the minute* — narrated, not simulated. — *Why:* it's the right clock for an adventure; blow-by-blow turns combat into a fencing manual.

- **Tactics-crunch, not fidelity-crunch.** A few tactical levers at the one-minute scale — position, range, swarms combining, chokepoints capping them — declared in the fiction (*describe the maneuver → it shifts the numbers*). No HEMA simulation (no binds, feints, hit locations). — *Why:* depth of *choice*, not depth of *physics*. Keep the levers on the danger side (they reward ending, avoiding, or surviving a fight); the moment combat becomes a playground you want to dive into, it has stopped being Wåndyr. See [[Wåndyr 1.5 — Unified Resolution (Experiment)]].

- **Advancement from play, not accounting.** Claim an advancement on double Sweet, tied to the fiction of the roll — replacing XP bookkeeping and GM deed-valuation. — *Why:* growth emerges from play and needs no GM judgment; the transparency move applied to advancement. See [[Character Advancement]].

- **Slot-based inventory.** Item slots (10 + Baggage) rather than freeform gear. — *Why:* a Wåndyr lesson worth keeping; makes encumbrance and the cost of carrying things visible.

## Strong nice-to-haves

- **One character sheet, both formats.** Express the sheet in the structured Legends 7-part layout so a character is portable between Wåndyr (light) and a future Warband (tactical). The slots map cleanly; only the resolution engine differs.
- **Aspects.** A Muster-time declaration (a Trait + Skill assigned to an Approach) that grants narration rights on doubles. See [[Aspects]].
- **Card-based Muster.** A suit per Approach (Spades/Clubs/Hearts/Diamonds) laid face-up to show your lead Approach at a glance. See [[Wandyr Approaches]].

## Out of scope (deliberately)

- **Crunchy combat that *invites* fighting** — that's Warband, a different game with the opposite combat stance. Wåndyr keeps combat thin and dangerous.
- **HEMA-style combat fidelity** — the road not to take twice.
