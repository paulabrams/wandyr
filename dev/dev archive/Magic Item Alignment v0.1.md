# Magic Item Alignment

In Wandyr, your alignment to a magic item determeins how well you can wield its power. Each magic item has an alignment rating from up to 6, representing the depth of your connection to the item's magic.

## Alignment

When you find a magic item, you will need to determine your Alignment with it based on the description of the item. For example, an evil sword with red runes that react to blood will respond better to a character with a dark or violent nature, skills in the sword, Necromantia, etc.

## Powers by Alignment Level

The item will have powers corresponding to Alignment levels. You can only access powers up to your current Alignment level with the item. For example, if you have Alignment 2 with a magic sword, you can use its Level 1 and Level 2 powers, but not Level 3 or higher.

Your understanding of powers is also based on Alignment. Anything above your level is either hidden from you or kept as a mystery until you reach that level. For example, if the Guide mentions a level 3 power, but you are only Alignment 2, you may not know what that power does or how to activate it, and the Guide is allowed to change the power at any time until you unlock it. In other words, you may not know what you don't know.

## Magical Writing

Magic items have writing on them corresponding to one of the magic schools. You can only read the writing if you have skill in that magic school, and your ability to understand how that defines powers of the item is based on your Alignment with the item.

## Stormcaller

*The Lightning Blade (longsword +1, item slots 3)*

A longsword forged from blue-tinted steel that crackles with static electricity. The blade bears swirling patterns that look like storm clouds, and when drawn, the air around it hums with energy. The hilt is wrapped in leather that never gets wet, and the pommel is a polished piece of amber that glows faintly during thunderstorms.

**Magical Writing:** Elementale - burning runes carved in stone along the blade's fuller. Those with Elementale Skill can read these dragon magic inscriptions.

**Alignment Factors:** Elementale Skill, storm-related Traits (fiery, energetic, wild), fighting in storms or during lightning, heroic deeds in battle.

### Powers

1. **Static Charge** (Alignment 1): On a hit, ask the Oracle if the blade delivers a jolt. On Yes, deal +1 damage. No Hearts cost.

2. **Lightning Strike** (Alignment 2): As an Action, pay 2 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you channel lightning through the blade. On Yes, deal an additional d6 Hearts damage to your target. Works similar to *Lightning Strike* (Elementale Level 3). Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 2.

3. **Storm Shield** (Alignment 3): As an Action, pay 3 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you create a barrier of wind and lightning around yourself. On Yes, gain 1 Iron Heart against ranged attacks for the remainder of combat. Works similar to *Wind Shield* (Elementale Level 6). Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 3.

4. **Storm Form** (Alignment 4): As an Action, pay 4 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you become partially insubstantial like mist and lightning. On Yes, you may pass through small gaps or become harder to hit for 1 Round. Works similar to *Mist Form* (Elementale Level 3). Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 4.

5. **Storm Mastery** (Alignment 5): During a thunderstorm, ask the Oracle if you can command the weather. On Yes, you may redirect lightning, calm winds, or intensify the storm. Pay 5 Hearts to activate. Works similar to *Control Weather* (Elementale Level 1) but more powerful. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 5.

6. **Perfect Storm** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need during a storm, ask the Oracle if the blade channels the full power of the tempest. On Yes, you may unleash devastating lightning or create a localized storm. Pay 6 Hearts to activate. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 6.

## The Verdant Staff

*Oakheart (staff +1, item slots 3)*

A staff carved from living wood that still bears green leaves even in winter. The wood is warm to the touch and pulses gently with life energy. Carved runes in Vitae script spiral up the length, and small flowers bloom along its surface when held by someone aligned with nature. The top is shaped like intertwined branches holding a crystal that glows with soft green light.

**Magical Writing:** Vitae - living ink that grows and changes like vines, forming the runes that spiral up the staff. Those with Vitae Skill can read this elven magic script.

**Alignment Factors:** Vitae Skill, nature-related Traits (hallow, dreamy, enduring), connection to animals or plants, peaceful or protective actions.

### Powers

1. **Life Sense** (Alignment 1): As an Action, ask the Oracle if you sense all living creatures within 50 feet. On Yes, you know their general location and vitality. No Hearts cost.

2. **Healing Light** (Alignment 2): As an Action, pay 2 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the staff channels healing energy. On Yes, restore 1d6 Hearts to yourself or an ally. Works similar to *Healing Touch* (Vitae Level 2). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 2.

3. **Animal Bond** (Alignment 3): As an Action, pay 3 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you form a lasting connection with a nearby animal. On Yes, the animal becomes a loyal companion. Works as *Animal Bond* (Vitae Level 1). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 3.

4. **Nature's Bounty** (Alignment 4): As an Action, pay 4 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the staff creates food and water. On Yes, enough provisions for the party for one meal. Works as *Nature's Bounty* (Vitae Level 5). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 4.

5. **Regeneration** (Alignment 5): As an Action, pay 5 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the staff speeds natural healing. On Yes, you or an ally heal 1 Heart per Turn for the next hour. Works as *Regeneration* (Vitae Level 5). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 5.

6. **Perfect Unity** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, ask the Oracle if the staff achieves perfect balance with nature. On Yes, you may restore life, grow forests, or commune with the natural world. Pay 6 Hearts to activate. Works similar to *Perfect Unity* (Vitae Level 6). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 6.

## Aegis of the Ancients

*The Unbreakable Shield (shield +2, item slots 4)*

A massive round shield of bronze and iron, its surface covered in ancient runes that shift and reform when struck. The shield bears the marks of countless battles—scratches, dents, and even a few arrow holes—but these seem to heal over time, leaving the shield pristine. The central boss is a stylized sun that glows faintly when danger approaches.

**Magical Writing:** Thaumaturgy - foundational runes that command reality, inscribed in the metal. The runes shift and reform as they respond to threats. Those with Thaumaturgy Skill can read this dwur engineering magic.

**Alignment Factors:** Shield Skill, protective Traits (steadfast, brave, honorable), defending others, standing firm against overwhelming odds.

### Powers

1. **Danger Sense** (Alignment 1): When danger approaches, ask the Oracle if the shield warns you. On Yes, you sense the threat before it arrives. No Hearts cost.

2. **Radiant Defense** (Alignment 2): As an Action, pay 2 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the shield emits blinding light. On Yes, enemies attacking you must roll with Disadvantage for 1 Round.

3. **Protective Aura** (Alignment 3): As an Action, pay 3 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the shield extends protection to nearby allies. On Yes, allies within 10 feet gain 1 Iron Heart against attacks for the remainder of combat.

4. **Reflect Harm** (Alignment 4): When you successfully block an attack, ask the Oracle if the shield reflects the damage back. On Yes, the attacker takes half the damage they would have dealt. Pay 4 Hearts to activate.

5. **Sanctuary** (Alignment 5): As an Action, pay 5 Hearts and ask the Oracle if the shield creates a zone of protection. On Yes, you and allies within 20 feet are protected from harm for 1 Round. Works similar to *Ward* (Vitae Level 4). Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 5.

6. **Perfect Defense** (Alignment 6): In moments of dire need, ask the Oracle if the shield becomes an impenetrable barrier. On Yes, you may block any attack, spell, or effect. Pay 6 Hearts to activate.

## Belt of the Hero

*Girdle of Might (belt, item slots 1)*

A wide leather belt studded with iron plates and bronze rivets. The buckle is a massive piece of worked metal shaped like a roaring lion. When worn, the belt makes the wearer feel stronger and more confident. The leather is worn smooth from use, suggesting it has been passed down through many heroes.

**Magical Writing:** Thaumaturgy - foundational runes that command reality, etched into the iron plates. Those with Thaumaturgy Skill can read this dwur engineering magic.

**Alignment Factors:** Strength-related Traits (strong, powerful, tough), heroic deeds, leadership, facing great challenges.

### Powers

1. **Might** (Alignment 1): You gain +1 to damage with melee attacks. Passive effect.

2. **Heroic Surge** (Alignment 2): As an Action, pay 2 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you perform a feat of strength. On Yes, you may break objects, lift heavy things, or perform extraordinary physical feats.

3. **Unbreakable Will** (Alignment 3): When you would be affected by fear or mind control, ask the Oracle if the belt protects you. On Yes, you resist the effect. Pay 3 Hearts to activate.

4. **Hero's Stand** (Alignment 4): When you or an ally would be reduced to 0 Hearts, ask the Oracle if the belt grants a second wind. On Yes, restore 1d6 Hearts instead of falling. Pay 4 Hearts to activate. Once per Muster.

5. **Legendary Strength** (Alignment 5): As an Action, pay 5 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you achieve legendary feats of strength. On Yes, you may perform impossible physical acts—bending iron bars, stopping charging beasts, or leaping impossible distances.

6. **Perfect Heroism** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, ask the Oracle if the belt awakens the true hero within. On Yes, you gain temporary bonuses to all actions and may perform legendary deeds. Pay 6 Hearts to activate.

## Ring of Shadows

*The Whisper Band (ring, item slots 1)*

A simple silver ring that seems to absorb light, making it appear darker than it should. When worn, shadows seem to cling to the wearer, and their movements become more fluid and silent. The ring bears no visible markings, but those with Umbrakala Skill can sense the dark magic woven into its metal.

**Magical Writing:** Umbrakala - written in darkness and void, the script is invisible to normal sight but appears as shadowy glyphs to those with Umbrakala Skill. This is the secret magic of the Alu.

**Alignment Factors:** Umbrakala Skill, stealth-related Traits (silent, cunning, elusive), working in darkness, thievery or deception.

### Powers

1. **Shadow Cloak** (Alignment 1): You are harder to notice in dim light or darkness. Ask the Oracle if enemies fail to spot you. Passive effect.

2. **Shadow Meld** (Alignment 2): As an Action, pay 2 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you blend into shadows. On Yes, you become nearly invisible in darkness for 1 Round. Works similar to *Shadow Meld* (Umbrakala Level 5). Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 2.

3. **Shadow Step** (Alignment 3): As an Action, pay 3 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you step through shadows. On Yes, you may teleport up to 30 feet through areas of darkness. Works as *Shadow Step* (Umbrakala Level 5). Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 3.

4. **Deep Shadow** (Alignment 4): As an Action, pay 4 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you create an area of pure darkness. On Yes, create a 20-foot radius sphere of darkness that blocks all light. Works as *Deep Shadow* (Umbrakala Level 1) or *Night Sphere* (Umbrakala Level 3). Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 4.

5. **Shadow Clone** (Alignment 5): As an Action, pay 5 Hearts and ask the Oracle if you create a shadow duplicate. On Yes, create a dark copy of yourself that can perform simple actions or distract enemies. Works as *Shadow Clone* (Umbrakala Level 4). Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 5.

6. **Perfect Shadow** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, ask the Oracle if you become one with shadow itself. On Yes, you may pass through walls, become completely undetectable, or travel vast distances through darkness. Pay 6 Hearts to activate. Works similar to *Perfect Shadow* (Umbrakala Level 6). Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 6.
