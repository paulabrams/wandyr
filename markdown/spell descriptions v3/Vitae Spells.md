# Vitae (Life) Spells

*Life force, blood, and natural harmony. The primal magic of the Wood Elves and Kith, written in blood-stained ogham runes and triple spirals that pulse with living energy. The hallow channel the raw power of life itself, understanding that all living things - plants, animals, and people - share the same vital essence through blood, sap, and the eternal cycle of birth, death, and renewal.*

**1.1.1** *Animal Bond* - Form lasting connection with animals. **Instant** Create an empathic link with one animal you can see within 30 feet, gaining immediate insight into the animal's current emotions and basic intentions. **Active** Maintain ongoing communication with the bonded animals throughout the encounter, allowing you to share simple concepts like danger, food, or friendship. The sustained connection requires concentration and ends if you move more than 100 feet from any bonded animal. **Level** Number of animals you can bond with.

**1.1.2** *Animal Friendship* - Calm agitated beasts or make neutral animals friendly. **Instant** Immediately calm agitated animals or gain momentary trust from neutral ones. **Active** The affected animals regard you as a trusted friend throughout the encounter. The target animals must be able to see and hear you, and they cannot be hostile when you cast this spell. **Level** Number of animals affected.

**1.1.3** *Balance Weave* - Sense disruptions in natural harmony. **Instant** Get a quick impression of any major imbalances present in the area. **Active** Continuously perceive the presence of poisons, diseases, curses, or unnatural corruption as faint colored auras throughout the encounter. You can identify the general type of imbalance but not specific details. **Level** Natural awareness becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can detect subtler imbalances.

**1.1.4** *Beast Bond* - Form magical connections with domesticated animals. **Instant** Establish contact and exchange brief emotional impressions or sensations. **Active** Share basic emotions and sensations with the bonded creatures across any distance throughout the scene. The animals can send you simple impressions of what they see, hear, or smell. **Level** Number of animals bonded.

**1.1.5** *Beast Form* - Take on minor physical traits of animals. **Instant** Briefly manifest animal traits for a single action or ability check. **Active** Maintain the transformations throughout the encounter, gaining multiple enhancements like wolf hearing, cat night vision, or squirrel climbing claws. You retain your normal mental abilities and cannot gain supernatural animal powers. **Level** Number of traits manifested.

**1.1.6** *Beast Speech* - Speak with natural animals as if you shared a common language. **Instant** Exchange a few quick words or concepts with the animals. **Active** Maintain ongoing conversations throughout the scene, limited by each animal's intelligence and attention span. Most animals can share basic information about recent events, dangers, or the location of food and water. **Level** Number of animals you can speak with simultaneously.

**1.2.1** *Blood Bond* - Form mystical connection through shared blood. **Instant** Create a brief empathic link with one creature within 30 feet by sharing a drop of blood, gaining insight into their current state. **Active** Maintain a sustained blood bond throughout the encounter, allowing you to share sensations, emotions, and even transfer life force between you. The bond requires both participants to be willing and ends if either takes significant damage. **Level** Number of creatures bonded.

**1.2.2** *Blood Healing* - Heal another by sacrificing your own blood and life force. **Instant** Give up 1 of your own Hearts to restore 2 Hearts to a target within touch range. **Active** Maintain a sustained healing connection where you continuously sacrifice your life force to heal the target, appearing as a crimson stream of energy flowing from your wounds to theirs. **Level** Healing ratio: 1 Heart sacrificed equals more Hearts restored.

**1.2.3** *Joy Song* - Sing melody that lifts spirits of allies. **Instant** Immediately remove fear effects and grant momentary optimism to all allies within 20 feet who can hear you. **Active** Maintain the uplifting melody throughout the encounter, granting resistance to despair or hopelessness. Affected creatures feel genuinely happier and more optimistic about their situation. **Level** Musical influence spreads further and becomes more uplifting.

**1.2.4** *Sap Flow* - Connect your blood to a plant's sap. **Instant** Touch a plant and briefly share life force, either healing yourself with the plant's vitality or nourishing the plant with your blood. **Active** Maintain a sustained connection where your blood and the plant's sap flow together, creating a symbiotic relationship where both you and the plant benefit from shared life force. **Level** Number of plants connected and strength of the bond.

**1.2.5** *Blood Ward* - Create protective barrier using your own blood. **Instant** Sacrifice 1 Heart to create a brief protective barrier that blocks one attack. **Active** Maintain a sustained blood ward throughout the encounter, continuously sacrificing life force to protect allies. The ward appears as a crimson barrier that grows stronger with each sacrifice. **Level** Number of creatures protected and ward strength.

**1.2.6** *Pacify* - Calm aggressive emotions in nearby creatures. **Instant** Momentarily defuse immediate tension or halt an aggressive action in all creatures within a 15-foot radius who can see you. **Active** Affected beings must make an effort to initiate combat or violence throughout the scene. The effect ends immediately if any creature within the area takes damage. **Level** Peaceful influence spreads further and becomes more compelling.

**1.3.1** *Peace Aura* - Project aura of tranquility that discourages violence. **Instant** Create a brief moment of clarity that allows one creature to reconsider a violent action. **Active** Creatures within a 20-foot radius find it difficult to initiate combat and prefer to seek peaceful solutions throughout the scene. The effect ends if you take a hostile action. **Level** Peaceful aura spreads further and becomes more compelling.

**1.3.2** *Snail Knight* - Call forth giant garden snail as mount. **Instant** The snail appears briefly to carry you a short distance or provide quick transport. **Active** The snail remains for the entire encounter, moving slowly (half normal speed) but carrying you and your equipment safely over any terrain. It has 3 Hearts and natural armor that reduces damage by 1 point. **Level** Snail's size and Hearts.

**1.3.3** *Star Guide* - Ask stars for guidance about your journey. **Instant** Receive a simple yes/no answer or brief directional sense. **Active** Maintain ongoing celestial guidance throughout the scene, providing continuous directional awareness and insight into the natural path ahead. The stars can only provide guidance about natural paths and destinations, not specific people or hidden locations. **Level** Detail and range of guidance.

**1.3.4** *Sun Beam* - Create ray of warm sunlight. **Instant** Flash of brilliant sunlight that extends 30 feet from your hand, illuminating an area and dealing 1d6 damage to undead creatures. **Active** Maintain a continuous beam that illuminates dark areas as bright as noon and provides comfortable warmth throughout the encounter. Undead creatures in the light feel uncomfortable and take ongoing damage. **Level** Range and damage: +1d6 per Level.

**1.3.5** *Thicket* - Rapidly grow barrier of thorny bushes. **Instant** Create a temporary barrier in a 10-foot square area that lasts only moments but can block immediate movement. **Active** Grow a permanent thicket 5 feet tall that blocks movement unless creatures spend extra effort to push through. The thorns deal 1d6 damage to anyone forcing their way through carelessly. **Level** Plant growth becomes more vigorous and extensive.

**1.3.6** *Truth Sight* - Gain ability to detect lies and deceptions. **Instant** Immediately know if the last statement you heard was a deliberate falsehood. **Active** Throughout the encounter, when someone speaks a deliberate falsehood in your presence, you see a faint red shimmer around them. The spell reveals intentional lies but not mistakes, omissions, or cleverly worded truths. **Level** Number of people monitored.

**1.4.1** *Ward* - Create protective barrier of life energy. **Instant** Absorb one incoming harmful magical effect immediately on yourself or an ally within 30 feet. **Active** The ward appears as a faint green shimmer and absorbs multiple harmful magical effects throughout the encounter. Multiple wards cannot be placed on the same creature. **Level** Number of effects absorbed.

**1.4.2** *Cure Disease* - Remove natural disease, poison, or infection. **Instant** Provide immediate relief and begin the purging process on yourself or a creature you touch. **Active** Your life magic thoroughly purges the harmful agents over several minutes, ensuring complete recovery. This spell cannot cure magical diseases, curses, or conditions caused by supernatural creatures. **Level** Number of creatures or severity of ailment.

**1.4.3** *Forest Touch* - Touch any plant and communicate with it. **Instant** Exchange a few basic concepts or get a quick impression of recent events. **Active** Maintain ongoing conversation throughout the scene where plants can share information about recent events they witnessed, weather patterns, and the health of the local ecosystem. Trees and older plants provide more detailed information than grass or flowers. **Level** Number of plants contacted.

**1.4.4** *Growing Script* - Write words that appear as living vines. **Instant** Create simple, static text that conveys your basic message, growing across any natural surface. **Active** The text slowly changes and evolves throughout the scene, conveying different aspects of your intended message in real-time. The script glows faintly green and feels warm to the touch. **Level** Length and complexity of message.

**1.4.5** *Nature's Blessing* - Call upon local nature spirits for assistance. **Instant** Receive immediate insight about fresh water, edible plants, or natural shelter in the area. **Active** Gain advantage on survival-related actions throughout the encounter as spirits manifest as helpful animals, favorable weather, or convenient natural formations. **Level** Nature spirit aid becomes more generous and responsive.

**1.4.6** *Purification* - Cleanse water, food, or air of contamination. **Instant** Quickly neutralize immediate contamination in up to one gallon of substance. **Active** Thoroughly purify larger quantities, neutralizing bacteria, parasites, and mild poisons over several minutes. The magic cannot affect magical corruption or supernatural effects. **Level** Purification becomes more thorough and extensive.

**1.5.1** *Regeneration* - Accelerate natural healing processes through touch. **Instant** The target regains 1 Heart immediately as wounds begin to close. **Active** The target regains 1 additional Heart at the start of each subsequent turn during the encounter, with enhanced healing appearing as a soft green glow around wounds. **Level** Hearts healed per turn.

**1.5.2** *Heart Glyph* - Inscribe magical symbol that conveys emotional state. **Instant** Create a simple glyph that shows your immediate feelings. **Active** The glyph continues to shift and change colors throughout the encounter, reflecting your evolving emotional state in real-time. Others who view the symbol understand your emotions and can respond accordingly. **Level** Complexity and duration of emotional display.

**1.5.3** *Nature's Bounty* - Manifest fresh food and water from ambient life force. **Instant** Create a quick snack or drink to satisfy immediate hunger or thirst. **Active** Gradually manifest enough sustenance for up to 4 people throughout the encounter, appearing as fruits, nuts, berries, and pure spring water perfectly suited to local tastes and dietary needs. **Level** Sustenance creation becomes more abundant and nourishing.

**1.5.4** *Pack Call* - Call small woodland creatures to assist with tasks. **Instant** Animals appear briefly to deliver a message or perform one quick task. **Active** 2-3 animals remain throughout the encounter, including squirrels, rabbits, or birds depending on the local environment. They can carry messages, gather information, or perform basic chores but will not fight or enter dangerous areas. **Level** Animal allies become more numerous and helpful.

**1.5.5** *Vital Surge* - Channel raw life energy into nearby creatures. **Instant** Provide immediate invigoration to plants, small animals, or willing creatures within 30 feet, granting advantage on the next physical action taken. **Active** Maintain a continuous flow of life energy throughout the encounter, causing wilted plants to perk up, tired animals to regain energy, and people to feel refreshed. You can affect up to 6 targets simultaneously. **Level** Life energy channeling becomes more powerful and extensive.

**1.5.6** *Wild Empathy* - Open your mind to wild animal emotions and intentions. **Instant** Get a quick snapshot of the general emotional state of all wild animals within 100 feet. **Active** Continuously sense their fear, curiosity, hunger, or contentment as a background emotional landscape throughout the encounter. This awareness helps you understand animal behavior and anticipate potential threats or opportunities. **Level** Empathic range expands naturally, emotional sensitivity deepens.

**1.6.1** *Life Transfer* - Transfer your own life energy to heal another. **Instant** Give up 1 of your own Hearts to restore 1 to a target within touch range immediately. **Active** Create an ongoing connection where you may transfer up to 3 of your Hearts throughout the encounter, appearing as a stream of golden light flowing between you and the recipient. This spell can bring someone back from the brink of death but cannot reduce your Hearts below 1. **Level** Transfer rate: 1 Heart per Level spent.

**1.6.2** *Nature's Rhythm* - Attune yourself to natural rhythms and cycles. **Instant** Gain immediate insight into current weather patterns or optimal timing for one specific action. **Active** Maintain deep attunement throughout the encounter, gaining intuitive knowledge of weather patterns, optimal travel times, and seasonal changes. This alignment grants advantage on all survival and navigation checks. **Level** Natural attunement becomes more profound and comprehensive.

**1.6.3** *Perfect Unity* - Achieve perfect harmony with your environment. **Instant** Create a momentary wave of peace in a 50-foot radius that stops all aggressive actions for one round. **Active** Plants lean toward you in greeting, animals approach peacefully, and the air feels refreshing and pure throughout the encounter. All creatures in the area feel a strong compulsion toward peace and cooperation, making violence extremely difficult to initiate. **Level** Harmony extends further and becomes more profound.

**1.6.4** *Season's Call* - Shift seasonal state of nearby area. **Instant** Create a brief seasonal display in a 30-foot radius, such as a momentary bloom of flowers or falling leaves. **Active** Maintain the seasonal shift throughout the encounter, making spring flowers bloom in winter or autumn leaves fall in summer. The change affects only plant growth and appearance, not temperature or weather. **Level** Seasonal influence spreads further and becomes more dramatic.

**1.6.5** *Circle of Life* - Create zone where all living things share natural harmony. **Instant** Create a brief moment where all creatures in a 30-foot radius feel connected to the natural world and each other. **Active** Maintain a sustained harmony where plants grow more vigorously, animals become more cooperative, and all living things share in the natural flow of life force. The area becomes a haven of natural balance where healing is enhanced and violence is difficult to initiate. **Level** Harmony spreads further and becomes more profound.

**1.6.6** *Blood Eagle* - Perform ancient ritual sacrificing life force for power. **Instant** Sacrifice 3 Hearts to gain overwhelming strength and ferocity for one round, dealing double damage and gaining advantage on all attacks. **Active** Maintain the blood eagle transformation throughout the encounter, continuously sacrificing life force to maintain the primal state. You become a terrifying figure of blood and fury, but the ritual will eventually consume you if maintained too long. **Level** Blood eagle power becomes more intense and lasts longer.
