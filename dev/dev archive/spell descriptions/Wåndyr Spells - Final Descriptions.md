# Wåndyr Spells - Final Descriptions

## Level 1 Spells

**EARTH SHAPE - Mold earth and stone (1st, Single target, Active)**
The caster can reshape clay-rich soil and soft stone within a moderate area, creating intricate and beautiful structures. The earth flows like liquid, forming stunning crystalline patterns as it solidifies. The spell creates surprisingly stable, permanent formations that can serve as steps, walls, or perfect camouflage. The reshaped earth glows with a subtle crystalline sheen and can purify contaminated soil. The spell works best with clay-rich soil and is very easy to dispel when needed. The formations are particularly effective in extreme conditions, though they may become unstable in such environments.

**ANIMATED OBJECT - Bring object to life (1st, Single target, Sustained)**
The caster can animate a hand-sized object (up to chair-sized) that has sentimental value, granting it simple awareness and movement. The object develops a basic personality and can perform tasks like fetching, carrying, or simple manipulation. Animated objects can coordinate with each other and become more cooperative with repeated use. The magical bond is very strong and stable, and objects protect themselves instinctively. The animation manifests as a warm, pulsing glow and fluid movements that seem almost natural. The spell only works on non-magical items and requires simple command words to control.

**FOG CLOUD - Create concealing mist (1st, Area, Sustained)**
The caster creates a dense bank of fog that provides perfect concealment within a moderate area. The fog responds to the caster's emotions and can be shaped into simple forms. It carries the caster's favorite scent and can transport whispered messages or small objects. The fog resists natural dispersal and requires magical force to dispel. It blocks all forms of vision and can be cast through reflections. The fog affects temperature and can carry objects, making it versatile for various applications.

**FETCH ITEM - Summon nearby object (1st, Single target, Instant)**
The caster can summon a small, unattended object from very close range with a simple pointing gesture. The spell works through transparent barriers and can retrieve items from containers. Objects are protected during transport and arrive nearly instantly, leaving a beautiful light trail. The spell can handle surprisingly heavy objects and can also return items to their original location. The spell can detect magical wards and works only on items the caster owns, ensuring safe and controlled use.

## Level 2 Spells

**LIQUID AIR - Transform air to liquid (2nd, Area, Sustained)**
The caster transforms air into a breathable liquid that purifies the atmosphere. The liquid air creates beautiful prismatic effects when shaped and can form perfect seals. It can carry sound perfectly and create air bridges for transportation. The liquid air is completely stable and unaffected by wind or fire. It can be shaped into complex structures and containers, though objects pass through it rather than being carried. The spell is very easy to maintain and versatile for various applications, affecting temperature as it transforms the air.

**SCULPT ELEMENTS - Shape natural materials (2nd, Single target, Active)**
The caster can reshape and enhance natural, unprocessed materials into intricate designs and useful objects. The spell can work on surprisingly large objects and creates completely stable, permanent results that appear entirely natural. It can repair damaged items, enhance material strength, and even create plant-like living structures. The spell can alter object appearances for disguise and purify contaminated materials. Results are perfect for artisans and craftspeople, focusing on quality and natural beauty. The spell works on magical items and doesn't require artistic skill to use effectively.

**HOVER - Defy gravity briefly (2nd, Self, Sustained)**
The caster's feet lift a few inches from the ground, allowing smooth, silent movement over any surface. The effect creates subtle light effects and a pleasant humming sound. Wind enhances the spell's stability, and it can create invisible steps to reach higher places or temporary platforms. The spell automatically protects from falls and can carry small objects. It's perfect for stealth and protects from ground-based hazards, leaving no footprints behind. The spell doesn't work over water but is very easy to maintain.

## Level 3 Spells

**WIND WALK - Ride the air currents (3rd, Self, Sustained)**
The caster gains complete freedom of movement through the air, swimming through invisible currents at walking speed. The spell creates beautiful wind trails and patterns, making the caster visible but allowing them to carry objects and messages. It works in any environment, including confined spaces where it can create air currents. The spell can reach impressive heights and create wind barriers, adapting to different conditions. The caster's senses remain normal, and the spell is very easy to maintain, though it's dangerous to use in storms.

**COUNTER SPELL - Disrupt magical effects (3rd, Single target, Instant)**
The caster can perfectly cancel spells of equal or lower level with simple but precise gestures. The spell works through transparent barriers and can counter area effects by creating safe zones. It can counter spells as they're being cast and provides insights into spell structure. The counter-spell creates harmless magical sparks when it backfires and cannot be blocked by magical protection. The spell becomes more effective with practice and can also weaken ongoing magical effects, though it cannot affect permanent enchantments.

**CREATE IDOL - Summon statue (3rd, Single target, Instant)**
The caster creates a permanent, beautiful idol or religious statue from stone or precious metals. The idol can range from hand-sized to building-sized and creates a sense of sacred space in its surroundings. The spell can embed precious materials like gems and metals, and the resulting idol can store magical energy for later use. The idol appears completely mundane but can channel divine energy and be used for divination. It's resistant to all forms of damage and cannot be moved after creation, making it perfect for establishing permanent religious sites or ceremonial spaces.
