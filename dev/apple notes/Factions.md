# Factions

Every dungeon has 2-3 factions vying for supremacy. This creates tension and options for PCs.

The GM gives each faction a rank, either 1, 2 or 3, with 3 having the most control over a dungeon.

Factions will move up and down ranks based on player actions (i.e. aiding them, foiling their plans).

A faction can never share a rank with another faction, instead they replace that rank and the other faction moves down one place.

**Example:** If faction 2 kobolds go up to 3 rank, the 3 rank skeletons drop to rank 2, ousted for supremacy.

Ranks can only ascend or descend by one point at a time.
