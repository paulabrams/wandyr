# Random Encounters - Workshop Halls (Upper Level)

*Roll 1d20. Check every 3 turns, encounter on 6.*

1. **Pale Cave Spiders** (1d3) - HD 1/2, AC 7; fever bite, weave in corners
2. **Mountain Wolves** (1d2) - HD 2+2, AC 7; pack hunters, entered seeking shelter
3. **Stone Gargoyles** (2d3) - HD 1/2, AC 4; animated by Dwur magic, guard workshops
4. **Giant Rats** (2d4) - HD 1/2, AC 7; nest in old food stores
5. **Cave Fisher** (1) - HD 3, AC 4; hangs from ceiling, fishing line attack
6. **Echo-Miners** (1d2) - HD 1, AC 9; ghostly Dwur workers, repeat last tasks
7. **Kobold Scavengers** (1d4) - HD 1/2, AC 7; alpine-adapted, seek shiny tools
8. **Dwur Clan Ghost** (1) - HD 3*, AC 5; ancient ancestor, may aid or test party
9. **Animated Tools** (1d6) - HD 1, AC 6; flying hammers and chisels
10. **Giant Cave Beetles** (1d3) - HD 2, AC 5; armored, feed on wood
11. **Workshop Golem** (1) - HD 4, AC 3; animated by Dwur magic, protects tools
12. **Cave Fisher Bat** (1d4) - HD 1, AC 7; echolocation, swarm in darkness
13. **Stone Gargoyle Sentry** (1) - HD 2, AC 4; larger guardian, more intelligent
14. **Giant Cave Centipede** (1) - HD 1/2, AC 9; poisonous bite, scuttles in darkness
15. **Workshop Spirit** (1) - HD 2*, AC 5; benevolent, may teach crafting
16. **Cave Fisher Spider** (1d2) - HD 2, AC 6; web-spinner, ambush predator
17. **Animated Armor** (1) - HD 3, AC 4; empty armor, defends workshop
18. **Giant Cave Cricket** (1d3) - HD 2, AC 7; chirping creatures, sensitive to light
19. **Workshop Guardian** (1) - HD 5*, AC 3; powerful spirit, tests craftsmanship
20. **Cave Fisher Rat** (1d4) - HD 1, AC 7; disease carriers, swarm in numbers

## Workshop Hazards (Roll when exploring)

- **Unstable ceiling** - STR check to avoid 1d6 damage
- **Rusted tools** - Sharp edges, DEX save or 1 damage + tetanus
- **Slippery floors** - Old grease/oil, DEX save or fall prone
- **Toxic fumes** - From old chemicals, CON save or sickened
