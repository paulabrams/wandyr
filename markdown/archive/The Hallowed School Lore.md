## The Hallowed School of Divine Authority

### Origins and Philosophy
The Hallowed School traces its origins to the first hallow - living saints who achieved communion with otherworldly beings. These early practitioners learned to channel the divine authority that flows from the hallowed realms into the mortal world. In sacred halls and hidden sanctuaries, these hallow discovered that true power comes not from domination, but from embodying the principles of divine law and righteousness.

The philosophy of The Hallowed School holds that magic is a sacred trust granted to those who prove themselves worthy of serving as vessels for divine authority. Practitioners believe that by living according to divine principles and serving the greater good, they can channel the power of the heavenly realms themselves. This stands in direct opposition to the corrupting influences of dark magic and the chaotic forces that threaten civilization.

Unlike other schools that manipulate existing energies, Hallowed School practitioners call upon the divine authority that exists beyond mortal understanding. They believe that magic should serve as a beacon of hope and a shield against darkness, leaving no doubt about the righteousness of their cause.

### Practitioners and Titles
**Hallow**: The most revered practitioners, these living saints have achieved communion with otherworldly beings and serve as vessels for divine authority. They often serve as spiritual guides, guardians of sacred places, and champions against evil.

**Divine Magistrates**: Noble practitioners who combine worldly authority with divine power, ruling their domains with wisdom and righteousness. They maintain the sacred traditions while adapting divine law to the mortal world.

**Paladins of Light**: Warriors who have sworn sacred oaths to uphold divine law and order, combining martial prowess with hallowed magic. They serve as protectors of the weak and defenders of civilization.

**Celestial Scribes**: Masters who specialize in celestial script and the creation of holy texts. Their spellbooks are works of art written in silver ink that glows with inner light.

**Guardians of the Sacred**: Specialists who focus on maintaining divine order and justice, using their magic to enforce sacred contracts, reveal divine truth, and protect the innocent from harm.

### Dress and Appearance
Hallowed School practitioners favor clothing that reflects their sacred calling and divine connection. White robes trimmed with gold or silver are common, often embroidered with celestial symbols and runes of power. Many wear crowns or circlets that mark their divine authority, and their weapons and armor are typically blessed and consecrated.

Their spell components are kept in ornate containers of precious metals, often decorated with sacred symbols and protective runes. Senior practitioners often bear ritual scars or tattoos that glow with divine light when they channel their power, and their eyes sometimes take on a golden or silver sheen from years of wielding hallowed authority.

### Sacred Components and Materials
**Celestial Silver**: The signature material of Hallowed School magic, this precious metal is said to be blessed by the heavenly realms themselves. When used to inscribe spells or create magical items, it glows with an inner light that banishes darkness and reveals truth.

**Divine Ink**: Made from crushed pearls, moonlight, and the tears of the righteous, this ink is used to write spells and create holy texts. The ink never fades and continues to glow with divine light.

**Blessed Crystals**: Crystals that have been exposed to divine light and blessed by holy ceremonies. Each type of crystal corresponds to different aspects of divine authority - diamonds for truth, sapphires for wisdom, rubies for courage.

**Sacred Oils**: Oils blessed by religious ceremonies and used to anoint weapons, armor, and magical items. Each oil imparts different properties - oil of blessing for protection, oil of truth for revelation, oil of purification for cleansing.

**Holy Water**: Water that has been blessed by divine ceremony and exposed to celestial light. It is used in purification rituals and to create protective barriers against evil.

**Divine Relics**: Objects that have been touched by divine power or used in holy ceremonies. These relics serve as powerful focuses for Hallowed School magic and are often passed down through generations.

### Traditions and Practices
Hallowed School practitioners begin each day with the "Dawn Salutation," facing the rising sun and reciting prayers of gratitude and dedication. They maintain personal shrines where they meditate on the principles of divine law, order, and righteousness.

During important ceremonies, Hallowed School practitioners gather for "Divine Councils" where they share knowledge, renew their vows to serve the divine will, and discuss threats to civilization. These gatherings often take place in sacred halls or temples marked by ancient symbols of divine authority.

Many Hallowed School practitioners undertake pilgrimages to sacred sites - ancient temples, holy mountains, or places where divine miracles are said to have occurred. These journeys are considered essential for deepening their connection to divine authority and understanding their role in the cosmic order.

The most important ceremony is the "Ritual of Ascension," held when a practitioner reaches the highest levels of mastery. This ceremony involves a series of tests and trials designed to prove the practitioner's worthiness to wield divine authority, and culminates in a transformation that marks them as true masters of The Hallowed School.
