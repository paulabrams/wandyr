# Illusione (Illusion) Spells

*Perception, thought, and dreams. The subtle magic of the Belerions, woven from phantasmal scripts that deceive the senses. Illusionists create illusions, alter memories, influence minds, and bend perceived reality.*

**4.1.1** *Arcane Eye* - Create invisible magical sensor. **Instant** Create an invisible, flying eye that you can control telepathically within 100 feet for a brief glimpse through the eye for reconnaissance. The eye can move through spaces as small as 1 inch and provides vision as if it were your own. You cannot cast spells through it, and bright light or magical detection can reveal its presence. **Active** Maintain sustained surveillance throughout the encounter with an invisible flying eye under your telepathic control. The eye persists and can scout continuously within 100 feet, allowing ongoing observation and reconnaissance. **Level** Additional eyes created.
**4.1.2** *Auditory Illusion* - Produce any sound you have heard before. **Instant** Create a single sound effect for distraction or misdirection at a location within 60 feet. The sound can be as quiet as a whisper or as loud as a shouting person, including voices, animal noises, footsteps, or environmental sounds. **Active** Maintain a continuous audio environment with moving and changing sounds throughout the scene. The sounds can move around at your will. Listeners who examine the sound closely may realize it's an illusion with careful attention. **Level** Simultaneous sound sources.
**4.1.3** *Bewitch* - Cloud the thoughts of one creature. **Instant** Create brief mental confusion causing disadvantage on their next action in one creature within 30 feet. The target feels foggy and uncertain but remains aware. **Active** Maintain sustained mental fog affecting all actions throughout the encounter. The target has difficulty concentrating and making decisions, suffering disadvantage on mental tasks and spellcasting. The effect breaks if they take damage or someone helps them focus. **Level** Additional targets affected.
**4.1.4** *Blur* - Make your outline constantly shift and waver. **Instant** Create brief distortion providing advantage on one defensive action. The effect is subtle to casual observers but noticeable to anyone trying to interact physically. **Active** Maintain continuous blurring effect protecting you throughout the encounter, making you difficult to target precisely. Attackers have disadvantage on attack rolls against you as your exact position becomes unclear. The blurring becomes more pronounced when you move quickly. **Level** Additional targets protected.
**4.1.5** *Charm* - Make one person regard you as trusted friend. **Instant** Create a momentary surge of goodwill for one favorable reaction in one person within 30 feet. The charmed person acts naturally but with a positive disposition toward you. **Active** Maintain sustained friendship throughout the scene, keeping their helpful attitude. They won't do anything obviously harmful to themselves or their true allies, but will be helpful and give you the benefit of the doubt. When the effect ends, they may realize they were magically influenced. **Level** Additional targets charmed.
**4.1.6** *Detect Magic* - Sense presence and nature of magical auras. **Instant** Perform a quick scan revealing magical presences and their general nature within 30 feet. You can determine the approximate power level and school of magic, and whether items or creatures are magical. **Active** Maintain continuous magical sight allowing detailed analysis throughout the encounter. The spell reveals illusions, enchantments, and other magical effects as faint glowing outlines visible only to you. Focusing on a specific aura reveals more detailed information. **Level** Magical sight range expands, aura detail clarity improves.

**4.2.1** *Disguise* - Create illusion that changes your appearance. **Instant** Create a quick disguise for a brief deception or escape, allowing you to look like any humanoid of roughly your size. Physical inspection or touch reveals the illusion, but casual observation is completely fooled. **Active** Maintain a sustained disguise that you can adjust throughout the scene. The disguise includes clothing, hair, skin tone, and minor physical features, but not your voice or mannerisms. **Level** Disguise complexity and detail.
**4.2.2** *Dream Message* - Send message to any sleeping person you know. **Instant** Send a brief dream message with simple content to any sleeping person within 100 miles. The message appears as a vivid dream that the target will remember clearly upon waking. **Active** Create an extended dream sequence allowing detailed communication and emotional connection. You can include simple images, emotions, and spoken words, though complex information may be distorted by the dreaming mind. **Level** Additional sleeping targets.
**4.2.3** *Dream Walking* - Project consciousness into dreams of sleeping person. **Instant** Take a quick glimpse into their current dream state for basic impressions of a sleeping person you can see within 30 feet. You experience their dream as if it were real. **Active** Achieve full dream immersion allowing deep exploration of their subconscious until they wake or you withdraw. You can interact with dream elements and the dreamer's subconscious, gathering information about their memories, fears, and desires, but cannot directly control the dream content. **Level** Depth of dream penetration and control.
**4.2.4** *Duplicate* - Create identical illusory copies of yourself. **Instant** Create a single duplicate that lasts only moments for one deceptive action. The duplicate occupies the same space as you initially, then spreads out as you move. **Active** Create 1d4 multiple duplicates that persist throughout the encounter, continuously confusing enemies. The duplicates mimic your movements and when a duplicate takes any damage or is touched, it immediately disappears. Attackers must guess which version is real, causing attacks to hit duplicates instead. **Level** Number of duplicates created.
**4.2.5** *Emotional Aura* - Emanate subtle emotional influence. **Instant** Create a brief emotional pulse affecting immediate reactions in a 20-foot radius. You can choose one emotion such as calm, fear, joy, or sadness that affects all creatures in the area. **Active** Maintain a sustained emotional atmosphere throughout the scene. Those affected feel the emotion as if it arose naturally, though strong-willed individuals may resist or recognize the artificial nature. The effect enhances existing emotions rather than overriding them. **Level** Emotional influence spreads further and becomes more compelling.
**4.2.6** *False Memory* - Implant false memory into one person's mind. **Instant** Create a brief false memory of a simple event or detail in one person within touch range. The memory feels completely real and integrates naturally with their existing memories. **Active** Plant a complex false memory of extended events that becomes deeply embedded in their recollection. The false memory cannot be obviously impossible or completely contrary to their nature and beliefs. Over time, especially if contradicted by evidence, they may question its authenticity. **Level** Memory complexity and permanence.

**4.3.1** *Feign Death* - Make yourself or willing creature appear completely dead. **Instant** Create a brief death-like state for avoiding one attack or check. Even close medical examination concludes death unless magical detection is used. **Active** Maintain a sustained death illusion throughout the scene, perfect for extended deception. The target's breathing and heartbeat become undetectable, their body temperature drops, and they become rigid and pale. They remain conscious but cannot move or speak, and take no damage from most sources. **Level** Additional targets affected.
**4.3.2** *Greed* - Cause one creature to become obsessively fixated. **Instant** Create a brief moment of obsession affecting one decision or action toward a specific object you designate in one creature within 30 feet. They rationalize their behavior as completely natural and reasonable. **Active** Maintain sustained fixation driving their behavior throughout the encounter. The target will prioritize acquiring or protecting that object above almost all other concerns, though they won't obviously endanger themselves. When the effect ends, they're confused about their intense interest. **Level** Additional targets affected.
**4.3.3** *Hypnotize* - Put willing or unconscious creature into deep trance. **Instant** Extract a single truthful answer to one important question from a creature within touch range. They cannot lie but may give incomplete answers if poorly questioned. **Active** Conduct an extended questioning session allowing thorough interrogation throughout the scene. The subject speaks in a monotone voice and responds only to direct questions about things they know. When it ends, they remember being questioned but it feels dreamlike. **Level** Depth of mental access and revelation.
**4.3.4** *Illusory Spray* - Create burst of shifting, colorful lights. **Instant** Create a single blinding flash causing brief disorientation in a 15-foot cone. The lights are purely illusory but create real sensory overload. **Active** Maintain a continuous light show keeping disorientation throughout the encounter. Those caught in the display see phantom images, distorted colors, and disorienting patterns that make concentration difficult. Affected creatures have disadvantage and may stumble or drop held items. **Level** Light patterns become more complex and disorienting.
**4.3.5** *Invisibility* - Make yourself or one creature completely invisible. **Instant** Create brief invisibility for one surprise action or escape. The spell creates a perfect optical illusion rather than true concealment. **Active** Maintain sustained invisibility throughout the encounter, ending if they attack or cast spells. The target cannot be seen by normal sight, but they still make sounds, leave footprints, and can be detected by other senses. Equipment and clothing also become invisible. **Level** Additional targets made invisible.
**4.3.6** *Mass Suggestion* - Plant same suggestion in multiple minds. **Instant** Create a brief impulse affecting immediate group behavior of up to 6 creatures within 60 feet. The suggestion must be reasonable and phrased as a course of action. **Active** Maintain a sustained suggestion driving coordinated group action throughout an extended period. Targets will pursue the suggested action as if it were their own idea, unless it would obviously harm them. The suggestion fades when completed, leaving no memory of magical influence. **Level** Additional targets affected beyond base 6.

**4.4.1** *Major Image* - Perfect illusion: Create a realistic illusion with all sensory effects. **Instant** Create a single impressive scene for immediate impact up to 20 feet in each dimension that includes sight, sound, smell, and temperature effects. Physical interaction reveals the illusion's true nature, but all other senses are completely deceived. **Active** Maintain a complex, evolving illusion that responds to events throughout the encounter. The illusion can move and change according to your will within the spell's area, mimicking complex scenes like people talking, fires burning, or flowing rivers. **Level** illusion becomes more convincing and detailed, sensory effects intensify, can encompass larger scenes naturally.
**4.4.2** *Mind Maze* - Trap thoughts: Trap one creature's consciousness in an illusory mental labyrinth. **Instant** Create brief mental confusion causing one lost action for a creature within 30 feet. The target's body stands motionless while their mind briefly navigates a confusing mental maze. **Active** Maintain a sustained mental trap requiring continuous escape attempts throughout the encounter. Their mind navigates an endless maze of their own memories and fears while they can attempt to escape by solving mental puzzles or overcoming psychological obstacles. While trapped, they're completely unaware of the outside world and vulnerable to attacks. **Level** maze complexity and escape difficulty.
**4.4.3** *Mind Shield* - Protect thoughts: Create a barrier around your mind that prevents mental intrusions. **Instant** Provide brief mental protection against one intrusion attempt. Creatures attempting to access your mind sense only static or false thoughts you project. **Active** Maintain a sustained mental barrier protecting against all intrusions throughout an extended period. The shield blocks telepathy, mind-reading magic, and other mental intrusions while allowing your own mental abilities to function normally. The protection is invisible but absolute. **Level** additional minds protected.
**4.4.4** *Mirror Image* - Create duplicates: Surround yourself with identical illusory copies. **Instant** Create a single mirror image for one defensive action. When attacked, roll randomly to see if the duplicate or you is hit - duplicates disappear when struck. **Active** Maintain 1d4+1 multiple independent duplicates that persist and regenerate throughout the encounter. Each duplicate occupies its own space and can move up to 30 feet from you before vanishing. The images mimic your actions imperfectly, making it difficult to determine which is real. **Level** number of mirror images created.
**4.4.5** *Mirror Walk* - Step through glass: Step into any mirror or reflective surface and emerge elsewhere. **Instant** Perform a single teleportation through mirrors for escape or positioning. You step into any mirror or reflective surface large enough to fit through and emerge from another reflective surface within 100 feet. **Active** Maintain continuous ability to step between reflective surfaces throughout the scene. You must be able to visualize or have seen the destination surface. You can bring one other willing creature touching you. The spell works on windows, polished metal, still water, or any clear reflective surface. **Level** additional creatures transported.
**4.4.6** *Perfect Illusion* - Undetectable fake: Create an illusion of any object that is completely convincing. **Instant** Create a brief perfect illusion for one critical deception of any object no larger than 5 feet in any dimension. Only magical detection or illusion-revealing spells can penetrate this perfect deception. **Active** Maintain a sustained perfect illusion that maintains its deception throughout an extended period. The illusion has apparent weight, texture, temperature, and even taste if appropriate. It can be picked up, manipulated, and examined closely without revealing its true nature. **Level** object size increases by 5 feet per dimension per Level.

**4.5.1** *Phantom Sound* - Distant noise: Create a sound that appears to come from anywhere within 120 feet. **Instant** Create a single sound effect for immediate distraction or misdirection. Volume ranges from a whisper to a shout, and the sound is completely convincing unless listeners examine it carefully. **Active** Maintain continuous phantom sounds that can move and change throughout the encounter. The sound can be a voice, animal call, footsteps, or any noise you've heard before, and can move around at your will. You can make it fade in and out or change pitch and tone. **Level** sound projection becomes more versatile and convincing, can create multiple simultaneous sounds, acoustic precision improves.
**4.5.2** *Phantasmal Force* - Moving illusion: Create an illusion visible only to one creature within 60 feet. **Instant** Create a brief nightmare vision causing immediate psychological trauma that manifests their fears or expectations. The illusion appears completely real to the victim but is invisible to others. **Active** Maintain sustained personal torment that continuously affects the target throughout the encounter. The phantasm deals psychic damage as the target's mind makes the illusion feel real. The target can attempt to disbelieve the illusion, but their own psychology works against them. **Level** additional targets affected by personal phantasms.
**4.5.3** *Programmed Illusion* - Triggered effect: Create an illusion that activates when specific conditions are met. **Instant** Create a simple triggered illusion that plays once when conditions are met up to 10 feet in each dimension. You set the trigger condition (such as "when someone opens this door") and the illusion that appears (visual and auditory). **Active** Maintain a complex programmed illusion that can trigger repeatedly and persist for extended periods. The illusion plays out when triggered, then resets to trigger again. The programmed effect can activate multiple times. **Level** illusion complexity and sensory scope.
**4.5.4** *Read Mind* - Hear thoughts: Listen to the surface thoughts of one creature within 30 feet. **Instant** Gain a brief glimpse of their current thoughts and immediate intentions, hearing their current mental commentary as whispered words. The spell picks up active thoughts, not subconscious processes. **Active** Maintain continuous monitoring of their surface thoughts throughout the encounter. You gain insight into their immediate intentions, concerns, and reactions, but cannot access deep memories or hidden knowledge. The target may sense something if they're mentally strong or magically sensitive. **Level** depth of mental access and memory reach.
**4.5.5** *Scry* - Share vision: Create a magical sensor that allows you to see and hear remotely. **Instant** Gain a brief glimpse of a distant location for reconnaissance within 1 mile. You must have visited the location before or have a clear mental image from detailed descriptions. **Active** Maintain sustained remote viewing allowing continuous observation throughout the scene. The sensor is invisible and intangible but can be detected by magical means. Your body remains in a trance-like state while your senses are projected. **Level** scrying becomes clearer and more stable, distance limitations relax gradually, can maintain longer viewing sessions.
**4.5.6** *Silent Image* - Create scene: Project a purely visual illusion up to 15 feet in each dimension. **Instant** Create a single static image for immediate visual deception. The image has no sound, smell, or physical presence, but can display any scene you can imagine with perfect visual clarity. **Active** Maintain a dynamic visual illusion that can move and change throughout the encounter. You can make it move naturally within its space, change colors and shapes, or cycle through different images. Close physical inspection reveals its incorporeal nature. **Level** visual complexity and detail improve dramatically, can maintain multiple changing scenes simultaneously, clarity becomes more convincing.

**4.6.1** *Spectacle* - Create grand illusion: Fill a 50-foot radius area with an impressive illusion involving all senses. **Instant** Create a brief spectacular display for dramatic effect or distraction. While convincing to all senses, physical interaction with major elements reveals their illusory nature. **Active** Maintain a sustained grand illusion that evolves and responds throughout the encounter. You might create the appearance of a royal court, a dragon's lair, or a magical forest complete with sounds, smells, and tactile sensations. The illusion can include moving creatures and environmental effects that respond to observers. **Level** illusion becomes more immersive and believable, environmental complexity increases, sensory authenticity deepens.
**4.6.2** *Telepathy* - Project thoughts: Establish a mental connection with any creature you've met. **Instant** Send a brief telepathic message for urgent communication to any creature within 100 miles. Language barriers don't matter as thoughts transfer meaning directly, and communication cannot be intercepted by normal means. **Active** Maintain a sustained mental link allowing continuous conversation throughout the scene. You can communicate wordlessly across any distance, sharing thoughts, emotions, and simple images instantly. The connection works both ways unless the target actively resists, allowing for true mental conversation. **Level** additional simultaneous telepathic connections.
**4.6.3** *True Seeing* - Pierce deception: See through all illusions, transmutations, and deceptions. **Instant** Gain a brief moment of true sight to pierce one specific deception within 120 feet. The spell reveals the true nature of magical auras and enchantments in that moment. **Active** Maintain sustained true vision revealing all deceptions throughout an extended period. You perceive the true form of shapeshifted creatures, see invisible objects and beings, detect magical disguises, and recognize illusions for what they are. Hidden doors, secret passages, and concealed objects become visible to your enhanced sight. **Level** truth vision becomes more penetrating and comprehensive, can pierce deeper deceptions, perception of magical nature intensifies.
**4.6.4** *Vision* - Personal illusion: Create a prophetic vision, false memory, or symbolic dream in one creature's mind. **Instant** Plant a brief prophetic flash or symbolic moment in one creature within touch range. The vision feels deeply significant and memorable to the target. **Active** Create an extended visionary experience with detailed symbolic narrative. You can craft the vision to convey warnings, inspiration, false information, or guidance, though complex details may be distorted by the recipient's interpretation. The target believes they experienced a genuine supernatural revelation unless given strong reason to doubt. **Level** vision complexity and emotional impact.
**4.6.5** *Visual Illusion* - Static images: Create a fixed visual illusion up to 10 feet in each dimension. **Instant** Make a simple visual change for immediate deception. The illusion has no physical presence and provides no actual concealment, only visual deception revealed by touch or examination. **Active** Create a permanent visual alteration that persists until dispelled or destroyed. The illusion cannot move or change but can depict any static scene you desire with perfect visual detail. Common uses include disguising doors as walls, making walls appear as windows, or creating false decoration. **Level** visual fidelity and detail become exceptional, can create larger static scenes, deception becomes more thorough and convincing.
**4.6.6** *X-Ray Vision* - See through matter: See through solid objects within 30 feet as if they were transparent glass. **Instant** Perform a quick x-ray scan to check one specific area or object. The vision penetrates up to 3 feet of stone, 1 foot of metal, or any thickness of wood or similar organic material. **Active** Maintain sustained x-ray vision allowing continuous observation throughout the encounter. You can look through walls, doors, containers, and clothing to observe what lies beyond or within. Living tissue appears as shadowy outlines, allowing you to see hidden weapons, injuries, or concealed objects. **Level** penetrating vision becomes more powerful and clear, can see through denser materials, perception detail improves dramatically.
