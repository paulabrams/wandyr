# Random Traps - Mechanical

*Roll 1d20 for Dwur-engineered mechanical traps.*

1. **Collapsing Ceiling** - Pressure plate; 3d6 crushing damage; Detection DC 12
2. **Swinging Hammer** - Tripwire; 2d8 bludgeoning, knockdown; Detection DC 13
3. **Dart Thrower** - Door handle; 1d4 + poison (save or sleep); Detection DC 14
4. **Rolling Boulder** - Door opening; 4d6 crushing, fills corridor; Detection DC 11
5. **Spike Pit** - False floor; 2d6 piercing, 10ft deep; Detection DC 15
6. **Gas Vent** - Chest opening; CON save or stunned 1d4 rounds; Detection DC 13
7. **Blade Wall** - Stepping stones; 2d4 slashing per failed save; Detection DC 16
8. **Weighted Net** - Doorway; entangled, STR 16 to break free; Detection DC 12
9. **Alarm Bell** - Movement; summons wandering monster; Detection DC 14
10. **Crushing Walls** - Magic word; 1d6 per round until escape; Detection DC 15
11. **Spinning Blades** - Lever pull; 3d4 slashing, blocks passage; Detection DC 13
12. **Lock Needle** - Lock picking; 1 damage + poison (paralysis); Detection DC 17
13. **Falling Cage** - Ceiling trigger; trapped in iron bars; Detection DC 14
14. **Swinging Axe** - Door opening; 2d6 slashing, decapitation risk; Detection DC 15
15. **Poison Dart** - Floor tile; 1d6 + poison (save or blindness); Detection DC 16
16. **Flame Jet** - Chest opening; 2d6 fire damage; Detection DC 13
17. **Scythe Trap** - Walking through; 3d4 slashing, automatic hit; Detection DC 14
18. **Acid Spray** - Door handle; 2d6 acid damage; Detection DC 15
19. **Arrow Volley** - Pressure plate; 1d8 piercing each, multiple arrows; Detection DC 16
20. **Pendulum Blade** - Movement sensor; 2d6 slashing, continuous; Detection DC 17

## Disarming

- **Thieves Tools** required for most traps
- **STR checks** can force/break some mechanisms  
- **Dwur Engineering** knowledge gives +2 bonus
- **Triggering safely** possible with careful study
- **Bypassing** may be easier than disarming
