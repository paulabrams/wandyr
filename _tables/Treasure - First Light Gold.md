## Stages of Gold Madness (Progressive Effects)

*How exposure to First Light gold affects the mind*

### Stage 1: Initial Contact (First hour)

- **Fascination** - Cannot stop looking at/touching the gold
- **Whispers** - Faint voices speaking in unknown languages  
- **Flashes** - Brief glimpses of unfamiliar faces/places
- **Compulsion** - Must acquire more gold items

### Stage 2: Memory Bleeding (First day)

- **Confusion** - Mixing up current identity with past ones
- **Language Shifts** - Speaking words from previous lives
- **Skill Bleed** - Knowing things never learned in this life
- **Emotional Storms** - Grief/rage from ancient losses

### Stage 3: Identity Fracture (First week)

- **Multiple Personalities** - Cycling through past identities
- **Species Confusion** - Believing you're Dwur/Elf when human
- **Temporal Displacement** - Living in multiple time periods
- **Moral Collapse** - Unable to reconcile contradictory ethics

### Stage 4: Total Dissolution (Permanent)

- **Soul Scatter** - Consciousness lost in infinite memories
- **Body Abandonment** - Physical form becomes empty shell
- **Gold Hunger** - Craving more items to "complete" the collection
- **Final Warning** - Last coherent act is warning others

## Protection Methods (Known to Thornbeard Circle)

*Ways to handle First Light gold safely*

- **Iron Gloves** - Prevents skin contact (reduces effects by 50%)
- **Blessed Silver** - Wrap items in consecrated metal (delays onset)
- **Mountain Salt** - Circle items with pure salt (weakens aura)
- **Running Water** - Rinse gold in stream (temporary cleansing)
- **Moonlight** - Store under full moon (slows corruption)
- **Distance** - Keep 10+ feet away (prevents proximity effects)

**WARNING:** No method provides complete protection. The only true safety is to avoid First Light gold entirely.
