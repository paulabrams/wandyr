# Weather Patterns

Roll 2d6 for weather conditions and their effects:

**1.1** Clear Skies - Perfect visibility and travel  
**1.2** Light Rain - Gentle moisture for crops  
**1.3** Heavy Storm - Dangerous but cleansing  
**1.4** Fog Bank - Reduced visibility and mystery  
**1.5** Strong Winds - Difficult travel, clear air  
**1.6** Calm Air - Still and peaceful conditions

**2.1** Hot Sun - Heat exhaustion risk, good for drying  
**2.2** Cool Breeze - Comfortable temperatures  
**2.3** Freezing Cold - Frostbite risk, preserves food  
**2.4** Warm Humidity - Uncomfortable but fertile  
**2.5** Dry Heat - Dehydration risk, good for storage  
**2.6** Mild Temperatures - Perfect for all activities

**3.1** Thunderstorm - Lightning strikes, dramatic  
**3.2** Snowfall - Travel difficult, beautiful landscape  
**3.3** Hailstorm - Dangerous to crops and animals  
**3.4** Sandstorm - Visibility zero, abrasive  
**3.5** Ice Storm - Slippery conditions, damage  
**3.6** Gentle Breeze - Pleasant and refreshing

**4.1** Drought - Crops fail, water scarce  
**4.2** Flooding - Travel impossible, damage  
**4.3** Wildfire - Rapid spread, destruction  
**4.4** Avalanche - Mountain danger, burial  
**4.5** Tornado - Destructive winds, chaos  
**4.6** Hurricane - Coastal destruction, evacuation

**5.1** Acid Rain - Corrosive, dangerous  
**5.2** Magical Storm - Reality warps, unpredictable  
**5.3** Time Distortion - Past and future blur  
**5.4** Dimensional Rift - Otherworldly creatures emerge  
**5.5** Memory Storm - Forgotten events surface  
**5.6** Prophecy Weather - Future glimpsed in sky

**6.1** Golden Hour - Perfect light for magic  
**6.2** Silver Night - Moonlight reveals secrets  
**6.3** Blood Dawn - Dark omens in sky  
**6.4** Rainbow Bridge - Hope and promise  
**6.5** Star Shower - Wishes granted to pure hearts  
**6.6** Aurora Display - Northern lights show hidden paths
