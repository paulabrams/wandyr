# Vitae (Life) Spells

*Life force, growth, and harmony. The magic of the Elves, written in living ink that grows and changes like vines. The hallow channel beneficial energies, promote growth, and keep nature's balance.*

**1.1.1** *Animal Bond* \- Form lasting connection: Create an empathic link with one animal you can see within 30 feet. **Level** (affects number of animals you can bond with). **Instant** (1 action): Gain immediate insight into the animal's current emotions and basic intentions. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ongoing communication with the bonded animals, allowing you to share simple concepts like danger, food, or friendship. The sustained connection requires concentration and ends if you move more than 100 feet from any bonded animal.

**1.1.2** *Animal Friendship* \- Benevolent control: Calm agitated beasts or make neutral animals friendly toward you. **Level** (affects number of animals). **Instant** (1 action): Immediately calm agitated animals or gain momentary trust from neutral ones. **Active** (2+ actions): The affected animals regard you as a trusted friend throughout the encounter. The target animals must be able to see and hear you, and they cannot be hostile when you cast this spell.

**1.1.3** *Balance Weave* \- Unite opposites: Sense disruptions in natural harmony around you. **Level** (natural awareness becomes more sensitive and far-reaching, can detect subtler imbalances, harmony perception deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Get a quick impression of any major imbalances present in the area. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously perceive the presence of poisons, diseases, curses, or unnatural corruption as faint colored auras. You can identify the general type of imbalance but not specific details.

**1.1.4** *Beast Bond* \- Create long-lasting bond: Form magical connections with domesticated animals. **Level** (affects number of animals). **Instant** (1 action): Establish contact and exchange brief emotional impressions or sensations. **Active** (2+ actions): Share basic emotions and sensations with the bonded creatures across any distance throughout the scene. The animals can send you simple impressions of what they see, hear, or smell.

**1.1.5** *Beast Form* \- Friendly transformation: Take on minor physical traits of animals you have touched recently. **Level** (affects number of traits). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly manifest animal traits for a single action or ability check. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the transformations throughout the encounter, gaining multiple enhancements like wolf hearing, cat night vision, or squirrel climbing claws. You retain your normal mental abilities and cannot gain supernatural animal powers.

**1.1.6** *Beast Speech* \- Talk with animals: Speak with natural animals as if you shared a common language. **Level** (affects number of animals you can speak with simultaneously). **Instant** (1 action): Exchange a few quick words or concepts with the animals. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ongoing conversations throughout the scene, limited by each animal's intelligence and attention span. Most animals can share basic information about recent events, dangers, or the location of food and water.

**1.2.1** *Divine Grace* \- Channel blessing: Call upon benevolent spirits to bless yourself or one ally within 30 feet. **Level** (affects number of creatures blessed). **Instant** (1 action): Grant advantage on the target's very next action. **Active** (2+ actions): The blessed creature gains advantage on all actions taken during the encounter, manifesting as a faint golden shimmer. You can only maintain one Divine Grace at a time, and casting it again moves the blessing to a new target.

**1.2.2** *Healing Touch* \- Cure wounds: Restore 1 Heart to any living creature through gentle physical contact. **Level** (Levels spent = Hearts healed). **Instant** (1 action): Immediate healing as warm, golden light flows from your hands. **Active** (2+ actions): Provide ongoing regenerative energy that heals an additional 1 Heart at the start of each subsequent turn during the encounter. This spell can also stabilize a dying creature, bringing them to 1 Heart if they were below 0.

**1.2.3** *Joy Song* \- Lift spirits: Sing or hum a melody that lifts the spirits of all allies within 20 feet who can hear you. **Level** (musical influence spreads further and becomes more uplifting, joy effects intensify, emotional resonance improves). **Instant** (1 action): Immediately remove fear effects and grant momentary optimism. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the uplifting melody throughout the encounter, granting resistance to despair or hopelessness. Affected creatures feel genuinely happier and more optimistic about their situation.

**1.2.4** *Life Bloom* \- Encourage growth: Touch any plant and cause it to experience rapid, healthy growth. **Level** (affects size/speed of growth). **Instant** (1 action): Flowers bloom instantly or fruit ripens immediately. **Active** (2+ actions): Sustained growth that can sprout seeds into saplings or produce enough fresh food for one person's meal from suitable plants. Dead or diseased plants cannot be affected by this spell.

**1.2.5** *Light Shield* \- Protect from evil: Create a faint aura of protective light around yourself or one ally within touch range. **Level** (affects number of creatures protected). **Instant** (1 action): Briefly flare with protective light, disrupting one incoming attack from an evil creature. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a ward that provides resistance against attacks from undead, demons, or other evil creatures throughout the encounter. Evil beings find it uncomfortable to approach within 5 feet of the protected creature.

**1.2.6** *Pacify* \- Remove violence: Calm aggressive emotions in all creatures within a 15-foot radius who can see you. **Level** (peaceful influence spreads further and becomes more compelling, aggression dampening intensifies, tranquility effects deepen). **Instant** (1 action): Momentarily defuse immediate tension or halt an aggressive action. **Active** (2+ actions): Affected beings must make an effort to initiate combat or violence throughout the scene. The effect ends immediately if any creature within the area takes damage.

**1.3.1** *Peace Aura* \- Calm violence: Project an aura of tranquility in a 20-foot radius that discourages violence and aggression. **Level** (peaceful aura spreads further and becomes more compelling, tranquility effects deepen, violence resistance strengthens). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief moment of clarity that allows one creature to reconsider a violent action. **Active** (2+ actions): Creatures within the area find it difficult to initiate combat and prefer to seek peaceful solutions throughout the scene. The effect ends if you take a hostile action.

**1.3.2** *Snail Knight* \- Summon noble mount: Call forth a giant garden snail the size of a pony to serve as your mount. **Level** (affects snail's size and Hearts). **Instant** (1 action): The snail appears briefly to carry you a short distance or provide quick transport. **Active** (2+ actions): The snail remains for the entire encounter, moving slowly (half normal speed) but carrying you and your equipment safely over any terrain. It has 3 Hearts and natural armor that reduces damage by 1 point.

**1.3.3** *Star Guide* \- Find true path: Ask the stars for guidance about your current journey or quest. **Level** (affects detail and range of guidance). **Instant** (1 action): Receive a simple yes/no answer or brief directional sense. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ongoing celestial guidance throughout the scene, providing continuous directional awareness and insight into the natural path ahead. The stars can only provide guidance about natural paths and destinations, not specific people or hidden locations.

**1.3.4** *Sun Beam* \- Channel daylight: Create a ray of warm sunlight that extends 30 feet from your hand. **Level** (affects range and damage: +1d6 per Level). **Instant** (1 action): Flash of brilliant sunlight that illuminates an area and deals 1d6 damage to undead creatures. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a continuous beam that illuminates dark areas as bright as noon and provides comfortable warmth throughout the encounter. Undead creatures in the light feel uncomfortable and take ongoing damage.

**1.3.5** *Thicket* \- Grow healthy plants: Rapidly grow a barrier of thorny bushes in a 10-foot square area. **Level** (plant growth becomes more vigorous and extensive, thorns grow denser and stronger, barrier durability improves). **Instant** (1 action): Create a temporary barrier that lasts only moments but can block immediate movement. **Active** (2+ actions): Grow a permanent thicket 5 feet tall that blocks movement unless creatures spend extra effort to push through. The thorns deal 1d6 damage to anyone forcing their way through carelessly.

**1.3.6** *Truth Sight* \- See through lies: Gain the ability to detect lies and simple deceptions. **Level** (affects number of people monitored). **Instant** (1 action): Immediately know if the last statement you heard was a deliberate falsehood. **Active** (2+ actions): Throughout the encounter, when someone speaks a deliberate falsehood in your presence, you see a faint red shimmer around them. The spell reveals intentional lies but not mistakes, omissions, or cleverly worded truths.

**1.4.1** *Ward* \- Create protective barrier: Create a small protective barrier of life energy around yourself or an ally within 30 feet. **Level** (affects number of effects absorbed). **Instant** (1 action): Absorb one incoming harmful magical effect immediately. **Active** (2+ actions): The ward appears as a faint green shimmer and absorbs multiple harmful magical effects throughout the encounter. Multiple wards cannot be placed on the same creature.

**1.4.2** *Cure Disease* \- Remove ailments: Remove one natural disease, poison, or infection from yourself or a creature you touch. **Level** (affects number of creatures or severity of ailment). **Instant** (1 action): Provide immediate relief and begin the purging process. **Active** (2+ actions): Your life magic thoroughly purges the harmful agents over several minutes, ensuring complete recovery. This spell cannot cure magical diseases, curses, or conditions caused by supernatural creatures.

**1.4.3** *Forest Touch* \- Speak with plants: Touch any plant and communicate with it as if it were a simple, friendly creature. **Level** (affects number of plants contacted). **Instant** (1 action): Exchange a few basic concepts or get a quick impression of recent events. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain ongoing conversation throughout the scene where plants can share information about recent events they witnessed, weather patterns, and the health of the local ecosystem. Trees and older plants provide more detailed information than grass or flowers.

**1.4.4** *Growing Script* \- Living magical writing: Write words with your finger that appear as living vines growing across any natural surface. **Level** (affects length and complexity of message). **Instant** (1 action): Create simple, static text that conveys your basic message. **Active** (2+ actions): The text slowly changes and evolves throughout the scene, conveying different aspects of your intended message in real-time. The script glows faintly green and feels warm to the touch.

**1.4.5** *Nature's Blessing* \- Enhance vitality: Call upon local nature spirits to provide minor assistance and guidance. **Level** (nature spirit aid becomes more generous and responsive, assistance becomes more detailed and helpful, spiritual connection strengthens). **Instant** (1 action): Receive immediate insight about fresh water, edible plants, or natural shelter in the area. **Active** (2+ actions): Gain advantage on survival-related actions throughout the encounter as spirits manifest as helpful animals, favorable weather, or convenient natural formations.

**1.4.6** *Purification* \- Remove toxins: Cleanse up to one gallon of water, food, or air of natural contamination and impurities. **Level** (purification becomes more thorough and extensive, can cleanse larger quantities, contamination resistance improves). **Instant** (1 action): Quickly neutralize immediate contamination in a small amount of substance. **Active** (2+ actions): Thoroughly purify larger quantities, neutralizing bacteria, parasites, and mild poisons over several minutes. The magic cannot affect magical corruption or supernatural effects.

**1.5.1** *Regeneration* \- Speed natural healing: Accelerate the natural healing processes in yourself or another living creature through touch. **Level** (Levels spent = Hearts healed per turn). **Instant** (1 action): The target regains 1 Heart immediately as wounds begin to close. **Active** (2+ actions): The target regains 1 additional Heart at the start of each subsequent turn during the encounter, with enhanced healing appearing as a soft green glow around wounds.

**1.5.2** *Heart Glyph* \- Inscribe emotions: Inscribe a magical symbol that conveys your current emotional state to anyone who sees it. **Level** (affects complexity and duration of emotional display). **Instant** (1 action): Create a simple glyph that shows your immediate feelings. **Active** (2+ actions): The glyph continues to shift and change colors throughout the encounter, reflecting your evolving emotional state in real-time. Others who view the symbol understand your emotions and can respond accordingly.

**1.5.3** *Nature's Bounty* \- Create food/water: Manifest fresh, nourishing food and clean water from the ambient life force around you. **Level** (sustenance creation becomes more abundant and nourishing, can feed more people, food quality improves dramatically). **Instant** (1 action): Create a quick snack or drink to satisfy immediate hunger or thirst. **Active** (2+ actions): Gradually manifest enough sustenance for up to 4 people throughout the encounter, appearing as fruits, nuts, berries, and pure spring water perfectly suited to local tastes and dietary needs.

**1.5.4** *Pack Call* \- Summon animal allies: Call 2-3 small woodland creatures to assist you with simple tasks. **Level** (animal allies become more numerous and helpful, creature variety increases, natural bond strengthens). **Instant** (1 action): Animals appear briefly to deliver a message or perform one quick task. **Active** (2+ actions): The animals remain throughout the encounter, including squirrels, rabbits, or birds depending on the local environment. They can carry messages, gather information, or perform basic chores but will not fight or enter dangerous areas.

**1.5.5** *Vital Surge* \- Boost life force: Channel raw life energy into plants, small animals, or willing creatures within 30 feet. **Level** (life energy channeling becomes more powerful and extensive, can invigorate more targets, vitality effects intensify). **Instant** (1 action): Provide immediate invigoration, granting advantage on the next physical action taken. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain a continuous flow of life energy throughout the encounter, causing wilted plants to perk up, tired animals to regain energy, and people to feel refreshed. You can affect up to 6 targets simultaneously.

**1.5.6** *Wild Empathy* \- Share animal feelings: Open your mind to the emotional states and basic intentions of all wild animals within 100 feet. **Level** (empathic range expands naturally, emotional sensitivity deepens, can understand more complex animal thoughts). **Instant** (1 action): Get a quick snapshot of the general emotional state of nearby animals. **Active** (2+ actions): Continuously sense their fear, curiosity, hunger, or contentment as a background emotional landscape throughout the encounter. This awareness helps you understand animal behavior and anticipate potential threats or opportunities.

**1.6.1** *Life Transfer* \- Share vital force: Transfer some of your own life energy to heal another creature within touch range. **Level** (affects transfer rate: 1 Heart per Level spent). **Instant** (1 action): Give up 1 of your own Hearts to restore 1 to the target immediately. **Active** (2+ actions): Create an ongoing connection where you may transfer up to 3 of your Hearts throughout the encounter, appearing as a stream of golden light flowing between you and the recipient. This spell can bring someone back from the brink of death but cannot reduce your Hearts below 1.

**1.6.2** *Nature's Rhythm* \- Align with cycles: Attune yourself to the natural rhythms and cycles of the world around you. **Level** (natural attunement becomes more profound and comprehensive, rhythmic awareness deepens, seasonal sensitivity increases). **Instant** (1 action): Gain immediate insight into current weather patterns or optimal timing for one specific action. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain deep attunement throughout the encounter, gaining intuitive knowledge of weather patterns, optimal travel times, and seasonal changes. This alignment grants advantage on all survival and navigation checks.

**1.6.3** *Perfect Unity* \- Complete balance: Achieve a state of perfect harmony with your immediate environment within a 50-foot radius. **Level** (harmony extends further and becomes more profound, natural unity deepens, peaceful influence intensifies). **Instant** (1 action): Create a momentary wave of peace that stops all aggressive actions for one round. **Active** (2+ actions): Plants lean toward you in greeting, animals approach peacefully, and the air feels refreshing and pure throughout the encounter. All creatures in the area feel a strong compulsion toward peace and cooperation, making violence extremely difficult to initiate.

**1.6.4** *Season's Call* \- Control growth cycle: Shift the seasonal state of a 30-foot radius area to any season you choose. **Level** (seasonal influence spreads further and becomes more dramatic, can create more pronounced seasonal effects, nature manipulation deepens). **Instant** (1 action): Create a brief seasonal display, such as a momentary bloom of flowers or falling leaves. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the seasonal shift throughout the encounter, making spring flowers bloom in winter or autumn leaves fall in summer. The change affects only plant growth and appearance, not temperature or weather.

**1.6.5** *Tranquil Heart* \- Create inner peace: Create a profound sense of inner peace and emotional balance in yourself or another willing creature. **Level** (affects number of creatures calmed). **Instant** (1 action): Immediately calm overwhelming emotions and provide momentary clarity. **Active** (2+ actions): The target feels completely calm and free from stress, anxiety, fear, or anger throughout the encounter. This tranquility provides immunity to fear effects and advantage on all social interactions.

**1.6.6** *Vital Harmony* \- Balance life forces: Harmonize and balance the life energies within a 40-foot radius, promoting health and natural growth. **Level** (life force harmony spreads further and becomes more powerful, healing effects intensify, natural balance deepens). **Instant** (1 action): All living creatures in the area immediately regain 1 Heart as life energies align. **Active** (2+ actions): Maintain the harmonious field throughout the encounter where sick plants begin to recover, minor wounds heal more quickly, and the environment becomes more vibrant and alive.
