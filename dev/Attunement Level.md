# Attunement Level

When characters encounter a magical force, entity, location, or situation, the Guide may ask players to calculate their Attunement Level.

## Calculating Attunement

1. Guide describes the force/entity/situation
2. Player scans their character sheet for relevant advancements
3. Count all advancements that resonate (Traits, Skills, Spells, Names, etc.)
4. Use triangular lookup to find Attunement Level

## Triangular Lookup

| Advancements | Attunement Level |
|--------------|------------------|
| 1 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 6 | 3 |
| 10 | 4 |
| 15 | 5 |
| 21 | 6 |

## Examples

Dead Bird God Temple:
- Arcane Eye (bird-like sensor)
- Necromantia spells (death)
- Cursebreaker trait (death/undead)

Fire Elemental Shrine:
- Elementalis skill
- Dragon's Breath spell
- Fire Shield spell

Thieves' Guild Initiation:
- Thief trait
- Lockpicks skill
- Invisibility spell
- Canting spells

## Uses

- Caster Level: Attunement to a magic school determines safe spell level
- Divine Favor: Attunement to a god determines blessing strength
- Relic Power: Attunement to a relic's source determines effect
- Location Bonuses: Attunement to a place grants advantage or access

Higher attunement means deeper connection—for good or ill.
