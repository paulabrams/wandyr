# The Canting School Spells

*The flash patter and darkmans lay of the canting crew. Hedge magic learned in boozing kens and stamping grounds, passed down through brotherhood cant from upright men to wandering rogues. These are the arts of cony-catchers, foists, and rufflers - the practical skills needed to work the lay and live by one's wits in the world of honest men.*


**6.1.1** *Steady Fambles* - Calm your hands for delicate work. **Instant** Steady your fambles (hands) for one precise task like working a lock or cutting a bung (purse) without fumbling the lay. **Active** Maintain rock-steady fambles throughout the encounter, perfect for any work requiring delicate touch. **Level** Hand steadiness improves to supernatural precision, eventually extending to full body coordination.

**6.1.2** *Feel the Ward* - Sense lock mechanisms through touch. **Instant** Touch any ward (lock) to feel how its internal mechanism works, learning the best approach for a successful prig (theft). **Active** Maintain enhanced sensitivity for feeling the tumblers and springs in any mechanical device through your fambles. **Level** Lock sensitivity improves to detect magical wards and divine protections.

**6.1.3** *Darkmans Creeping* - Move unseen in shadow. **Instant** Slip 20 feet through the darkmans (darkness) without making a sound, timing your movement like a proper member of the canting crew. **Active** Maintain the silent step of an experienced rogue, moving like a ghost through familiar territory. **Level** Stealth improves until you can walk invisible even in lightmans (daylight).

**6.1.4** *Quiet Strike* - Reduce attack noise. **Instant** Make one attack with minimal noise, muffling weapon sounds and preventing your target from immediately crying out. **Active** Maintain reduced noise when fighting, knowing how to muffle impacts and strike to limit vocal reactions. **Level** Sound reduction improves, eventually achieving complete silence in combat.

**6.1.5** *Knot Work* - Basic rope skills. **Instant** Quickly tie or untie basic knots and handle rope efficiently for climbing or binding purposes. **Active** Maintain competent rope handling for standard thief tasks like climbing, securing items, or creating simple restraints. **Level** Rope skills improve to handle more complex knots and work with other flexible materials.

**6.1.6** *Basic Climb* - Improved climbing technique. **Instant** Climb difficult surfaces more easily by finding better handholds and using proper technique. **Active** Maintain improved climbing ability that helps you scale challenging but climbable surfaces more efficiently. **Level** Climbing skills improve to handle increasingly difficult surfaces and architectural challenges.

**6.2.1** *Soft Landing* - Professional falling technique. **Instant** Use proper technique to land safely from significant falls, reducing injury through expert rolling and positioning. **Active** Maintain professional falling skills that let you land safely from most heights that won't kill you outright. **Level** Falling techniques improve to handle extreme heights and impossible landing conditions.

**6.2.2** *Tool Mastery* - Professional tool use. **Instant** Use thief tools with expert technique, accomplishing tasks faster and more quietly than normal training allows. **Active** Maintain professional-level tool use that makes standard thief equipment more effective in your hands. **Level** Tool expertise improves to accomplish seemingly impossible tasks with standard equipment.

**6.2.3** *Counterfeit Crank* - Assume false affliction. **Instant** Instantly adopt the appearance and manner of a specific type of beggar or afflicted person, making honest folk pity rather than suspect you. **Active** Maintain convincing portrayal of a harmless unfortunate - lame soldier, mad tom, or diseased beggar - that deflects suspicion. **Level** Disguise mastery improves to perfectly mimic any social role or affliction.

**6.2.4** *Trap Spotter* - Notice obvious dangers. **Instant** Spot obvious signs of traps or hazards that most people would miss - suspicious floor tiles, unusual wall features, or telltale scratches. **Active** Maintain awareness for standard trap indicators and hazardous construction. **Level** Trap detection improves to spot increasingly subtle and well-concealed dangers.

**6.2.5** *Aimed Strike* - Better weapon accuracy. **Instant** Make one well-aimed attack that targets weak points in armor or strikes exactly where you intend. **Active** Maintain improved weapon accuracy that makes your attacks more likely to hit vital areas. **Level** Weapon precision improves to achieve seemingly impossible accuracy and targeting.

**6.2.6** *Wall Walking* - Advanced climbing skills. **Instant** Climb challenging surfaces using advanced techniques that let you ascend where others cannot. **Active** Maintain professional climbing skills that handle difficult architectural climbing challenges. **Level** Climbing ability improves to scale seemingly impossible surfaces and unusual structures.

**6.3.1** *Play the Gonnof* - Assume false identity. **Instant** Instantly adopt the manner and appearance of an honest citizen, making any harman beck (constable) or justice overlook you as just another cull (person). **Active** Maintain perfect concealment among the straight world, blending seamlessly with honest folk while working your lay. **Level** Identity concealment improves until even those who know your true face fail to recognize you.

**6.3.2** *Break the Ward* - Open any lock with cant knowledge. **Instant** Use brotherhood secrets to open any ward (lock), knowing the hidden weaknesses that all locks share from generations of rogue experience. **Active** Maintain mastery over all mechanical wards, using techniques passed down through the canting crew. No mundane lock can withstand your knowledge. **Level** Ward-breaking extends to magical protections and divine seals.

**6.3.3** *Decoy* - Create convincing distractions. **Instant** Use expert misdirection to create a convincing distraction - thrown objects that sound like footsteps, shadows that look like movement, or sounds that draw attention away. **Active** Maintain ongoing misdirection that keeps enemies confused about your location and actions. Create false trails, misleading sounds, and visual tricks that make pursuers look in the wrong places. **Level** Misdirection techniques become more sophisticated, creating increasingly convincing false impressions.

**6.3.4** *Escape Artist* - Slip restraints and bonds. **Instant** Use expert contortion and technique to escape from ropes, manacles, or similar restraints, or slip from someone's grasp. **Active** Maintain enhanced flexibility and escape techniques that make you extremely difficult to restrain. Dislocate joints safely, compress your body profile, and exploit any weakness in bonds or holds. **Level** Escape techniques improve to handle increasingly secure restraints and more challenging situations.

**6.3.5** *Sucker Punch* - Devastating surprise attack. **Instant** Make one perfectly timed surprise attack when your target is distracted or unaware, dealing maximum possible damage. **Active** Maintain perfect timing that lets you exploit every opening in combat. Strike when enemies are distracted, off-balance, or focused elsewhere, making every attack count for maximum effect. **Level** Combat timing improves to exploit increasingly brief opportunities and create more devastating surprise attacks.

**6.3.6** *The Foist's Art* - Master pickpocketing. **Instant** Execute a perfect dive (theft) on any cony (victim), lifting their bung (purse) or any small item without them feeling a thing. **Active** Maintain the supernatural dexterity of a master foist, working the crowd with fingers so quick they seem magical. **Level** Pickpocketing skills improve until you can steal anything from anyone, even items they're actively using.

**6.4.1** *Ghost Walk* - Move without trace. **Instant** Move through one area leaving absolutely no physical evidence of your passage - no footprints, scent trail, disturbed dust, or displaced objects. **Active** Maintain expert movement that leaves no trace anywhere you go. Step without disturbing surfaces, avoid leaving scent trails, and pass through areas without displacing air or creating any sign of your presence. **Level** Traceless movement becomes more comprehensive, eventually avoiding magical detection and supernatural tracking.

**6.4.2** *Wall Running* - Advanced climbing techniques. **Instant** Use momentum and expert technique to briefly run up walls, across ceilings, or along vertical surfaces for one critical movement. **Active** Maintain advanced parkour techniques that let you traverse urban environments like a natural habitat. Run up walls, leap between buildings, and use architectural features in ways that seem impossible. **Level** Movement techniques become more sophisticated, allowing navigation of increasingly challenging urban environments.

**6.4.3** *Find the Way* - Navigate any obstacle. **Instant** Find a path through any complex obstacle - locate hidden passages, discover structural weaknesses, or identify routes others would miss. **Active** Maintain expert navigation that finds paths through any environment. Spot hidden doors, identify climbable routes, and discover ways through seemingly impossible barriers using architectural knowledge. **Level** Navigation expertise improves to find increasingly obscure and difficult passages.

**6.4.4** *Perfect Timing* - Expert synchronization. **Instant** Time one action with perfect precision - pick a lock exactly when guards turn away, strike just as an enemy drops their guard, or move precisely when no one is watching. **Active** Maintain supernatural timing that makes every action perfectly synchronized. Know exactly when to move, strike, or act for maximum effectiveness and minimum detection. **Level** Timing expertise becomes more acute, eventually predicting and exploiting increasingly brief opportunities.

**6.4.5** *Deep Cover* - Long-term identity assumption. **Instant** Instantly assume the mannerisms, speech patterns, and general behavior of someone you've studied, becoming convincing enough to fool casual acquaintances. **Active** Maintain a deep cover identity with complete behavioral mimicry. Adopt all the target's habits, knowledge, and social relationships well enough to fool even close associates for extended periods. **Level** Identity assumption becomes more complete and convincing, eventually fooling even intimate partners and family members.

**6.4.6** *The Great Lay* - Coordinate complex criminal operations. **Instant** Organize a sophisticated lay (criminal scheme) involving multiple members of the brotherhood, each playing their part with perfect timing. **Active** Maintain coordination of elaborate criminal enterprises with foists, rufflers, stalls, and bulks all working together like a well-rehearsed canting crew. **Level** Criminal coordination improves to handle increasingly complex schemes involving entire brotherhoods.

**6.5.1** *The Brotherhood's Call* - Rally the canting crew. **Instant** Send word through the brotherhood to activate every rogue, ruffler, and wandering thief in your territory for one coordinated lay. **Active** Maintain command over an entire canting crew, directing complex operations through cant signals and brotherhood loyalty. Every member of the fraternity follows your commands. **Level** Brotherhood influence expands until upright men across multiple territories answer your call.

**6.5.2** *Impossible Theft* - Steal the unstealable. **Instant** Steal something that shouldn't be possible to take - a person's shadow, their reflection, their voice, or their luck. **Active** Maintain the ability to steal abstract qualities and intangible things. Take someone's confidence, steal their reputation, or pilfer their relationships. The thefts feel natural to everyone except the victim. **Level** Abstract theft becomes more comprehensive, eventually stealing concepts like time, memory, or identity.

**6.5.3** *Legendary Fence* - Sell anything to anyone. **Instant** Find a buyer for any object no matter how illegal, cursed, or impossible to sell, completing the transaction immediately. **Active** Maintain connections to buyers for literally anything. Sell stolen goods, forbidden knowledge, abstract concepts, or even things that don't belong to you. Transactions always complete successfully. **Level** Fencing operations become more sophisticated, eventually selling concepts, experiences, and fundamental aspects of reality.

**6.5.4** *Master of Disguise* - Become anyone perfectly. **Instant** Instantly assume the complete identity of any person you've studied - appearance, voice, mannerisms, memories, and even their relationships and social connections. **Active** Maintain perfect impersonation that fools everyone who knew the original person. Access their memories, relationships, and social status while retaining your own goals and personality underneath. **Level** Identity assumption becomes more complete, eventually copying skills, abilities, and even supernatural traits.

**6.5.5** *Everywhere at Once* - Simultaneous operations. **Instant** Appear to be in multiple places simultaneously, allowing you to commit crimes across a wide area while maintaining perfect alibis. **Active** Maintain the ability to conduct operations in multiple locations at once. Plan and execute simultaneous heists, maintain multiple identities, and appear to be in several places at the same time. **Level** Simultaneous presence becomes more extensive, eventually operating across multiple cities and maintaining dozens of parallel lives.

**6.5.6** *Crime Lord* - Command all criminal activity. **Instant** Issue commands that will be obeyed by every criminal, thief, spy, and scoundrel in a major city, coordinating their activities to serve your purposes. **Active** Maintain absolute authority over all criminal organizations in multiple cities. Every illegal operation, underground network, and criminal enterprise follows your directives and pays you tribute. **Level** Criminal authority expands to encompass entire kingdoms and eventually influences criminal activity across multiple realms.

**6.6.1** *Perfect Crime* - Leave no evidence. **Instant** Commit any crime in such a way that no evidence of your involvement can ever be found - all traces disappear, witnesses forget, and records alter themselves. **Active** Maintain the ability to commit crimes that retroactively never happened. Evidence vanishes, witnesses develop false memories, and official records change to support your innocence. **Level** Evidence elimination becomes more comprehensive, eventually erasing crimes from history itself and collective memory.

**6.6.2** *Master of All Trades* - Every criminal skill perfected. **Instant** Demonstrate perfect mastery of any criminal skill at the moment you need it - expert lockpicking, flawless forgery, perfect disguise, or supernatural stealth. **Active** Maintain perfect expertise in every aspect of criminal activity. Master every thief skill, criminal technique, and underground trade simultaneously while adapting instantly to new challenges. **Level** Criminal mastery becomes truly comprehensive, eventually including skills that don't technically exist and capabilities beyond normal human limits.

**6.6.3** *The Arch-Rogue* - Ultimate criminal authority. **Instant** Issue commands in the high cant that will be obeyed by every upright man, ruffler, and member of the canting crew across multiple kingdoms. **Active** Maintain the legendary authority of the Arch-Rogue, the mythical king of all rogues whose word is law in every criminal brotherhood. **Level** Criminal dominion transcends normal reality, commanding rogues across multiple planes of existence.

**6.6.4** *Steal History* - Rewrite the past. **Instant** Steal one historical event, making it never have happened while you gain credit for its positive effects or avoid blame for its negative consequences. **Active** Maintain ongoing theft of historical events and their consequences. Rewrite the past to serve your purposes, stealing credit for great achievements while erasing your failures and crimes from history. **Level** Historical theft becomes more comprehensive, eventually rewriting entire timelines and fundamental aspects of how events unfolded.

**6.6.5** *Steal Everything* - Universal theft. **Instant** Steal any concept, object, quality, or aspect of reality from anyone or anything, making it permanently yours while they lose it forever. **Active** Maintain ongoing universal theft that makes everything in existence potentially yours. Steal beauty, intelligence, power, memories, relationships, or even abstract concepts like justice or time. **Level** Universal theft becomes truly absolute, eventually allowing you to steal the fundamental nature of existence itself.

**6.6.6** *The Master of the Cant* - Perfect criminal knowledge. **Instant** Demonstrate perfect mastery of any criminal art known to the canting crew, executing any lay with supernatural skill and guaranteed success. **Active** Maintain absolute expertise in every aspect of the criminal arts, knowing every secret of the brotherhood and every technique of the rogues. You become the living embodiment of centuries of criminal knowledge. **Level** Criminal mastery becomes truly legendary - you know every secret ever whispered in cant and can accomplish any feat ever dreamed of by the brotherhood.
