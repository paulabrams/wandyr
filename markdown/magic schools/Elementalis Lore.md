## The Baal School of Elementalis

### Origins and Philosophy
Elementalis magic originates from the ancient Draconian Empire of the Baal, whose remnants still tower above the land like stone islands around the great city of Akkar in the southeast. The Baal were the first to learn dragon speech and discover that the primal forces of creation could be commanded through properly spoken draconic incantations carved in enduring stone.

The philosophy of Elementalis holds that all magic stems from the four fundamental forces: Fire (creation and destruction), Water (flow and adaptation), Earth (stability and endurance), and Air (change and movement). The dragons taught that these elements exist in constant tension, and a master of Elementalis learns to balance and direct these competing forces rather than dominating them.

Unlike other schools that work with subtle energies, Elementalis practitioners embrace the raw, visible power of nature itself. They believe magic should be as obvious as lightning and as permanent as carved stone, leaving clear evidence of the caster's mastery for all to witness.

### Practitioners and Titles
**Dragon Priests**: The highest rank of Baal Elementalis practitioners, these individuals serve in the great stone temples of Akkar and claim direct lineage to the ancient Draconian Empire. They wear ceremonial masks carved to resemble dragon faces and speak only in formal draconic during rituals.

**Stormcallers**: Specialists in weather magic who often serve as navigators and guides for trading vessels crossing the treacherous Dragon Sea. They can read the winds and summon favorable currents for ships brave enough to navigate the hidden rocky "teeth" that lurk beneath the mists.

**Forgemasters**: Elementalis practitioners who focus on earth and fire magic, working closely with smiths and builders. They can superheat forges with a gesture and shape stone as easily as clay, making them invaluable in construction and metalworking.

**Windwalkers**: Masters of air magic who have learned to ride the thermal currents and speak with the spirits of the sky. Many serve as messengers between distant cities or scouts for military forces.

**Tidebinders**: Water specialists who work along coastlines and rivers, controlling currents and purifying water sources. They often serve aboard Southmaren ships or in the great port cities.

### Dress and Appearance
Elementalis practitioners favor clothing that reflects their chosen elemental focus, but all wear stone ornaments carved with draconic runes. Fire specialists dress in reds and golds with bronze fittings, often wearing fireproof leather treated with dragon's blood. Water mages prefer flowing blues and greens with silver accents, while earth practitioners wear browns and grays with iron or copper details. Air specialists favor light colors and flowing fabrics that move with every breeze.

All serious Elementalis practitioners bear ritual brands or tattoos in draconic script that mark their advancement in the school. These marks often glow faintly when channeling elemental power, and the most accomplished masters can make their brands flare with actual elemental energy - fire brands that burn, ice brands that frost over, or lightning brands that spark.

### Sacred Components and Materials
**Dragon's Blood**: The most prized component, this crimson liquid (not always from actual dragons) serves as both ink for inscribing runes and as a catalyst for fire magic. True dragon's blood glows when heated and never fully dries.

**Stormglass**: Crystalline formations found only in areas repeatedly struck by lightning. When properly cut and polished, these crystals can store electrical energy and amplify air and weather magic.

**Primordial Salt**: Harvested from ancient dry lake beds or deep ocean trenches, this salt contains trace minerals from the world's creation. It's used to stabilize elemental bindings and create protective circles.

**Volcanic Ash**: Collected from active volcanoes, this ash represents the marriage of earth and fire. When mixed with water, it forms a clay perfect for creating temporary magical items.

**Windcaptured Flames**: Specially prepared candles that burn with different colored flames depending on air pressure and weather conditions. They serve as both components and weather prediction tools.

**Depth Stones**: River rocks that have been carried by water for decades, worn perfectly smooth. Each stone contains the memory of every current that shaped it and can be used to divine water sources or predict floods.

### Traditions and Practices
Elementalis practitioners begin each day with the "Fourfold Salutation," facing each cardinal direction and speaking a draconic phrase to honor the elemental forces. Many maintain personal shrine chambers with alcoves dedicated to each element - a brazier for fire, a bowl of ever-flowing water, a pot of sacred earth, and wind chimes for air.

The most important ceremony is the "Dragon's Awakening," held during the spring and autumn equinoxes when elemental forces are most balanced. Practitioners gather at stone circles or temple complexes to perform group rituals that can influence weather patterns across entire regions.

Master Elementalis practitioners often undertake the "Pilgrimage of Storms," traveling to locations where natural elemental forces are strongest - active volcanoes, hurricane-lashed coasts, deep caves, or mountain peaks. These journeys are considered essential for advancing beyond basic mastery of the school.
