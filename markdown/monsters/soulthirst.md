# Soulthirst

**Level 4** - Grim enchanted weapon of the Fallen Eldar

**DESCRIPTION** - A long-handled mace forged from void crystal, a transparent material that contains swirling darkness within. The weapon appears to contain living shadows that move and writhe within its transparent surface, embodying the weapon's motto: "The night hungers."

**Hearts:** 36
**Move:** 40'
**Attacks:** 2 claws + 1 bite (deadly)
**Armor:** Thick fur

**Soul Drain:** Once per encounter, the barghest can exhale a cone of putrid breath that extinguishes all torches, lanterns, and open flames within 40'. Range: 40' cone.

**Mountain Camouflage:** In snowy terrain, the barghest can hide with 90% effectiveness and gains surprise on 1-4 on d6.

**Cold Resistance:** Immune to cold.

Found in high mountain passes and ice caves. Territorial and aggressive. Uses breath to create darkness before attacking.

---

## Weapon Properties

**Type:** Long-handled mace with extended reach
**Material:** Void crystal - transparent material showing darkness within
**Enchantment:** Grim enchanted weapon (not talkative)
**Damage:** Deadly bite attack (3d6 Hearts)
**Special:** Soul drain - steals life force from wounded targets

## History

**Original Owner:** Jaelith the Life-Drainer (#17 Fallen Eldar)
- **Race:** Avathar (vampiric Eldar)
- **Appearance:** Sickly yellow with pale hair
- **Magic School:** Necromantia
- **Spells:** Death Touch, Vampiric Touch, Life Sense
- **Desire:** To feel their own heartbeat

**Creation:** Forged as a life-draining weapon to feed Jaelith's vampiric nature before the Baal-Dwur alliance.

**Corruption:** Corrupted by First Light gold during the ancient wars, causing progressive gold madness in wielders.

**Abandonment:** Left behind when Jaelith became undead, stored by Dwur as dangerous artifact.

## Current Status

**Location:** Dwur armory in Vault of First Light
**Danger Level:** High - attracts shadow creatures and other beings of darkness
**Resonance:** Still connected to Jaelith's undead presence in vault depths
**Purpose:** Stored to keep away from Fallen Eldar who seek such powerful artifacts

## Wielder Considerations

**Advantages:**
- Extended reach for keeping enemies at bay
- Soul drain ability for life force absorption
- Void crystal construction for shadow magic
- Pre-war craftsmanship quality

**Disadvantages:**
- Attracts shadow creatures and undead
- Corrupted by First Light gold
- Connected to Fallen Eldar presence
- Dangerous to carry in populated areas

**Compatible Users:**
- Characters aligned with darkness/shadow
- Those seeking vampiric abilities
- Users of Umbrakallo or Necromantia magic
- Characters willing to risk corruption

**Incompatible Users:**
- Characters aligned with light/good
- Those avoiding shadow creatures
- Users of Vitae or Elementalis magic
- Characters concerned about corruption
