# Card Oracle Rules

## Setup
Use a standard deck of 52 playing cards. Each turn, the Guide draws one card and places it face-up on the table in a growing line. Interpret the combination of all visible cards together.

## Quick Interpretation Guide

### Suits - Environmental Themes
- **Hearts** ❤️ - Social energy, emotions, relationships, community
- **Diamonds** ♦️ - Wealth, trade, material concerns, opportunities
- **Clubs** ♣️ - Growth, nature, wilderness, organic threats
- **Spades** ♠️ - Conflict, danger, death, sharp challenges

### Numbers - Intensity & Development
- **Ace (1)** - New beginnings, subtle hints, first signs
- **2-4** - Building pressure, early manifestation
- **5-7** - Clear presence, active influence
- **8-10** - Strong impact, demanding attention
- **Jack** - Unpredictable element, wild card
- **Queen** - Refined influence, elegant complexity
- **King** - Dominant force, major presence

## Reading the Line

### Single Card
Interpret the suit's theme at the number's intensity level.

### Building Combinations
- **Same Suit** - The theme intensifies and develops
- **Alternating Colors** - Conflicting forces, tension
- **Sequential Numbers** - Gradual progression, natural flow
- **Face Cards Together** - Important NPCs or major events
- **All Red/All Black** - Harmony vs. foreboding

### Weather & Atmosphere Examples

**Hearts 2 → Hearts 5 → Clubs 7**
- Gathering of friendly travelers → Growing camaraderie → Lush forest sanctuary

**Spades Ace → Spades 4 → Diamonds 8**
- Distant storm clouds → Cold wind picks up → Valuable shelter found

**Clubs 3 → Hearts Jack → Spades Queen**
- Morning mist → Unexpected ally appears → Elegant but dangerous pursuer

- 5-7: "Gathering crowds"

## Foreshadowing Technique

Start with low numbers to plant seeds, let higher numbers bring them to fruition. Face cards introduce major elements that reshape the entire situation.

*Trust your instincts - the cards provide texture, not rigid outcomes.*
