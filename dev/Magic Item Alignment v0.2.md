# Magic Item Alignment

In Wandyr, your alignment to a magic item determeins how well you can wield its power. Each magic item has an alignment rating from up to 6, representing the depth of your connection to the item's magic.

## Alignment

When you find a magic item, you will need to determine your Alignment with it based on the description of the item. For example, an evil sword with red runes that react to blood will respond better to a character with a dark or violent nature, skills in the sword, Necromantia, etc.

## Powers by Alignment Level

The item will have powers corresponding to Alignment levels. You can only access powers up to your current Alignment level with the item. For example, if you have Alignment 2 with a magic sword, you can use its Level 1 and Level 2 powers, but not Level 3 or higher.

Your understanding of powers is also based on Alignment. Anything above your level is either hidden from you or kept as a mystery until you reach that level. For example, if the Guide mentions a level 3 power, but you are only Alignment 2, you may not know what that power does or how to activate it, and the Guide is allowed to change the power at any time until you unlock it. In other words, you may not know what you don't know.

## Magical Writing

Magic items have writing on them corresponding to one of the magic schools. You can only read the writing if you have skill in that magic school, and your ability to understand how that defines powers of the item is based on your Alignment with the item.

## Power Types

Magic items can have three types of powers:

**Arcana Spells:** If a magic item contains spells written in magical script, it functions as an **Arcana**. You cast spells from it using the normal spell rules, paying Hearts equal to the spell's level. The item's Alignment determines which spells you can access.

**Properties:** Passive effects that are always active or trigger automatically. These don't cost Hearts but may have Spicy/Double Spicy drawbacks.

**Knacks:** Special activated abilities unique to the item. These cost Hearts (like spells) and may have Spicy/Double Spicy drawbacks.

**Per Muster:** Activated abilities that can be used once per Muster instead of costing Hearts. These may have Spicy/Double Spicy drawbacks.

## Stormcaller

*The Lightning Blade (longsword +1, item slots 3)*

A longsword forged from blue-tinted steel that crackles with static electricity. The blade bears swirling patterns that look like storm clouds, and when drawn, the air around it hums with energy. The hilt is wrapped in leather that never gets wet, and the pommel is a polished piece of amber that glows faintly during thunderstorms.

**Magical Writing:** Elementale - burning runes carved in stone along the blade's fuller. Those with Elementale Skill can read these dragon magic inscriptions.

**Arcana:** Functions as an Arcana. Requires Elementale Skill.

**Alignment Factors:** Elementale Skill, storm-related Traits (fiery, energetic, wild), fighting in storms or during lightning, heroic deeds in battle.

### Arcana Spells

- **Lightning Strike** (Alignment 2): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the lightning arcs to a nearby ally. On Double Spicy, you are struck by your own lightning, taking d6 Hearts. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 2.

### Properties

- **Static Jolt** (Alignment 1): On a hit with Sweet, the static jolt causes the target to drop what they're holding. On Spicy, the static jolts you as well, taking 1 Heart. On Double Spicy, the blade shocks you and you drop it for 1 Round.

### Per Muster

- **Wind Shield** (Alignment 3): As an Action, gain 1 Iron Heart against ranged attacks for the remainder of combat. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the shield also blocks your own ranged attacks. On Double Spicy, the shield becomes a cage of lightning, trapping you for 1 Round. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 3.

- **Mist Form** (Alignment 4): As an Action, become partially insubstantial like mist and lightning for 1 Round. You may pass through small gaps or become harder to hit. Once per Muster. On Spicy, you become too insubstantial and cannot interact with objects. On Double Spicy, you become fully mist and cannot reform for 1 Turn. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 4.

- **Weather Command** (Alignment 5): During a thunderstorm, as an Action you may redirect lightning, calm winds, or intensify the storm. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the weather turns against you. On Double Spicy, you lose control and the storm becomes a natural disaster. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 5.

- **Storm Call** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need during a storm, as an Action you may unleash devastating lightning or create a localized storm. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the storm consumes you, taking d6 Hearts. On Double Spicy, you are struck by the full force of the tempest and reduced to 0 Hearts. Requires Elementale Skill and Alignment 6.

## The Verdant Staff

*Oakheart (staff +1, item slots 3)*

A staff carved from living wood that still bears green leaves even in winter. The wood is warm to the touch and pulses gently with life energy. Carved runes in Vitae script spiral up the length, and small flowers bloom along its surface when held by someone aligned with nature. The top is shaped like intertwined branches holding a crystal that glows with soft green light.

**Magical Writing:** Vitae - living ink that grows and changes like vines, forming the runes that spiral up the staff. Those with Vitae Skill can read this elven magic script.

**Arcana:** Functions as an Arcana. Requires Vitae Skill.

**Alignment Factors:** Vitae Skill, nature-related Traits (hallow, dreamy, enduring), connection to animals or plants, peaceful or protective actions.

### Arcana Spells

- **Animal Bond** (Alignment 3): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the animal becomes overly protective and aggressive. On Double Spicy, the bond is too strong and the animal follows you even when it should flee, putting it in danger. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 3.

- **Nature's Bounty** (Alignment 4): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the food is tainted or spoiled. On Double Spicy, the food draws life from the land, creating a dead zone around you. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 4.

- **Regeneration** (Alignment 5): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the regeneration causes rapid aging. On Double Spicy, the regeneration draws life from nearby creatures, harming them. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 5.

### Properties

- **Life Sense** (Alignment 1): You sense all living creatures within 50 feet, knowing their general location and vitality. On Spicy, you sense all life as overwhelming noise, becoming disoriented for 1 Round. On Double Spicy, you sense the approach of death and are filled with dread.

### Per Muster

- **Healing Touch** (Alignment 2): As an Action, restore 1d6 Hearts to yourself or an ally. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the healing draws life from nearby plants, killing them. On Double Spicy, the healing draws life from you instead, taking d6 Hearts. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 2.

- **Perfect Unity** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, as an Action you may restore life, grow forests, or commune with the natural world. Once per Muster. On Spicy, nature's balance demands a price—something must die for something to live. On Double Spicy, you become one with nature and cannot act for 1 Turn as you experience all life and death. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 6.

## Aegis of the Ancients

*The Unbreakable Shield (shield +2, item slots 4)*

A massive round shield of bronze and iron, its surface covered in ancient runes that shift and reform when struck. The shield bears the marks of countless battles—scratches, dents, and even a few arrow holes—but these seem to heal over time, leaving the shield pristine. The central boss is a stylized sun that glows faintly when danger approaches.

**Magical Writing:** Thaumaturgy - foundational runes that command reality, inscribed in the metal. The runes shift and reform as they respond to threats. Those with Thaumaturgy Skill can read this dwur engineering magic.

**Alignment Factors:** Shield Skill, protective Traits (steadfast, brave, honorable), defending others, standing firm against overwhelming odds.

### Arcana Spells

- **Ward** (Alignment 5): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the ward traps you inside, preventing movement. On Double Spicy, the ward becomes a prison that cannot be escaped for 1 Turn. Requires Vitae Skill and Alignment 5.

### Properties

- **Danger Sense** (Alignment 1): You sense threats before they arrive. On Spicy, you sense false threats everywhere, becoming paranoid. On Double Spicy, you are overwhelmed by all potential dangers and cannot act for 1 Round.

- **Reflect Harm** (Alignment 4): When you successfully block an attack, the shield reflects half the damage back to the attacker. On Spicy, the reflection damages the shield itself. On Double Spicy, the shield shatters from the strain and loses this power until repaired.

### Per Muster

- **Radiant Blast** (Alignment 2): As an Action, the shield emits blinding light. Enemies attacking you must roll with Disadvantage for 1 Round. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the light also blinds you. On Double Spicy, the light is so intense it damages your eyes, causing permanent vision problems.

- **Protective Aura** (Alignment 3): As an Action, allies within 10 feet gain 1 Iron Heart against attacks for the remainder of combat. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the protection draws from your own defenses, reducing your Iron Hearts. On Double Spicy, the shield protects others but not you, leaving you vulnerable.

- **Perfect Defense** (Alignment 6): In moments of dire need, as an Action you may block any attack, spell, or effect. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the perfect defense requires perfect stillness—you cannot act for 1 Round. On Double Spicy, the shield becomes too perfect, blocking everything including air, and you begin to suffocate.

## Belt of the Hero

*Girdle of Might (belt, item slots 1)*

A wide leather belt studded with iron plates and bronze rivets. The buckle is a massive piece of worked metal shaped like a roaring lion. When worn, the belt makes the wearer feel stronger and more confident. The leather is worn smooth from use, suggesting it has been passed down through many heroes.

**Magical Writing:** Thaumaturgy - foundational runes that command reality, etched into the iron plates. Those with Thaumaturgy Skill can read this dwur engineering magic.

**Alignment Factors:** Strength-related Traits (strong, powerful, tough), heroic deeds, leadership, facing great challenges.

### Properties

- **Might** (Alignment 1): You gain +1 to damage with melee attacks. On Spicy, your strength becomes too much and you break your weapon. On Double Spicy, your might is overwhelming and you cannot use delicate tools or handle fragile objects.

- **Unbreakable Will** (Alignment 3): When you would be affected by fear or mind control, you resist the effect. On Spicy, your will is so unbreakable you cannot be reasoned with or persuaded. On Double Spicy, your will becomes rigid and you cannot change your mind or adapt to new situations.

### Per Muster

- **Heroic Surge** (Alignment 2): As an Action, perform a feat of strength—break objects, lift heavy things, or perform extraordinary physical feats. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the surge exhausts you, taking 1 Heart. On Double Spicy, you overexert yourself and are unable to act for 1 Round.

- **Hero's Stand** (Alignment 4): When you or an ally would be reduced to 0 Hearts, restore 1d6 Hearts instead of falling. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the second wind comes from your own life force, taking d6 Hearts from you. On Double Spicy, the stand requires a heroic sacrifice—you must give up something important permanently.

- **Legendary Strength** (Alignment 5): As an Action, perform impossible physical acts—bending iron bars, stopping charging beasts, or leaping impossible distances. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the strength damages your body, taking d6 Hearts. On Double Spicy, you push beyond your limits and are crippled, unable to use the belt's powers for the rest of the Muster.

- **Perfect Heroism** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, as an Action you gain temporary bonuses to all actions and may perform legendary deeds. Once per Muster. On Spicy, the heroism demands a great sacrifice. On Double Spicy, you become the perfect hero but lose your humanity, becoming unable to relate to normal people.

## Ring of Shadows

*The Whisper Band (ring, item slots 1)*

A simple silver ring that seems to absorb light, making it appear darker than it should. When worn, shadows seem to cling to the wearer, and their movements become more fluid and silent. The ring bears no visible markings, but those with Umbrakala Skill can sense the dark magic woven into its metal.

**Magical Writing:** Umbrakala - written in darkness and void, the script is invisible to normal sight but appears as shadowy glyphs to those with Umbrakala Skill. This is the secret magic of the Alu.

**Arcana:** Functions as an Arcana. Requires Umbrakala Skill.

**Alignment Factors:** Umbrakala Skill, stealth-related Traits (silent, cunning, elusive), working in darkness, thievery or deception.

### Arcana Spells

- **Shadow Meld** (Alignment 2): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, you meld too well and cannot un-meld for 1 Round. On Double Spicy, you become trapped in shadow and must be pulled free by an ally. Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 2.

- **Shadow Step** (Alignment 3): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, you step into the wrong shadow and emerge in an unexpected location. On Double Spicy, you become lost in shadow and cannot return for 1 Turn. Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 3.

- **Deep Shadow** (Alignment 4): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the darkness is too deep and you cannot see even with darkvision. On Double Spicy, the darkness becomes a void that threatens to consume you, taking d6 Hearts. Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 4.

- **Shadow Clone** (Alignment 5): Cast using normal spell rules. On Spicy, the clone has a mind of its own and acts against you. On Double Spicy, the clone becomes real and you must fight it. Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 5.

### Properties

- **Shadow Cloak** (Alignment 1): You are harder to notice in dim light or darkness. On Spicy, shadows cling too tightly and you become visible as a dark silhouette. On Double Spicy, you merge too deeply with shadow and cannot interact with the physical world for 1 Round.

### Per Muster

- **Perfect Shadow** (Alignment 6): In moments of great need, as an Action you may pass through walls, become completely undetectable, or travel vast distances through darkness. Once per Muster. On Spicy, you become shadow and cannot return to physical form for 1 Turn. On Double Spicy, you are consumed by shadow and must make an Invocation to return. Requires Umbrakala Skill and Alignment 6.
